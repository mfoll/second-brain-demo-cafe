# Coffee Roaster Change

Moving the house espresso from a large wholesale roaster to Ombrel Roasters in the next town. Done on 2025-11-28 after blind tastings and a day of barista training.

## Outcome

House espresso switched to Ombrel Roasters after blind tastings, with every barista trained on the new recipe.

## Current State

- Completed 2025-11-28. Ombrel's Estuary blend on both grinders; decaf from Ombrel since January 2026.
- Price 24.50 EUR/kg against 19.80 before. At about 90 kg a month that is roughly 420 EUR a month more, covered by the 0.20 EUR rise on milk drinks in the [[price-review-2026]].
- Recipe: 18 g in, 38 g out, 28 to 30 seconds ([[espresso-dial-in-notes]], [[espresso-brew-ratio]]).
- [[dev-sandhu]] retrains the team every six months; next visit booked for late November 2026.

## Scope and Boundaries

Espresso blend and decaf. Filter coffee was tried for a month and dropped (too slow at the counter). Milk stays with Glenvarrow; oat milk is a separate thread ([[should-we-keep-the-oat-milk-surcharge]]).

## Working Context

Why: the old roaster changed the blend twice in 2025 without telling us and Amira could not keep the shots consistent. Two regulars said the coffee had gone bitter. The old supplier's free grinder service was the main thing we gave up.

How we chose: Amira shortlisted three roasters. On 2025-10-15 eight of us tasted three espressos and three flat whites blind ([[coffee-blind-tasting-2025-10-15]], [[coffee-blind-tasting-scores-2025]]). Ombrel's Estuary won six of eight on espresso and all eight in milk. Dev offered grinder calibration and a training day as part of the deal ([[ombrel-roasters-wholesale-offer-2025]]).

Rejected: the cheapest of the three at 21.00 EUR/kg (fine in milk, flat as espresso), and roasting a single origin as house (too variable through the year for a café our size).
