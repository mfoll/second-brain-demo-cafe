# Quarrymoor Farm Egg Supply Terms

The one-page terms Ada Quarrie sends each January, with the price per egg, delivery days and standing order.

## Summary

Free-range eggs, mixed sizes graded medium and large, delivered Tuesday and Saturday before 07:00 in trays of 30. Standing order 1,080 eggs a week (36 trays). Price 0.29 EUR per egg from 2026-01-06, up from 0.24 in 2025. Changes to the standing order by phone by Friday.

## Why It Matters

Eggs are in almost everything: egg wash, brioche, cakes, custard. The 21% rise was the second-largest after butter in the January 2026 costing run and is part of why the [[price-review-2026]] could not be just viennoiserie. It also set out the late-winter shortfall that comes up every February.

## Key Details

- Weekly spend about 313 EUR at the new price, against 259 EUR in 2025.
- Late-winter laying drop: Ada warns two weeks ahead and can cut us to 800 a week; we use liquid egg for egg wash in those weeks.
- No contract term; either side can change with a month's notice.

## Caveats

Ada's terms do not cover Christmas volumes; those are agreed in November.
