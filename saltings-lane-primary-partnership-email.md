# Saltings Lane Primary Partnership Email

Email from [[samir-belkacem]] to the bakehouse address on 10 September 2025, and my reply the next day. Summarised.

## Summary

- Samir's Year 4 class had an autumn topic on "food from field to table" and he was looking for a local food business to show children how bread is made.
- He asked for one visit or one session in school, at no cost to the school, with a teacher present.
- I replied offering three after-school sessions in the school food room, with us bringing dough and ingredients, and suggested starting in October.

## Why It Matters

This is how the [[school-bake-club]] started. The pilot grew from three sessions to eight after the first one ([[school-bake-club-first-session-2025-10-08]]). It also set two lasting terms: no cost to the school, and the school handles everything about the children.
