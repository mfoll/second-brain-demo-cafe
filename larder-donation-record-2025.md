# Larder Donation Record 2025

Weekly kilos of bread handed to the Brackenmouth Community Larder from 15 April to 31 December 2025, from the Larder sheet signed by the volunteers at each collection. Checked monthly against [[bernard-osei]]'s totals.

## Key Details

| Period | Kilos | Per trading day |
|---|---|---|
| April (from 15th) | 168 | 12.9 |
| May | 290 | 11.2 |
| June | 255 | 9.8 |
| July | 250 | 9.3 |
| August | 205 | 7.9 |
| September | 200 | 7.7 |
| October | 215 | 8.0 |
| November | 230 | 8.8 |
| December | 340 | 14.2 |
| Total | about 2,150 | about 9 |

## Why It Matters

It shows the Larder agreement working and gives the figure we quote publicly (about 2 tonnes in 2025). December is high because we baked extra bread for the Christmas weeks and closed early on the 24th. Used in the [[food-waste-review-2026-01-13]] and in [[courier-article-larder-donations-2025-11]].

## Caveats

Weighed in sacks on the shop scale; frozen Sunday bread is counted when it leaves, not when baked.
