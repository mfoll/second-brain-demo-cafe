# Prime Cost

Ingredients plus labour, as a share of sales. The two costs a food business controls week to week.

## Why It Matters Here

[[gross-profit-margin]] leaves labour out, and labour is our biggest cost: about 39% of turnover in 2025 ([[marchbank-ochoa-year-end-accounts-2025]]). Prime cost puts them together. Hollowgate's 2025 prime cost was about 69% (30.6% ingredients, 38.8% labour).

Lunch is the example. At 51% gross margin it looked weak; with 30 cook hours a week added, its prime cost was over 85%, which is what made the cut at [[team-meeting-2026-02-05]] look obvious. The [[savoury-lunch-trim]] later showed that the cut also took bakery and coffee sales with it, which prime cost by section cannot see.

## How I Track It

Monthly, from the [[management-accounts-monthly-pack]] (ingredients) and Deputy hours costed at the average rate. Target under 70% for the whole shop.
