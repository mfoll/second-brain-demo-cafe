# Wholesale Pause Decision 2025-10-06

On Monday 6 October 2025 I decided to pause wholesale from 20 October, after a morning with [[kofi-asare]] and [[lena-marsh]] in the basement looking at September's rota and margins.

## What we looked at

- Deputy hours for September: Kofi 48 hours a week on average, Joel 44; about 15 a week across them went on wholesale.
- [[wholesale-margin-workbook-2025]]: 33% gross margin June to September.
- The hired oven: wholesale had to go in first at 04:30, and the shop's croissants were late on 9 of 22 trading days in September.
- The new oven: due mid-November, but commissioning dates had already slipped once ([[deck-oven-replacement]]).

## Options

1. Raise wholesale prices by 12% and keep going. Rejected: it would not fix the hours.
2. Drop one customer. Rejected: it would save about 6 hours a week and make the other customer's order uneconomic to bake alone.
3. Pause both with two weeks' notice and review after the new oven. Chosen.

## Actions

- Letters to Nadia and Ewan the same afternoon ([[wholesale-pause-letter-2025-10-06]]), followed by phone calls.
- Last collection Saturday 18 October.
- Kofi to put the freed hours into a proper early-shift break and an earlier finish, not into new products.

Afterwards I missed the finance course that evening, the only session I missed.
