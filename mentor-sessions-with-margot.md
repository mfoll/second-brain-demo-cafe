# Mentor Sessions With Margot

Running notes from monthly sessions with [[margot-elling]] through the traders' mentoring scheme, since 11 March 2025. An hour, usually the second Monday, at her kitchen table. Only what I want to keep.

## Themes so far

- **March to June 2025: cash.** She told me to stop treating the January overdraft as bad luck. Led to the forecast and, later, the [[food-business-finance-course]].
- **September 2025: saying no.** On wholesale: "Growth that eats your best people's sleep is not growth." Three weeks later I paused it ([[wholesale-pause-decision-2025-10-06]]).
- **February 2026: telling the team first.** After the lunch cut, she asked whether the team heard before the regulars. Some did not. Since then, decisions that change anyone's hours go to a team meeting before anything public.
- **May 2026: delegating to Lena.** I still sign off every order over 500 EUR. Margot thinks the limit should be 2,000 EUR and Lena should own the ordering calendar. Agreed to try 1,000 EUR from July. It has worked; I have not overruled anything.
- **September 2026: the second site.** Her question was who runs Hollowgate on the days I am at the hall. I do not have an answer yet ([[should-we-open-a-second-site]]).

## Her habits worth copying

- Writes down three things after every hard conversation: what was decided, who does what, and what she would say differently.
- Never makes a decision about people on a Friday.
