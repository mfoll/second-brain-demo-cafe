# All-Butter Croissants Are Not Where We Save

## Stance

The croissant stays all butter and stays the size it is. When butter goes up we raise the price, fix the butter price or save elsewhere; we do not use a blend, a margarine sheet or a smaller piece.

## Scope

Laminated viennoiserie: croissants, pains au chocolat, almond croissants, Danish. Cakes and buns can use what works best for them.

## Why I Think This

- It is the product we are known for and the top seller by units. People come in for it and buy a coffee.
- Anselm Ferreira's rule from the hotel: you can tell a kitchen by its croissant. Old-fashioned, still true.
- The numbers allow it. Even at 8.90 EUR/kg butter, the croissant's food cost is 29% of the shelf price ([[croissant-cost-breakdown]]), close to target.

## What Would Change My Mind

Butter above about 11 EUR/kg with no fixed price available, at which point the croissant would need to pass 3.40 EUR. Even then I would cut the range before the butter.

## Implications

Butter is a quality decision first and a price decision second. A cheaper dairy must laminate as well ([[priyanka-email-butter-sheet-trial-2026-10]]).
