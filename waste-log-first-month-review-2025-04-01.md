# Waste Log First Month Review 2025-04-01

After closing on Tuesday 1 April 2025, Kofi, Lena and I looked at the first four weeks of the [[end-of-day-waste-log]]. Short meeting at the big table, 16:20 to 17:10.

## What the log showed

- March waste: 9.0% of bakery production by value, about 610 EUR a week at shelf price. Nobody had guessed higher than 4%.
- Bread was about 60% of it. The 13:00 baguette bake on weekdays was the single worst item: on average 14 of 30 left at closing.
- Viennoiserie about 30%, almost all on Tuesdays and Saturdays. Cakes 10%.
- Sunday was close to zero.

## Decisions

- Kofi to drop the weekday 13:00 baguette bake for a month and size the 11:30 bake from Square sales by hour. This became [[late-bake-sizing]].
- I would approach the Community Larder about bread donations. Bernard Osei had asked in 2024 and I had not followed up. Agreement signed two weeks later ([[community-larder-donation-agreement]]).
- Lena to try a day-old pastry offer in the morning. After a bad Saturday trial with an afternoon discount, this became [[yesterdays-bags]] from 6 May.
- Keep logging every day, including Sundays, and review monthly.

## Notes

Kofi was defensive at first about the bread numbers, fairly: the late bake had been my rule from 2022, to never have empty baskets at lunch. We agreed that empty baskets after 14:00 are acceptable.
