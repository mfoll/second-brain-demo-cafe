# Christmas Collection Days 2025-12-23

Collection of the 2025 Christmas preorders on Tuesday 23 and Wednesday 24 December 2025. Kept under the 2026 project because it is the baseline the 2026 plan is built on.

## Numbers

- 214 orders: 101 collected on the 23rd, 108 on the 24th, 5 not collected.
- Collection slots by half hour from 08:00. Longest queue on the 24th was about 12 minutes at 10:30.
- Balances taken at collection through Square; deposits had been about 4,900 EUR in total.

## How it went

- Bûche moulds were the limit: [[priyanka-desai]] finished the last 30 bûches at 19:00 on the 22nd.
- Stollen were made from 15 December and wrapped by the 19th; no problem.
- Walk-in trade on the 23rd was thin on bread because so much oven time went to orders. Several regulars without orders were disappointed.
- Uncollected orders went on sale at 14:00 on the 24th and sold out; deposits kept as per the terms ([[should-christmas-deposits-be-refundable]]).
