# Vegan Croissant Trial Recipes

Priyanka's recipe sheets for the four vegan croissant rounds, with food cost per piece. Last updated after round 4 on 18 May 2026.

## Key Details

| Version | Fat | Dough changes | Food cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Soft plant spread | Oat drink instead of milk | 0.62 |
| 2 | Block plant fat for pastry | Colder dough, longer rests | 0.74 |
| 3 | Same block fat | Glucose syrup, lamination at 12°C | 0.78 |
| 4 | Block fat and coconut spread | As 3 | 0.81 |

- All versions: 3 single folds, 24 pieces a batch, 80 g dough each.
- Butter croissant food cost for comparison: about 0.91 EUR at 8.90 EUR/kg butter ([[butter-price-tracker]]).

## Why It Matters

Cost is not the obstacle; quality and bench time are ([[should-we-offer-a-vegan-croissant]]). Version 3 is the base for the November 2026 round.

## Caveats

Costs exclude labour; lamination takes about 40 extra minutes a batch on a separate bench slot.
