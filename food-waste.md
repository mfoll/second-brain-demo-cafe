# Food Waste

Retrieval surface for everything about what we bake and do not sell: how we measure it, how we cut it, and where the leftovers go. The short story: end-of-day waste was 9% of bakery production by value in March 2025 and 3.5% by December 2025.

> [!info]
> What brought waste down was measuring it every day ([[end-of-day-waste-log]]), sizing the late bakes from hourly sales in [[square-pos-sales-data]], selling pastries next morning in yesterday's bags at half price, and giving unsold bread to the Community Larder under the [[community-larder-donation-agreement]].
