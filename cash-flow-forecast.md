# Cash Flow Forecast

Build a rolling 13-week cash flow forecast for the bakehouse after the January 2025 overdraft squeeze, and get into the habit of updating it every Monday.

## Outcome

A rolling 13-week cash flow forecast in use, updated every Monday, after the January 2025 overdraft squeeze. Reached on 2025-03-28.

## Current State

- Completed. The forecast is the [[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]]; the Monday routine is [[update-the-cash-flow-forecast]].
- It replaced the [[monthly-budget-2024]], which showed profit by month and hid the timing of VAT and rent.
- It has run every Monday since 2025-03-31 except two Mondays in August 2025 when the oven failed and I forgot.
- It now also carries the second-site scenarios for [[second-site-financing]].

## Scope and Boundaries

Cash only: money in and out of the Pellow Mutual current account and the VAT pot, by week. Profit, margins and pricing stay in [[pricing-and-margins]]. Personal money stays out.

## Working Context

What went wrong in January 2025 ([[overdraft-squeeze-january-2025]]): the Q4 2024 VAT payment of 11,800 EUR was due on 2025-01-31, the old deck oven needed a 3,400 EUR repair from [[colm-ashworth]] in the same fortnight, and January takings were 31% below December. The 6,000 EUR overdraft was used in full for 19 days. Nothing was wrong with the business on paper; the year was profitable. The money simply left in the wrong weeks. The full reasoning is in [[why-did-we-run-out-of-cash-in-january-2025]].

How the forecast was built, February to March 2025:

1. Pulled 2024 weekly takings from Square and bank statements to get a seasonal pattern.
2. Listed every fixed outgoing with its real date: rent quarters to [[cyril-ashdown]], VAT dates ([[vat-return-calendar]]), insurance, the card terminal fees, payroll fortnights.
3. Added variable costs as a percentage of takings (food and packaging 29%).
4. Set a floor: end-of-week balance never below 15,000 EUR without a plan ([[cash-buffer]]).
5. Reviewed it twice with [[ruth-marchbank]], who suggested the separate VAT pot ([[should-we-set-aside-vat-weekly]]).

Rejected options: an app that predicts cash from bank feeds (too clever for seasonal takings; I want to see each line), and a monthly forecast (January 2025 happened inside one month).
