# Coffee cupping

## Summary

Article on coffee cupping, the structured tasting of brewed coffee. Tasters smell the coffee, then slurp it from a spoon so it spreads across the tongue, and assess body, sweetness, acidity, flavour and aftertaste. Professionals are certified as Q Graders, but anyone can cup informally. Experienced cuppers can sometimes tell a coffee's origin from the cup.

## Why It Matters

Amira proposed a proper cupping for the roaster shortlist. We decided against the formal method for the [[coffee-blind-tasting-2025-10-15]]: our customers drink espresso and flat whites, not cupping bowls, so we tasted the coffees as we would serve them. We did borrow the vocabulary. The five attributes became the columns on the score sheet's comment section ([[coffee-blind-tasting-scores-2025]]).

## Key Details

- Attributes assessed: body, sweetness, acidity, flavour, aftertaste.
- Slurping aerates the coffee; spoons are rinsed between samples.

## Caveats

Cupping judges the beans, not how they behave on our machine and in our milk.
