# Running A Bake Club Session

How to run one session of the [[school-bake-club]] at Saltings Lane Primary. First Wednesday of the month, 15:30 to 16:45, in the school food room.

## A week before

1. Check the email from Samir Belkacem with the allergen exclusions for the group. Do not ask for names.
2. Check the recipe in [[bake-club-session-plans]] against the exclusions. If an allergen is present, change the recipe or the product.
3. Book the second person (Priyanka or Grace) on Deputy for 13:30 to 17:00.

## The day before

1. Order ingredients with the normal shop order.
2. Print one recipe card per child plus two spare.

## On the day

1. 10:00: make the dough in the basement, in lidded tubs, labelled with product, time and allergens. Keep chilled until 14:45.
2. 14:45: pack the crate: tubs, scrapers, one tray and one apron per child, oven gloves, probe thermometer, first-aid kit, allergen sheet. Nothing else.
3. 15:15: arrive, check the food room oven with the probe: set 20°C lower than the recipe says.
4. 15:30: hands washed, aprons on. Ten-minute talk on the product and where the flour comes from.
5. Children shape and fill; adults handle the oven and all hot trays.
6. Bake, cool on the racks for 10 minutes, wrap in paper bags with a label. Children take their bake home.
7. 16:30: tidy up with the children. Wipe surfaces, wash equipment, leave the room as found.
8. 16:45: hand over to the teacher. Do not hand children over to parents yourself.

## Rules

- A teacher is in the room the whole time. If the teacher leaves, stop.
- No photographs of children.
- No knives in children's hands except table knives.

Risks and controls: [[school-bake-club-risk-assessment]].
