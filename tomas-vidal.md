# Tomas Vidal

Chef-owner of the tapas bar across the street at 19 Hollowgate. Opens at 17:00 when we close, so we share the street rather than compete for it.

## How we work together

- He buys bread from us at retail most days, 6 to 10 baguettes collected at 15:30, paid weekly. Not wholesale; it is a standing counter order at shop prices less 10%.
- We lend each other things in emergencies: he lent us a blast chiller shelf during the oven failure in August 2025; we gave him flour when his delivery missed a Saturday.
- Both of us have pavement licences. We coordinate so that our tables and his evening tables do not overlap on the same stretch; he supported our 2027 renewal with 6 extra covers ([[pavement-seating-licence]]).

## Current threads

- He has asked whether we would bake a pan con tomate loaf for him if wholesale ever restarts. Noted in [[when-could-wholesale-resume]].
