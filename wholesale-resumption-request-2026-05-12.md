# Wholesale Resumption Request 2026-05-12

[[nadia-volkova]] came in on Tuesday 12 May 2026 and asked whether we could restart supplying Pier Street Coffee. Twenty minutes at the window table.

## What she asked

- 30 loaves a day, Tuesday to Saturday, white sourdough and rye only.
- She would pay more than in 2025; she suggested 10% more. Her current bake-off supplier is cheaper but her customers have noticed.

## What I said

- Not before the [[second-site-decision]] in December 2026. If we bake for a second site, any wholesale would come from the same kitchen and the same plan.
- If we restart, prices will start from the cost floor, not from 2025 plus 10% ([[wholesale-pricing-floor]]). For white sourdough that is about 3.35 EUR, nearly 16% more than in 2025.
- She is the first call when we know.

## After

Told Kofi the same evening. His answer: not with the current early shift, and not before Hana is hired and trained. Recorded in [[when-could-wholesale-resume]].
