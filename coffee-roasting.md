# Coffee roasting

## Summary

How heating green coffee turns it into something worth drinking. Green beans already hold the acids, sugars, protein and caffeine; roasting creates the flavour through Maillard and caramelisation reactions. The beans lose around 15 to 18% of their mass and roughly double in volume. Roasters track two audible "cracks", the first around 196 °C and the second around 224 °C, and name roast levels from light to dark by colour, temperature, oil and sound. Light roasts keep more of the origin's character; dark roasts taste mostly of the roast.

## Why It Matters

Read before the [[coffee-blind-tasting-2025-10-15]], because two of the three shortlisted roasters described their blends in roast-level terms and I wanted to understand what they meant. It explained what Kofi called "tea" in roaster X: a light roast for espresso, origin-forward and sharp in milk. Ombrel's Estuary is a medium roast, which suits a café selling mostly flat whites.

## Key Details

- Weight loss on roasting is one reason roasted coffee costs much more per kg than green coffee, which helped me read Dev's pricing ([[ombrel-roasters-wholesale-offer-2025]]).
