# Break-Even Takings

The net monthly sales (excluding VAT) at which the bakehouse covers all its costs and makes no profit. My working figure, from the [[break-even-model-workbook]].

## How I calculate it

break-even takings = monthly fixed costs / average contribution margin ratio

- Fixed costs: rent, all wages and employer costs (the rota does not shrink when it rains), equipment leases, insurance, utilities, software subscriptions, the accountant, repairs reserve. About 31,800 EUR a month in 2025; about 32,400 EUR since the new oven lease.
- Contribution margin ratio: net sales minus ingredients, packaging and card fees, as a share of net sales. About 66% across the shop.

2025: 31,800 / 0.66 = about 48,200 EUR a month. Actual average 50,800 EUR. Margin of safety about 5%.

## How I use it

- Daily: about 2,200 EUR of net takings on a weekday is roughly break-even; Lena knows the number.
- For decisions: any change that adds fixed cost (a hire, a lease) raises the bar by that cost divided by 0.66. The assistant baker added about 2,700 EUR a month to the break-even.
- For the second site: each site needs its own figure; Hollowgate's 5% margin is not enough to carry a loss-making second shop for long ([[should-we-open-a-second-site]]).

## Limits

The 66% average hides a wide spread (bakery about 71%, lunch about 51% before the trim). Seasonal months are far apart: January is below break-even every year.
