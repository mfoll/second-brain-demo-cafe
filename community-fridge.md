# Community Fridge

English Wikipedia article on community fridges.

## Summary

A community fridge is a refrigerator in a public space where people can leave and take food freely, a form of mutual aid. Some also hold non-perishable food. The article contrasts them with traditional food pantries: anyone can put food in or take it out without limit, which reduces stigma. They are usually kept going by volunteers, residents and local businesses, with donations from individuals and food rescue organisations.

## Why It Matters

Our Sunday leftovers go to the Larder's community fridge, since there is no Sunday collection ([[end-of-day-waste-and-donation-routine]]). I read this in June 2026 when [[bernard-osei]] asked about a Monday pickup of frozen bread. The "no limit, no questions" point is why bread there must be labelled clearly with allergens: nobody is there to answer a question.
