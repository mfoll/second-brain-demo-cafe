# Rolling Forecast

A forecast that always looks the same distance ahead: each week the oldest week drops off, the actuals replace the forecast for the week just gone, and a new week is added at the end.

## Why Rolling And Why Thirteen Weeks

- A fixed annual budget gets less useful every month and hides timing ([[monthly-budget-2024]]).
- Thirteen weeks always contains at least one VAT payment and one rent quarter, the two lumps that matter.
- It is short enough that the numbers are mostly known: contracts, rota, the last few weeks' takings.

## Rules We Keep

- Update weekly, on the same day ([[update-the-cash-flow-forecast]]).
- Replace forecast with actuals; never adjust last week's forecast to match.
- Keep four weeks of history visible, to see how wrong we were ([[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]] tracks this).
- Longer questions (a second site over 36 months) go in a separate model, not the rolling forecast ([[saltings-hall-three-scenario-model]]).
