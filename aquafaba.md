# Aquafaba

English Wikipedia article on aquafaba.

## Summary

Aquafaba is the thick cooking water of legumes such as chickpeas. The article credits its culinary discovery to a French musician and notes it has been used for years as an ingredient in vegan cooking.

## Why It Matters

Read the week before the January 2026 trial round, when [[priyanka-desai]] made aquafaba meringue kisses alongside the second vegan croissant ([[vegan-bake-trials-log]]). They held their shape and are good enough to sell. Chickpeas are not one of the 14 allergens we track, but a legume-based product needs its ingredients clear on the label for customers avoiding pulses ([[allergen-management]]).

## Caveats

The article is about the ingredient, not recipes or baking ratios.
