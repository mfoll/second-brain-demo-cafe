# Gross margin

## Summary

Explains gross margin as the share of revenue left after the cost of goods sold, expressed as a percentage of the selling price, and distinguishes it from gross profit, which is an amount rather than a ratio.

## Why It Matters

Checked when I wrote [[gross-profit-margin]] for the menu work in January 2026, to make sure the margins I quote to the team mean the same thing as the textbook. They do, with two house choices: we exclude VAT from the price and we leave labour out.

## Caveats

Says nothing about which costs a café should count in cost of goods; packaging and staff meals are our own choices. Margin also hides labour, see [[prime-cost]].
