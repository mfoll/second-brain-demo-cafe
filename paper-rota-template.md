# Paper Rota Template

The A3 weekly rota sheet used from 2019 to 2025-05-30, now retired. Kept as a record of how shifts were laid out before Deputy.

## Summary

A grid of staff names against days, with shift times written by hand, a column for swaps, and a box for holiday requests. Lena filled it every Thursday and pinned it on the basement door; a photo went to the staff chat. Hours were added up by hand in a notebook for payroll.

## Why It Matters

Superseded by Deputy in the [[rota-system-switch]]. It is worth keeping because the shift blocks we built in Deputy copied this layout, and because it explains why overtime was invisible until 2025: the sheet showed planned shifts, never the hours actually worked.

## Source Location

Last filled sheet (week of 2025-05-26) in the office drawer; blank template in `Operations / Rota / old`.
