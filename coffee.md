# Coffee

Coffee is about a quarter of takings and most of the reason people sit down. This Topic collects the roaster, the recipe, the training and the reading.

## Where things stand

House espresso is Ombrel Roasters' Estuary blend since 2025-11-28, dialled in at 18 g in, 38 g out in 28 to 30 seconds. Amira owns quality; [[dev-sandhu]] at Ombrel comes in twice a year to retrain the team. Beans cost 24.50 EUR/kg against 19.80 from the previous roaster; we use about 90 kg a month.

## How we got here

From opening in 2019 to November 2025 we used a large wholesale roaster's house blend at 19.80 EUR/kg, with free grinder servicing. It was fine and nobody thought about it. In 2025 the blend changed twice without notice, shots ran sour one week and bitter the next, and two regulars told Amira the coffee had gone off. That started the [[coffee-roaster-change]]: a shortlist of three roasters, a blind tasting with eight of us, and a decision driven mostly by how each coffee tasted in milk, because three quarters of what we sell is milk drinks ([[coffee-sales-by-drink-2025]]).

## Numbers worth remembering

- About 61,000 coffee drinks in 2025, roughly 200 a trading day; flat white the top seller at 29%.
- Coffee is about 24% of gross sales ([[square-pos-sales-data]]).
- Coffee in a drink costs about 0.45 EUR at 18 g a shot; a flat white's full food cost is 0.66 EUR against a 3.50 EUR price ([[recipe-costing-sheet]]).
- Ombrel's price is fixed to 2026-11-28; expect a rise for 2027.
