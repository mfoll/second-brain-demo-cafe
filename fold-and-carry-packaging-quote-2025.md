# Fold And Carry Packaging Quote 2025

Yusuf Karimi's quote of September 2025 for moving our bags and boxes to compostable stock and for the label roll used in the allergen labelling work.

## Summary

Compostable window bags (small and large), single cake boxes, six-pastry boxes and 50 mm thermal labels. Bags about 8% dearer than the old paper bags; boxes the same; labels 21.00 EUR per roll of 1,000.

## Why It Matters

Packaging is in every costing as a fixed line, and the label stock was the practical bottleneck of the [[allergen-labelling-overhaul]]. The bag change added roughly 0.01 EUR per bag, which we absorbed.

## Key Details

- Small window bag 0.064 EUR, large 0.088, six-pastry box 0.42, single cake box 0.31.
- Minimum order 2,000 bags per size.
- Lead time five working days; Christmas orders must be in by mid-November.

## Caveats

Prices held to 2026-12-31; cup prices rise 6% from January 2027 ([[supplier-price-rises-2026]]).
