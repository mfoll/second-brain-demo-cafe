# Pellow Mutual Loan Offer 2026-09-08

[[devon-pryce]] sent Pellow Mutual's formal offer for the second-site loan, four weeks after I sent the business plan.

## The Offer

85,000 EUR over five years at 7.9% fixed, about 1,720 EUR a month, debenture, no personal guarantee, valid until 2026-12-08 ([[pellow-mutual-second-site-loan-offer]]).

## Call With Devon

Same afternoon, 20 minutes:

- The credit team liked the trading record and the forecast. They want to see the low case before drawdown.
- Own contribution at least 25% (confirmed in writing on 2026-09-15: [[pellow-mutual-loan-conditions-email]]).
- I asked about holding the offer past December. He said possibly, not promised.

## My Reaction

Relief that the bank says yes, followed by the uncomfortable thought that a bank saying yes is not the same as the business being ready. Sent it to [[ruth-marchbank]] the same evening, which led to the buffer calculation before the [[second-site-review-2026-09-30]].
