# When Could Wholesale Resume?

## Why It Matters

Two former customers would come back ([[nadia-volkova]] asked in May 2026, [[tomas-vidal]] would add a standing order), and wholesale is the cheapest growth we have. But the reasons we paused ([[why-did-we-pause-wholesale-to-cafes]]) have not all gone away.

## Current Answer

Deferred until the [[second-site-decision]] on 15 December 2026. Three conditions, all needed:

1. Baker hours: either the [[central-production-kitchen]] goes ahead with a shift pattern [[kofi-asare]] accepts, or the early shift has spare hours after Hana's induction. Kofi's objection to 03:00 starts at the [[second-site-review-2026-09-30]] makes the first route uncertain.
2. Margin: prices that clear 35% gross margin at 2026 costs. At current flour and butter prices the 2025 wholesale prices would give about 29%; white sourdough would need to go from 2.90 to about 3.35 EUR ([[wholesale-pricing-floor]]).
3. No pastries, collection only, as before.

## What Would Settle It

A yes to central production with an agreed shift pattern, then a recosting with [[recipe-costing-sheet]]. If the second site is a no, I would test a small restart (one customer, 25 loaves) on the early shift alone from spring 2027.
