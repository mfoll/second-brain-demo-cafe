# Bernard Osei

Coordinator of the Brackenmouth Community Larder, the town's food bank and community fridge, run from the church hall on Ferry Lane by volunteers.

## Role

- Signed the [[community-larder-donation-agreement]] with me on 14 April 2025 and organises the volunteers who collect our unsold bread at closing.
- Sets the Larder's rules on what it can accept: no cream or custard fillings, no cooked meat or fish, everything labelled with the product, date and allergens.

## How we work together

- Collections Tuesday to Saturday at 16:15 from the shop door; Sunday leftovers are dropped at the community fridge by whoever closes ([[end-of-day-waste-and-donation-routine]]).
- He sends a monthly total of kilos received, which we check against our own [[larder-donation-record-2025]].
- When a volunteer cannot come he texts Lena by 15:00 and we freeze the bread for the next day.

## Current threads

- Renewal of the agreement in April 2027. He would like to add a Monday pickup of frozen bread; we are open to it if the freezer space allows.
- He asked whether the bake club could make bread rolls for the Larder's Christmas lunch. Probably yes, at the December session.

He was interviewed with me for [[courier-article-larder-donations-2025-11]].
