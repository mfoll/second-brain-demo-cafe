# Stellan Brook

Owner of Brook & Daughters, the fruit and vegetable wholesaler that supplies the bakehouse three times a week.

## Role

Fruit for tarts and specials, vegetables for soup, lemons and herbs. Since May 2026 he sends a seasonal availability sheet four to six weeks ahead, which is what the [[seasonal-bake-calendar]] is planned against ([[brook-and-daughters-seasonal-availability-sheet]]).

## How we work together

- Orders by text the evening before, Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday delivery.
- Good at warning early when a crop is short or dear. He told us in May that the June strawberries would be expensive, and he was right.
- Will take back fruit that arrives bruised without argument.

## Current threads

- Squash and pears for the autumn and November slots ([[autumn-menu-2026]]).
- Rhubarb prices for spring 2027.
- Soup vegetables for [[mateus-ferraz]]'s rota, roughly 18 kg a week.
