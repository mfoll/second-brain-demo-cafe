# Fixed And Variable Costs

Fixed costs stay the same whatever we sell in a week: rent, the oven lease, insurance, software, salaried hours. Variable costs move with sales: ingredients, packaging, card fees.

## At Hollowgate

| Fixed (monthly, 2026) | EUR | Variable | Share of takings |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rent | 3,250 | Ingredients | about 28% |
| Oven lease | 812 | Packaging | about 2% |
| Utilities (mostly fixed) | 1,450 | Card fees | about 1.4% |
| Insurance, software, fees | 1,100 | | |

Payroll is the awkward one: the rota is fixed in the short run (contracts, opening hours) and variable over months.

## Why It Matters

- The second site adds fixed costs (rent, service charge, loan) before it adds any sales; that is why the buffer comes first ([[cash-buffer]]).
- Each extra sale above fixed costs contributes its [[contribution-margin]]; that is how the [[break-even-month]] is reached.
- Cutting lunch removed a variable cost but not the fixed cost of the space and the kitchen, so takings lost hurt more than expected.
