# Ewan Macrae

Owner of Lantern Books, a second-hand bookshop on Quayside with a small café at the back. Wholesale customer from 3 June to 18 October 2025, about 25 loaves a day, mostly seeded rye and baguettes for sandwiches.

## How we work together

- Terms in [[wholesale-supply-agreement-lantern-books]], the same as Pier Street apart from a smaller minimum order (15 loaves) and no Saturday rye.
- He collected himself on a bicycle trailer, usually at 07:10. Twice in August he asked for a later collection, which we could not do with the hired oven.
- He took the pause notice ([[wholesale-pause-letter-2025-10-06]]) calmly and said he would rather wait for us than switch for good. In practice he buys from another bakery now.

## Current threads

- Nothing active. He sells our Christmas stollen at his till on commission (10%) in December, which we agreed in 2025 and will repeat in 2026 for up to 20 stollen, counted inside the [[christmas-preorders-2026]] capacity.
- He hosts the traders' book swap with [[harriet-lowe]]'s association each spring.
