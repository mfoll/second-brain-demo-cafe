# Should We Take The Pellow Mutual Loan?

If we go ahead with unit 7, is the Pellow Mutual offer (85,000 EUR, five years, 7.9%) the right way to pay for it?

## Why It Matters

It is the largest borrowing the company would have made, and it expires on 2026-12-08, a week before the decision date.

## Current Answer

Probably yes if the second site goes ahead, but possibly smaller. The rate is fair, there is no personal guarantee, and repayments of about 1,720 EUR a month are already in the [[saltings-hall-three-scenario-model]]. The alternative is leasing about 32,000 EUR of equipment and borrowing about 55,000 EUR, which cuts the loan repayment and keeps own cash, at a slightly higher total cost ([[loan-repayment-back-of-envelope]]).

The loan is not the reason to open or not; [[ruth-marchbank]] said so plainly at the [[second-site-review-2026-09-30]].

- Whether [[devon-pryce]] will hold the offer to 2027-01-31 ([[pellow-mutual-loan-conditions-email]]).
