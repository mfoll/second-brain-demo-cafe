# Stollen

English Wikipedia article on stollen.

## Summary

Stollen is a fruit bread with nuts, spices and dried or candied fruit, usually dusted with icing sugar and often filled with marzipan or almond paste. It is a traditional holiday bread in north-western Europe.

## Why It Matters

Stollen is our biggest Christmas preorder line (142 in 2025) and the bottleneck for 2026 ([[how-many-christmas-preorders-can-the-basement-bake]]). I reread the article before writing the 2026 form to check the description on the counter card. The marzipan is why our stollen carries a nut allergen (almond) on the [[christmas-preorder-form-2026]]; the article's "often" is a reminder that not every stollen has it, so customers should not assume either way.

## Caveats

Our recipe is Anselm Ferreira's hotel version adapted over the years, not taken from anything in the article.
