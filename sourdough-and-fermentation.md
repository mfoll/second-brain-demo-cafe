# Sourdough And Fermentation

Everything about how our bread rises: the levain, hydration, retarding overnight, and the flour that feeds it. Kofi runs the bread; this is where his methods and my reading meet.

## Our bread in one paragraph

The house sourdough is a white loaf with 15% wholemeal, built on a stiff levain kept since 2019, mixed at 76% hydration and retarded at 4 degrees for 14 to 16 hours before the 04:30 bake. Since 2025-07-01 it uses Tarnwick Mill stone-ground flour, which drinks more water than the old roller-milled flour, so the hydration went up from 72% when we switched. See [[house-sourdough-recipe-card]].

## Daily rhythm

- 08:00 and 14:00: Kofi or Joel feeds the starter.
- 16:00: mix; three sets of folds over a three-hour bulk.
- 19:30: divide, shape, into baskets and the retarder at 4 °C.
- 04:30: early shift starts; first loaves in at 04:45, out by 05:25; second bake follows.

The retarder-proofer carries the whole schedule overnight, which is why its service reports matter ([[proofer-service-report-2026-02]], [[proofing-baking-technique]]).

## What changed in two years

- 2025-07: Tarnwick flour, hydration up four points, a darker crust and an extra day of keeping ([[flour-bake-trial-2025-05-14]]).
- 2025-09: the first new harvest from a single mill taught us to drop hydration for a fortnight each autumn ([[tarnwick-new-harvest-adjustment-2025-09-23]]).
- 2026-03: sourdough loaf from 4.80 to 5.20 EUR in the [[price-review-2026]]; food cost 24% ex VAT.
