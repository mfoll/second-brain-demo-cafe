# Butter Price Tracker

The monthly price per kg we pay Glenvarrow Dairy for block butter, with the kilos bought and the croissant food-cost line next to it. Started in January 2025 when the first rise of the year landed; updated from the first invoice of each month.

## Summary

> [!abstract]
> Butter went from 6.20 EUR/kg in January 2025 to 8.90 EUR/kg in September 2026, up 44%. The croissant's food cost went from 24% of its selling price to 31% by January 2026, which drove the March 2026 price rise; at 2.95 EUR it is back up to 29%.

## Why It Matters

This sheet is the evidence for the [[price-review-2026]] and for the [[dairy-contract-renegotiation]]. It is also what I open when someone asks why the croissant costs 2.95 EUR. Glenvarrow's fixed offer of 8.40 EUR/kg for November 2026 to April 2027 is judged against the last six rows.

Odd months are in the sheet; the table above keeps the turning points. Twelve months to September 2026: about 1,980 kg, roughly 16,400 EUR. The same twelve months at January 2025 prices would have cost about 12,300 EUR.

- The croissant uses about 45 g of butter (dough plus lamination block); at 8.90 that is 0.40 EUR of a 0.86 EUR food cost. The rest is flour, milk, sugar, eggs, yeast and egg wash, also up about 20% over the period. Breakdown in [[croissant-cost-breakdown]].
- Food cost here is against the shelf price, the way I read it at the counter. The [[recipe-costing-sheet]] has the ex-VAT version.

## Caveats

- Prices are Glenvarrow's invoice prices before any credit notes; two short deliveries in 2025 were credited later.
- The kilos include butter for cakes and the toasties, not only viennoiserie.
- One supplier's prices are not the market. Coldbeck's quote ([[second-dairy-product-specifications-2026-10]]) is the first outside comparison.
