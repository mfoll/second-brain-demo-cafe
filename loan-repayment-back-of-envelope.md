# Loan Repayment Back Of Envelope

Sunday evening sums, 2026-10-12, comparing the full loan with a smaller loan plus an equipment lease. Rough; check against the real lease quote.

| Option | Borrowed | Monthly | Total cost of money over 5 years |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pellow Mutual, full | 85,000 at 7.9% | 1,720 | about 18,200 + 850 fee |
| Loan 55,000 + lease 32,000 | 55,000 at 7.9%, 32,000 at about 9% | 1,113 + 664 = 1,777 | about 11,800 + 7,800 = 19,600 |

So the lease route costs about 1,400 EUR more over five years and 57 EUR more a month. What it buys: the bank's 25% own-contribution rule applies to a smaller project, and the equipment is not ours to sell if things go badly, which cuts both ways.
