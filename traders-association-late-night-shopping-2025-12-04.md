# Traders' Association Late-Night Shopping 2025-12-04

The Brackenmouth Traders' Association's Christmas late-night shopping evening, Thursday 4 December 2025. Hollowgate shops stayed open until 20:00; the street was closed to traffic from 17:00.

## What we did

- Stayed open 16:00 to 20:00 with a reduced counter: mulled apple juice, hot chocolate, mince pies, slices of stollen, and Christmas preorder forms.
- Staff: Lena, Grace and Finn on the counter, me on preorders. Kofi went home at the normal time.

## Numbers

- Takings 16:00 to 20:00: 1,312 EUR (Square).
- 31 Christmas preorders taken on the night, the best single day of the 2025 season.
- Extra staff cost for the evening about 260 EUR.

## What I would change

- More mince pies: sold out at 18:40.
- A separate table for preorders; the queue for drinks blocked it.
- [[harriet-lowe]] asked traders to post the same image on the same day; it worked well and we will do it again.

The 2026 evening is on Thursday 3 December ([[community-events-calendar-2026]]). The preorder table idea is in [[taking-christmas-preorders]].
