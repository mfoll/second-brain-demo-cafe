# Dominic Fairley

Former lunch cook at Hollowgate, from 2023 until 2026-03-27, when he left for a restaurant job in town. Kept here because the lunch decisions and their recipes trace back to him.

## What he did here

Dominic cooked the nine-item savoury lunch: shakshuka, the quiche plate, salad bowls, two soups and the toasties. He wrote most of the lunch recipes still in the [[recipe-costing-sheet]] and trained [[mateus-ferraz]] on the soups.

## How it ended

At [[team-meeting-2026-02-05]] I cut lunch to bakery only from 2026-02-23. Dominic raised the effect on his hours at the meeting; I offered him bakery and porter shifts to make up part of the time, which was not the job he wanted. He gave notice in March and left on good terms on 2026-03-27 ([[dominic-leaves-2026-03-27]]). With hindsight I should have talked to him before the meeting, not at it.
