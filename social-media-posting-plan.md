# Social Media Posting Plan

How we post, agreed with [[grace-oyelaran]] in January 2026 and revised in July after the Q2 figures ([[social-media-insights-summary-2026-q2]]).

## Rhythm

- Four posts a week: Tuesday (the week's bake), Thursday (behind the scenes in the basement), Saturday morning (what is on the counter now), Sunday (something seasonal or a supplier).
- Stories daily when something sells out or comes out of the oven.
- Grace posts at weekends; I post in the week. Nobody posts after 21:00.

## What works

- Basement photos at 05:00 with steam and trays: our best posts every quarter.
- Short clips of lamination or scoring.
- Supplier stories with permission: Quarrymoor eggs, Tarnwick Mill, Ombrel Roasters.

## What does not

- Discounts and offers: few likes, and they bring bargain hunters, not regulars.
- Text-only announcements. Use a photo even for the opening hours.

## Rules

- No customers' faces without asking. No children ever, including bake club.
- No staff in photos who have not said yes. Kofi is happy to appear; some of the team are not.
- No complaints or replies to criticism in public. Lena or I answer by direct message.
- Talia Reyes's professional photos are for the seasonal menus ([[talia-reyes]]).

## Christmas 2026

Two posts a week on preorders from mid-October, then a countdown on caps when a product passes 80%.
