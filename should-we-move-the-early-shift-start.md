# Should We Move The Early Shift Start

Could the early shift start at 05:00 instead of 04:30 on some days without bread reaching the shop late?

## Context

Joel asked in March 2026 for a 05:00 start on Saturdays and Sundays, when the first customers come later. The shop opens at 07:30 Tuesday to Saturday and 08:30 on Sunday.

## What we know

- Sunday opens at 08:30, so a 05:00 start is easy; we moved it from 2026-04-05 and it has worked.
- Saturday is the biggest bake and the shop is busiest from 10:00. Kofi says a 05:00 start means baguettes at 07:40 instead of 07:20; Lena says nobody buys baguettes before 08:00 on a Saturday anyway ([[square-pos-sales-data]]).
- Weekdays: 08:00-09:00 is the busiest hour. Bread must be on the shelf by 07:30.

## What would settle it

A four-week Saturday trial at 05:00 with the first bake moved to croissants and bread second. Planned for January 2027, after Christmas.

## Related

The second-site idea goes the other way, toward 03:00 ([[central-production-kitchen]]); this question would need reopening if that happens.
