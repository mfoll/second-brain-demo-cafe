# How Many Christmas Preorders Can The Basement Bake?

## Why It Matters

The 2026 target is 320 orders, half as many again as 2025. If we take more than we can bake, we fail on the two days people remember. If we cap too low, we lose deposits that carry January.

## Evidence So Far

- 2025: 214 orders, all delivered, but the bûche moulds were full on 23 December and Priyanka worked until 19:00 ([[christmas-2025-preorder-debrief]]).
- The new oven (from November 2025) has about 30% more deck space than the old one at the same temperature.
- Stollen is the bottleneck: 750 g each, a long rest, and 2025 sold 142. Kofi's estimate is 260 stollen across 19 to 22 December if Hana covers the morning bread.
- Bûche: 10 more moulds bought in October 2026, so 40 a day over three days.

## Current Answer

Provisional: 320 orders is possible if no single product passes its cap (stollen 240, bûche 110, mince pie boxes 260). Kofi thinks 320 is the ceiling; Lena thinks walk-in trade on 23 December will suffer either way.

## What Would Settle It

A test bake of 60 stollen on Monday 16 November with the full team, timed, and the order mix at 15 November. If stollen orders reach 200 by then we stop selling stollen and keep the other products open. Owner: me with [[kofi-asare]].
