# Deputy Timesheet Export Summer 2025

The approved Deputy timesheets for 2025-06-02 to 2025-08-31, exported on 2025-09-01 and summarised for the [[early-shift-overtime-review-2025-09-02]].

## Summary

Thirteen weeks of clocked hours for all staff, the first full quarter after the [[rota-system-switch]]. Only totals and averages are kept here.

## Key Details

| Group | Average hours a week | Highest week |
|---|---|---|
| Head baker | 47.0 | 54.5 (week of the oven failure) |
| Other early-shift baker | 41.5 | 46 |
| Pastry | 38 | 42 |
| Front of house (full-time) | 37.5 | 41 |
| Whole team, total | 286 hours a week | 312 |

- Early shift weekly hours rose from about 84 in June to 95 in August, when the hired oven meant longer bakes ([[deck-oven-replacement]]) and wholesale was still running.
- Odile's own bench hours are not in Deputy.

## Why It Matters

The paper rota had hidden this for years. It is the evidence behind the [[assistant-baker-hiring]] and the reason I hold the stance in [[no-one-should-routinely-work-more-than-45-hours]].

## Caveats

Clock-in started for the bakery only on 2025-05-13, so the summer is the first clean comparison. Hours include breaks taken on site.
