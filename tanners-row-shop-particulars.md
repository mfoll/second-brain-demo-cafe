# Tanner's Row Shop Particulars

The agent's particulars for the empty shop on Tanner's Row, sent by [[simone-ward]] on 2026-02-04.

## Summary

Ground-floor shop of 74 m2 with a 12 m2 back room and WC, former phone repair shop, available on a new ten-year lease at 2,100 EUR a month with four months rent-free.

## Why It Matters

The first second-site candidate ([[tanners-row-site]]). The particulars looked good; the absence of any food use history, which they did not mention, turned out to be the problem.

## Key Details

- Frontage 5.8 m, large window, level access.
- Use class: retail. A change to café use would need the council's consent.
- Service charge none; business rates estimated at 6,300 EUR a year.
- Three-phase power: not present, would need a new supply (quoted later at 4,800 EUR).
- Location: ten minutes' walk from Hollowgate, on the route from the station car park.

## Caveats

Agent's document; floor areas not measured by us.
