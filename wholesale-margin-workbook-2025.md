# Wholesale Margin Workbook 2025

My spreadsheet of wholesale sales, food cost and margin by customer and month, June to September 2025, finished the night before the pause decision.

## Summary

Wholesale ran at about 33% gross margin on food and packaging, below the 35% target. Counting the extra baker hours, it was close to break-even.

## Key Details

| Month | Loaves | Sales EUR | Gross margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025-06 | 1,260 | 3,290 | 35% |
| 2025-07 | 1,310 | 3,420 | 33% |
| 2025-08 | 1,250 | 3,260 | 32% |
| 2025-09 | 1,290 | 3,370 | 32% |

- By customer: Pier Street about 34%, Lantern about 31% (smaller, more mixed orders).
- Margin fell after Tarnwick flour from 1 July (about 4 cents a loaf) and higher packaging costs.
- Extra baker hours: about 15 a week. At full employer cost that is about 260 EUR a week, roughly equal to the gross profit.

## Why It Matters

The core evidence in [[why-did-we-pause-wholesale-to-cafes]], and the source of the baker-time line in [[wholesale-pricing-floor]].

## Caveats

Baker hours are estimates from Deputy, comparing weeks before and after the start; nobody clocked wholesale separately.
