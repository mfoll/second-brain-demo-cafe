# VAT Return Q4 2024

The VAT return for October to December 2024, prepared from the old spreadsheet and filed on 2025-01-20, with payment of 11,800 EUR due on 2025-01-31.

## Summary

Output VAT on 167,400 EUR of December-quarter sales less input VAT on purchases gave a liability of 11,800 EUR, the largest quarter of the year because of Christmas.

## Why It Matters

This is the payment that, together with the [[oven-repair-invoice-january-2025]], emptied the account in January 2025 ([[overdraft-squeeze-january-2025]]). The money had been collected in December and spent on stock and wages by mid-January. That is why we now move VAT into a separate pot every week ([[should-we-set-aside-vat-weekly]], [[vat-money-is-not-our-money]]).

## Key Details

- Quarter: 2024-10-01 to 2024-12-31. Filed 2025-01-20, paid 2025-01-31.
- Liability 11,800 EUR, against 8,900 EUR in Q4 2023.
- Prepared by me from the spreadsheet, reviewed by [[ruth-marchbank]]'s team in two hours.
