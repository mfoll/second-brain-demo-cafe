# Menu Refresh 2026

The year's menu work under one roof: new prices, a smaller lunch and a seasonal calendar, all aimed at holding margin while butter, chocolate and eggs kept going up.

## Outcome

A spring and an autumn menu that keep overall gross margin at 68% or better despite ingredient price rises.

## Current State

- Spring menu live from 2026-03-09 with new prices ([[spring-menu-2026]]); autumn menu live from 2026-09-22 ([[autumn-menu-2026]]).
- Overall gross margin from Xero, April to September 2026: 68.6%, against 66.9% for the same months of 2025. On target, just.
- Two children done ([[price-review-2026]], [[savoury-lunch-trim]]); [[seasonal-bake-calendar]] running.
- The butter decision ([[dairy-contract-renegotiation]]) decides whether viennoiserie needs another price move before spring 2027.

## Scope and Boundaries

Counter, coffee and lunch menus at Hollowgate. Christmas preorders are their own project ([[christmas-preorders-2026]]) though they use the same costings. A second-site menu is not designed here.

## Working Context

In January the [[recipe-costing-sheet]] showed bakery at about 71% gross margin and lunch at 51%, with the croissant's food cost up from 24% to 31% of the selling price in a year. The plan was three moves: raise prices once and properly, stop the lunch dragging the average down, and plan specials around cheap seasonal produce instead of inventing them on Friday. The lunch move overshot and was partly reversed; see [[savoury-lunch-trim]] and the reflection in [[lunch-should-stay-small-but-not-disappear]].
