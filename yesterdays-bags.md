# Yesterday's Bags

Our name for the paper bags of three day-old pastries sold at half their combined price from opening the next morning. Started 6 May 2025.

## Rules

- Plain and fruit viennoiserie only (croissants, pains au chocolat, pains aux raisins, fruit danishes). Nothing with cream, custard or cooked fillings.
- Bagged at closing, labelled "baked yesterday" with the date and allergens, kept in the dry store overnight.
- On the counter at opening, in a basket to the left of the till; off the counter at 13:00 or when gone. Never sold two days after baking.
- Not part of the loyalty scheme.

## Why it works

It recovers some value without teaching people to come late for discounts. The customers are mostly early: students, builders, people who want three pastries for the office.
