# Can We Get The Head Baker Under 42 Hours

Can Kofi Asare's average working week come under 42 hours and stay there, with the current range and without wholesale?

## Current state

- Summer 2025: 47 hours average ([[deputy-timesheet-export-summer-2025]]).
- Winter 2025-26, after one fixed day off and wholesale paused: about 45.
- September and October 2026, with [[hana-kobayashi]] on four mornings: 43.5 ([[rota-pattern-autumn-2026]]).

## What stands in the way

- Fridays: the heaviest bake plus lamination; Kofi stays until 15:00.
- Ordering and planning sit with Kofi. Some could move to me.
- Hana does not yet run a section alone.

## What would settle it

Hana running the Tuesday and Thursday bake with Joel from December 2026, and Friday ordering moved to me. If the average for January to March 2027 is still above 42, the answer is a fourth baker or a smaller range.

## Why it matters beyond Kofi

Any second-site plan that bakes at Hollowgate adds hours here first ([[central-production-kitchen]], [[second-site-review-2026-09-30]]).
