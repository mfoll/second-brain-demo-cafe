# Forecast First Monday 2025-03-31

The first Monday the [[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]] was updated as routine rather than as a project, two days after the [[cash-flow-forecast]] project closed.

## What Happened

Took 55 minutes, with Square and the bank open side by side. The forecast showed the April rent quarter and the Q1 VAT payment comfortably covered, lowest week 24,800 EUR, and the first sign of a summer problem: July has the Q2 VAT, the July rent quarter and the holiday payroll in the same three weeks.

## Decisions

- Monday morning, before the shop's weekly order calls, is forecast time. Written into [[update-the-cash-flow-forecast]].
- VAT pot transfer every Monday at 9% of the previous week's gross takings ([[should-we-set-aside-vat-weekly]]).
- Any week under 15,000 EUR gets a written plan the same day.

## Notes

By late May it took 25 minutes. By August 2025, after the oven failure, it was the only reason I knew we could afford the hired oven.
