# Second Dairy Product Specifications 2026-10

Coldbeck Creamery's spec sheets for its block butter and dough butter, left by Nils Jorgensen on 2026-10-14. No prices; the written quote is due by 2026-10-30.

## Summary

Lamination block: 82% fat, sold in 2 kg plates, recommended working temperature 12 to 15 °C. Dough butter: 82% fat, 10 kg cartons. Delivery twice a week (Tuesday and Friday), minimum order 40 kg.

## Why It Matters

The comparison point for the [[dairy-contract-renegotiation]]. Plates instead of blocks would save Priyanka about ten minutes a batch of shaping; that is worth something even at the same price. The twice-weekly delivery means more cold storage per drop than Glenvarrow's daily runs.

## Key Details

- Glenvarrow's block is also 82% fat but comes in 5 kg blocks we cut and pound into sheets.
- Shelf life chilled: 10 weeks for plates.

## Caveats

Specs only. Nils's "around 8.20" is verbal. The first lamination test is one batch ([[priyanka-email-butter-sheet-trial-2026-10]]).
