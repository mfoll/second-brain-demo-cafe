# Samir Belkacem

Year 4 teacher at Saltings Lane Primary School and our partner for the [[school-bake-club]]. He wrote to us in September 2025 asking for help with a "field to table" topic, and the club grew out of it ([[saltings-lane-primary-partnership-email]]).

## Role

- Runs the class side of every session: consent, supervision, pickup, and telling us which allergens to leave out for that group.
- Books the school food room and its two domestic ovens.

## How we work together

- We plan the year in one meeting in September and agree dates for the spring term before February half-term.
- He sends the allergen exclusions for the group by email a week before each session; we never receive children's names or medical details ([[bake-club-allergy-consent-form]]).
- He prefers short emails and a phone call the day before.
