# Christmas Preorder Planning 2026-09-22

Planning meeting for [[christmas-preorders-2026]] after closing on Tuesday 22 September 2026, with Lena, Kofi and Priyanka.

## Decisions

- Target 320 orders, up from 214. Close orders on 12 December or at the product caps.
- Deposit stays at 30%, same refund rule as 2025 ([[should-christmas-deposits-be-refundable]]).
- Prices up about 6%: stollen 24.00 EUR, bûche 34.00 EUR, mince pies box of 6 13.50 EUR, fruited sourdough 9.50 EUR, gingerbread tin 16.00 EUR. New this year: the gingerbread tin, which keeps and can be made early.
- Collection slots by half hour again, with a separate collection table by the window.
- 10 more bûche moulds; Priyanka to own the bûche plan.
- Orders open Thursday 1 October.

## Disagreements

- Kofi: 320 is the ceiling, and only if Hana takes the morning bread from 19 December. He wanted 280. We settled on 320 with hard caps per product ([[how-many-christmas-preorders-can-the-basement-bake]]).
- Lena: walk-in customers on the 23rd suffered in 2025. Agreed a fixed 80 loaves for the counter on the 23rd before any order bread.

## Cash

I said the deposits would go into the [[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]] only as orders come in. Ruth Marchbank had asked for that while we worked through the buffer calculation for the second-site review ([[cash-buffer-calculation-2026-09]]).
