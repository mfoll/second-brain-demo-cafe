# Autumn Menu 2026

Live from 2026-09-22. Same core counter and prices as the [[spring-menu-2026]]; the changes are seasonal.

## What changed

- Specials: plum and frangipane tart (4.60), apple and cheddar scone (3.60). Plums finished in the second week of October; the tart switched to pear on 2026-10-13.
- Pumpkin loaf by the slice from October (3.40).
- Soup rota moved to roast squash, leek and potato and tomato and lentil for Mateus.
- Hot chocolate with Kestrin's dark couverture back on the board from 2026-10-01.

## Early numbers

First four weeks: specials at 70% gross margin, best of the year. The apple and cheddar scone outsold the plain scone two to one on Saturdays.
