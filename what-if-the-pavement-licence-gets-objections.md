# What If The Pavement Licence Gets Objections

If someone objects to the extra side-street covers before 2026-11-02, what do we do, and what do we give up?

## Possible objections

- Walkway or access: answered by the plan, which Aled Vaughan has already accepted.
- The gift shop window: answered by the 1.2 m gap; Harriet Lowe has said she supports it.
- Noise or smoking from the residents above number 16: the most likely real objection. We close at 16:00, which helps.

## Fallback positions

1. Offer to bring the side-street tables in at 16:00 instead of 18:00.
2. Drop to one side-street table (3 covers).
3. Withdraw the extension and renew the 12 covers only. The renewal must not be put at risk.

## What would settle it

The end of the consultation on 2026-11-02 and Aled Vaughan's decision, expected by the end of November.
