# Contribution margin

## Summary

Defines contribution margin as selling price less variable cost per unit: the part of each sale available to cover fixed costs and then profit. It is presented as the basis of break-even analysis.

## Why It Matters

From the [[food-business-finance-course]]. It explained something the lunch debate later needed: dropping a product saves its variable costs but loses its contribution to fixed costs. That is part of why the [[savoury-lunch-trim]] hurt more than the margin table suggested ([[fixed-and-variable-costs]]).

## Caveats

Deciding which costs are variable is a judgement. Our payroll is fixed week to week and variable over months.
