# Oven Repair Invoice January 2025

Invoice from [[colm-ashworth]] for an emergency repair to the old deck oven, 3,400 EUR including parts, paid on 2025-01-14.

## Summary

The middle deck's heating element and its controller failed on Saturday 2025-01-11. Colm came on the Monday and replaced both. The oven was then 14 years old.

## Why It Matters

It landed in the same fortnight as the rent quarter and the Q4 VAT payment and tipped the account into the overdraft ([[overdraft-squeeze-january-2025]]). In hindsight it was also the first warning of the failure on 2025-07-29 that led to the [[deck-oven-replacement]].

## Key Details

- Labour 6 hours, call-out on a closed day, parts (element, controller board).
- Total 3,400 EUR including VAT; input VAT reclaimed in Q1 2025.
- Colm's note on the invoice: the oven had "two or three years left at best". It had six months.
