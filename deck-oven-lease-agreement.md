# Deck Oven Lease Agreement

Five-year equipment lease for the new three-deck oven, signed 2025-09-26 with an equipment finance company introduced by the oven supplier.

## Key Details

- Equipment value 41,500 EUR; term 60 months from installation (November 2025 to October 2030).
- Monthly payment 812 EUR, by direct debit on the 1st, first payment 2025-12-01.
- Total payable 48,720 EUR, so the finance cost is 7,220 EUR over five years.
- Option to buy for a nominal 100 EUR at the end.
- We must keep a service contract and insurance in place.

## Why It Matters

Leasing kept the cash buffer intact going into the January VAT quarter. The 812 EUR is a fixed line in the [[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]], and Ruth counts it as a commitment when looking at second-site borrowing ([[second-site-financing]]). The reasoning is in [[should-we-buy-or-lease-the-deck-oven]].
