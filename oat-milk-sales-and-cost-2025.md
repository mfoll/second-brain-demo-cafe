# Oat Milk Sales And Cost 2025

Amira's summary of plant milk drinks for 2025 from the Square modifier export, with the cost of a barista oat milk per drink.

## Summary

Oat milk was chosen in 19% of milk drinks in 2025, up from 13% in 2024. The barista oat milk costs 2.35 EUR a litre against 1.18 for Glenvarrow whole milk. A flat white uses about 160 ml of milk including waste, so oat costs about 0.19 EUR more per drink. The 0.40 EUR surcharge covers that with some left for the extra jugs and waste.

## Why It Matters

Settled the surcharge question for the [[price-review-2026]]: keep it at 0.40, do not raise it, do not drop it ([[should-we-keep-the-oat-milk-surcharge]]).

## Key Details

- About 31 oat drinks a day across the week.
- Waste is higher with oat because it is steamed in smaller jugs.
- No other plant milk sold enough to keep; soy dropped in October 2025.

## Caveats

Square counts modifiers, not litres; litres come from the supplier invoices divided by drinks.
