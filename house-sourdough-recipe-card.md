# House Sourdough Recipe Card

Kofi's card for the house sourdough, rewritten on 2025-07-08 after the move to Tarnwick flour. The version on the basement wall.

## Summary

For 40 loaves of 900 g: 17 kg Tarnwick strong white, 3 kg Tarnwick wholemeal, 15.2 kg water (76%), 4 kg stiff levain, 400 g salt. Mix at 16:00, bulk 3 hours with three sets of folds, divide and shape by 19:30, retard at 4 °C for 14 to 16 hours, bake from 04:45 at 250 °C falling to 230 °C, 40 minutes with steam for the first 12.

## Why It Matters

The recipe behind the shop's best-selling loaf and the one the [[flour-supplier-review]] changed: hydration up from 72% to 76%, salt unchanged. Costed in the [[recipe-costing-sheet]] at 1.12 EUR a loaf.

## Key Details

- [[levain]] fed at 08:00 and 14:00, 50% hydration.
- After a new harvest Kofi drops hydration by one or two points for the first fortnight and works back up.
- Proof test: the poke test, and the basket should feel lighter than it looks.

## Caveats

Quantities are for the summer bake; in winter the bulk runs nearer 4 hours.
