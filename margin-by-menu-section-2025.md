# Margin By Menu Section 2025

Gross margin for each sales section for calendar 2025, from Xero sales by account against purchases allocated by section, pulled on 2026-01-19 for the menu work.

## Summary

Bakery 71%, drinks 78%, savoury lunch 51%, wholesale 33%; whole shop 69.4% on 610,000 EUR of turnover.

## Why It Matters

This is the table I took to the [[team-meeting-2026-02-05]], where we decided to cut lunch to bakery only. It also showed wholesale under its 35% floor ([[why-did-we-pause-wholesale-to-cafes]]).

## Key Details

| Section | Sales EUR | Share | Gross margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bakery counter (incl. preorders) | 302,000 | 49.5% | 71% |
| Drinks | 205,000 | 33.6% | 78% |
| Savoury lunch | 82,000 | 13.4% | 51% |
| Wholesale (June-October) | 21,000 | 3.4% | 33% |
| **Total** | **610,000** | | **69.4%** |

Lunch also needed about 30 cook hours a week, which gross margin does not show ([[prime-cost]]).

## Caveats

Ingredient allocation between bakery and lunch is approximate: butter, eggs and flour are shared. A 2-point error either way would not change the ranking. Turnover rounded; the accounts say 609,800 EUR ([[marchbank-ochoa-year-end-accounts-2025]]).
