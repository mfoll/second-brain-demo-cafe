# Food Safety Plan Briefing 2025-04-14

A Monday briefing, paid, 10:00 to 12:00 in the café with the shop closed, to introduce version 3 of the [[food-safety-management-plan]] and the new daily sheets.

## Notes

- All nine staff attended. Kofi explained the four critical control points in his own words, which landed better than my slides.
- Walkthrough of the sheet at the counter and in the basement, with everyone filling in a practice sheet.
- Questions from the team: what happens if a fridge reads 6 °C at opening (answer on the sheet), and whether front of house needs the probe (yes, for deliveries when the bakery has gone home).
- Nobody asked about cooling, and I did not notice that the sheet we handed out had no cooling section. That gap surfaced at the [[environmental-health-inspection-2025-06-18]].
