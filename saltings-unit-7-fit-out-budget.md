# Saltings Unit 7 Fit-Out Budget

The budget for fitting out and opening unit 7, put together from three supplier quotes and my own estimates in May and June 2026.

## Summary

120,000 EUR in total: 84,500 EUR of fit-out and equipment, 9,500 EUR of opening costs and 26,000 EUR of working capital to carry the unit through its first months.

## Why It Matters

It sets the size of the [[second-site-financing]] problem and the investment in the [[saltings-hall-three-scenario-model]].

## Key Details

| Line | EUR |
|---|---|
| Counter, joinery, display case | 31,000 |
| Espresso machine (two-group) and grinders | 14,500 |
| Convection oven, proofer, fridges, freezer | 17,800 |
| Electrics, plumbing, hand-wash sink | 9,200 |
| Signage, smallwares, packaging stock | 5,000 |
| Contingency (about 9%) | 7,000 |
| **Fit-out subtotal** | **84,500** |
| Lease deposit (three months' rent) | 4,350 |
| Recruitment, training, two weeks' pre-opening wages | 5,150 |
| Working capital | 26,000 |
| **Total** | **120,000** |

The equipment lines (about 32,000 EUR) could be leased instead of bought, which is the alternative in [[second-site-financing]].

## Caveats

Joinery quote valid until 2026-12-31; espresso machine price assumes the same model as Hollowgate's from [[coffee-roaster-change]] supplier terms. No allowance for a delayed hall opening beyond the contingency.
