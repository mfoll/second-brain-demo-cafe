# Waste Percentage By Value

How we state food waste: the value at selling price of bakery products left at closing, divided by the value at selling price of everything baked that day.

## Definition

waste % = sum(left at closing × shelf price) / sum(baked × shelf price), bakery products only, per day, summed over the month.

## Why by value, not by weight or count

- By weight, bread dominates and a tray of croissants looks like nothing. By value, a croissant left over counts as 2.95 EUR, which is what it cost us in lost sales.
- By count, a mini roll and a whole stollen count the same.
- Selling price rather than cost: the decision we take with the number is how much to bake, and what we lose by over-baking is mostly margin, not ingredients.

## Limits

- It overstates the money lost, since only food cost (about a quarter to a third of the price) is actually spent.
- It ignores what we recover: yesterday's bags and Larder donations are not netted off. That is deliberate; recovery should not hide over-baking.
- Lunch items are left out because their shelf life and logging are different.
