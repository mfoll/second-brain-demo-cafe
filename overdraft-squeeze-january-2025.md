# Overdraft Squeeze January 2025

The fortnight in January 2025 when the bakehouse ran on its full overdraft. The account was overdrawn from 2025-01-14 to 2025-02-01 and hit the 6,000 EUR limit on 2025-01-31 when the Q4 VAT payment went out.

## What Happened

- 2025-01-01: rent quarter of 9,750 EUR to [[cyril-ashdown]].
- 2025-01-11: the old deck oven's middle deck failed on a Saturday; [[colm-ashworth]] repaired it on the Monday for 3,400 EUR ([[oven-repair-invoice-january-2025]]).
- January takings were 31% below December, as every year.
- 2025-01-31: the Q4 2024 VAT payment of 11,800 EUR ([[vat-return-q4-2024]]).

Statement detail: [[pellow-mutual-overdraft-statement-january-2025]].

## What We Did

- Asked Glenvarrow Dairy and Fold & Carry Packaging to take payment a week late; both agreed without fuss.
- Called [[devon-pryce]] on 2025-01-29. He allowed us to sit at the limit through the weekend and asked for a cash forecast by March.
- Paid wages on time. That was the one line I would not move.

## Afterwards

I was angry with myself more than worried: the year had been profitable, and every one of these payments was known in advance. The [[cash-flow-forecast]] project started on 2025-02-03, and the lessons are in [[cash-squeeze-lessons-january-2025]] and [[why-did-we-run-out-of-cash-in-january-2025]].
