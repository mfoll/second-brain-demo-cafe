# Deputy And Alternatives Comparison

One-page comparison Lena and I made in March 2025 of three ways to replace the paper rota.

## Summary

| Option | Monthly cost (9 staff) | Swaps and leave | Clock-in | Payroll export | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deputy | about 45 | In app, manager approves | Kiosk tablet and phone | Matches Marchbank Ochoa's columns | Chosen |
| A second scheduling app | about 30 | In app | Phone only, extra fee for kiosk | Manual reformat | Rejected |
| Shared spreadsheet | 0 | By message, as now | None | Manual | Rejected |

## Why It Matters

This is why we pay for Deputy. The deciding points were the kiosk at the back door (the early shift do not carry phones in the bakery) and an export that Ruth's payroll clerk could take as it is. The spreadsheet would have been free and fixed nothing about the two Saturdays we opened short-staffed.

## Caveats

Prices were the published plans in March 2025 and have since risen. We did not trial the second app; we watched its demo only.
