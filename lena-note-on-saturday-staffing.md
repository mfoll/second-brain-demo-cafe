# Lena Note On Saturday Staffing

A note Lena sent me on 2026-02-16, summarised.

> [!note]
> Lena's main point: Saturdays need five on the floor from 09:30 to 13:30, not four, and she cannot keep opening every weekend herself.

Her points:

- Saturday 10:00-11:00 is our busiest hour of the week (she checked [[square-pos-sales-data]]). With four on, the queue goes out of the door and the tables do not get cleared. Coffee tickets wait 8 to 10 minutes.
- She worked 22 of the last 26 weekends.
- Suggests a fifth person on Saturdays from 09:30 to 13:30 (Finn in term time, a new weekend hire later) and a weekend supervisor so she can have more weekends off.
- Grace is happy to open Sundays but does not want to supervise.

What I did: fifth person on Saturdays from March 2026 (about 16 extra hours a month), and Lena off two weekends a month with me opening one of them. The supervisor idea is deferred ([[should-we-hire-a-weekend-supervisor]]).
