# Flour Bake Trial 2025-05-14

Blind side-by-side bake of the house sourdough with three flours: the old wholesaler's strong white (A), Tarnwick Mill's stone-ground strong white (B) and a second regional mill (C). Baked by Kofi and Joel on the early shift, tasted at 14:00 and again the next day.

## At a Glance

> [!success]
> Tarnwick (B) won on crust, crumb, taste and keeping with all six tasters: 104 points out of 120, against 71 for the old flour. It needed 76% hydration to feel like the old dough at 72%.

## How we ran it

- Same levain, same salt, same retard, same oven deck. Water adjusted per flour by Kofi to reach the same dough feel; recorded rather than fixed.
- Two levain builds for each flour, the first a week before, because the flour changes the starter too ([[sourdough]]).
- Loaves labelled A, B, C on the base by Joel; tasters did not know which was which.
- Scores in [[flour-bake-trial-scores-2025]].

## Observations

- B browned faster at the same oven setting ([[maillard-reaction]]); Kofi dropped the bottom deck by 10 degrees for the repeat.
- B's crumb was more open and slightly creamier in colour; C was close but drier on day 2.
- B was still good toasted on day 3, which matters for the yesterday's bags.

## Decision

Go to Tarnwick for bread flours, subject to the agreement terms ([[tarnwick-mill-supply-agreement-2025]]). Pastry flour to be tested separately by Priyanka; she tested it on 2025-05-28 and said no ([[should-pastry-flour-move-to-tarnwick]]). Repeat bake of A and B on 2025-05-21 matched the result.
