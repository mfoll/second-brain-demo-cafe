# Cooling Log Sheet

The cooling section added to the daily check sheet on 2025-06-19, the day after the inspection, and written into the plan as version 3.1 on 2025-07-11.

## Key Details

One line per batch: product, batch size, time out of the pan or oven and core temperature, time and temperature at 2 hours (below 20 °C), time and temperature at 6 hours (below 5 °C), initials, and "action if missed". The tray label carries the two check times so whoever is on can find them without reading the sheet.

Typical batches: ham and leek filling 4 kg, spinach and feta 3 kg, soup 12 litres in four shallow containers. In practice the fillings reach 20 °C in about 70 minutes on the rack by the extractor; soup in deep pots did not, which is why soup goes into shallow containers.

## Why It Matters

This line is the point we lost in June 2025 ([[environmental-health-inspection-2025-06-18]]). Since then it has been filled in every day the fillings are made; Imogen Hale checked a random fortnight at the re-rating visit ([[re-rating-visit-2025-10-07]]).

## Source Location

Back of the daily sheet; completed sheets in the red binder by week.
