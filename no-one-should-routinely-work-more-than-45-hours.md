# No One Should Routinely Work More Than 45 Hours

## Stance

Nobody on our team should work more than 45 hours a week as a normal pattern, and on the early shift the aim is 42. Odd weeks happen; a summer of them is a planning failure, and it is mine, not theirs.

## Scope

All staff, and especially anyone starting before 05:00. It does not cover me, which is a separate problem.

## Why I think this

- The Deputy export showed Kofi at 47 hours for a whole summer ([[deputy-timesheet-export-summer-2025]]). He did not complain until asked.
- Tired bakers make the mistakes that matter: a missed cooling reading, a burn, a wrong label.
- Early starts on top of long weeks are hard on people's health and sleep ([[shift-work]]).
- I worked 60-hour weeks in a hotel kitchen under Anselm Ferreira in my twenties and do not want to run that kind of kitchen.

## What would change my mind

Little. The question is how to meet it, not whether.

## Implications

Hours are a cost of any growth plan. A second site that needs the early shift to start at 03:00 fails this test unless it comes with more bakers ([[central-production-kitchen]]).
