# A Second Site Must Not Depend On The Overdraft

## Stance

> [!info]
> We should only open a second site when Hollowgate could carry the new site's worst realistic year without touching the overdraft. If the plan only works in the base case, it is not ready.

## Scope

Any expansion with fixed commitments: a lease, a loan, permanent staff. Not small trials like a market stall or a pop-up.

## Why I Think This

- January 2025 showed how fast a profitable shop runs out of cash ([[overdraft-squeeze-january-2025]]).
- The unit 7 low case loses about 1,100 EUR a month and pushes Hollowgate under its floor in January 2028 ([[saltings-hall-three-scenario-model]]).
- [[ruth-marchbank]]: "Your profit is fine. Your cash is a seasonal animal" ([[year-end-meeting-with-ruth-2026-03-18]]).
- [[margot-elling]]'s second hotel ([[mentor-session-margot-2026-10-06]]).

## What Would Change My Mind

- Trading evidence from the hall in its first months that makes the low case implausible. That would mean waiting for it to open, which loses unit 7.
- A financing structure where the unit's losses cannot reach Hollowgate. I do not think one exists for a business our size.
- Ruth agreeing that a buffer of about 80,000 EUR is enough ([[how-big-a-cash-buffer-do-we-need]]).

## Implications

The December decision turns on the 2026-11-30 forecast rerun, not on enthusiasm or on the hall's deadline ([[should-we-open-a-second-site]]).
