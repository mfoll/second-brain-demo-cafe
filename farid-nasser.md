# Farid Nasser

Tutor of the evening course in food business finance at Brackenmouth College, which I took from September to December 2025. Ran a catering company for fifteen years before teaching.

## How We Work Together

- Twelve Monday-evening sessions in a class of fourteen owners and managers. He answered questions by email between sessions, with a strict two-line limit he enforced on himself.
- Since the course, an occasional email when I am stuck on a model; he answered once about the [[saltings-hall-three-scenario-model]].

## What I Took From Him

- Separate margin from cash. A profitable month can still empty the bank, which was exactly my January 2025 ([[overdraft-squeeze-january-2025]]).
- [[prime-cost]] as the weekly number to watch, not food cost alone.
- Every new site needs a [[break-even-month]], not just a break-even turnover.

## Current Threads

None open. He asked whether he could use the Hollowgate break-even model, anonymised, in next year's course. I said yes on condition no figures identify the shop.
