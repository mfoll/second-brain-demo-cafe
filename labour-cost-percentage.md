# Labour Cost Percentage

Total staff cost (wages, employer contributions, holiday pay) as a percentage of net takings, measured by month and by year. With food cost, it is one of the two numbers that decide whether the shop makes money.

## Our figures

- 2025: about 36% of net takings, up from 33% in 2024. The rise came from the summer overtime and the hired-oven months ([[deputy-timesheet-export-summer-2025]]).
- Target: 33 to 35%. The assistant baker added about 4.5 points of cost at first; the aim is that fewer overtime hours and holiday cover without me on the bench pay back part of it.
- Monthly figure comes from Xero payroll against net sales; weekly estimate from Deputy hours against Square takings.

## How I use it

- Not as a reason to cut people. As a signal: a high month usually means a broken rota or a broken oven.
- With [[gross-profit-margin]] for menu decisions. The savoury lunch looked worse once its 30 cook hours a week were counted ([[savoury-lunch-trim]]).

## Caveats

Odile's own hours are not in the figure, which flatters it.
