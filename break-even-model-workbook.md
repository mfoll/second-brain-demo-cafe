# Break-Even Model Workbook

The break-even model built as the final assignment of the [[food-business-finance-course]] and updated each quarter after the VAT return. Last update 6 July 2026 with Q2 2026 figures.

## Summary

Monthly fixed costs about 32,400 EUR (since the new oven lease), contribution margin about 66%, break-even net sales about 49,100 EUR a month. 2025 average net sales were 50,800 EUR.

## Key Details

Fixed costs, monthly, 2026:

| Line | EUR |
|---|---|
| Wages and employer costs (all staff, rota-based) | 21,900 |
| Rent, 14 Hollowgate | 3,650 |
| Oven lease (from November 2025) | 790 |
| Other equipment leases | 420 |
| Utilities | 2,250 |
| Insurance | 380 |
| Software (Square, Deputy, Xero) | 210 |
| Accountant | 450 |
| Repairs reserve, cleaning, pest control, sundries | 2,350 |
| Total | 32,400 |

Contribution margin by section (Q2 2026): bakery about 71%, coffee about 78%, lunch about 55% after the partial reinstatement.

## Why It Matters

It gives the daily takings Lena watches and the bar every decision has to clear ([[break-even-takings]]). It is also the starting point for the second-site models: a second shop needs its own version of this sheet.

## Caveats

Section margins split shared costs such as card fees by takings, which is approximate. Wages include the assistant baker from August 2026.
