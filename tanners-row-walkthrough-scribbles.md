- 74 m2, maybe 24 covers + counter. Back room 12 m2: enough for a small oven only if there is a flue. There isn't.
- No 3-phase. Ask cost. (Later: 4,800 EUR.)
- Floor: vinyl over concrete, one step at the back door. Deliveries via the lane, van can stop 10 min.
- Flats above x3, owner-occupied. Flue up the back = their windows. Simone pulled a face.
- Kofi: "you could bake croissants in here or you could have neighbours, not both."
- Car park walk-past 07:30-09:00 looked steady, I counted 63 people in 15 minutes standing outside.
- Rent 2,100/month, 4 months free. Rates ~6,300/yr.
- Gut: the street is right, the building is wrong.
