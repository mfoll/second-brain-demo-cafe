# Pellow Mutual Loan Conditions Email

[[devon-pryce]]'s email of 2026-09-15 answering my questions about the conditions behind the [[pellow-mutual-second-site-loan-offer]]. Summarised here; the email is in the shared mailbox.

## Summary

Three conditions: we put in at least 25% of the project cost ourselves; we send quarterly management accounts from Xero within six weeks of quarter end; the loan is secured by a debenture, with no personal guarantee required because of the company's trading record.

## Why It Matters

The 25% own contribution means at least 30,000 EUR of our cash goes into unit 7 on top of the loan, which is exactly the money [[ruth-marchbank]] wants kept as buffer ([[how-big-a-cash-buffer-do-we-need]]). No personal guarantee was the condition I cared most about.

## Key Details

- Own contribution: 25% of 120,000 EUR = 30,000 EUR minimum.
- Covenants: none on ratios; reporting only.
- The overdraft stays separate at 6,000 EUR and would be reviewed in February 2027 as usual.
- He would consider holding the offer to 2027-01-31 "if the file is otherwise unchanged". Not confirmed in writing.
