# Summer 2025 Early Shift Pressure

A synthesis of why the early shift was overloaded from June to October 2025, pulled together for the wholesale and hiring decisions.

## What piled up

- **Wholesale** from 2025-06-02: about 60 loaves a day for Pier Street Coffee and Lantern Books, an hour a day of shaping and packing on top of the shop bake ([[wholesale-to-cafes]]).
- **The oven**: one deck from 2025-07-29, then a smaller hired oven from 2025-08-06 ([[deck-oven-replacement]]). Bakes ran longer.
- **Holidays**: I was away for a week in August, so Kofi covered my viennoiserie mornings.
- **The cooling log** added about 15 minutes a day from June ([[cooling-log-sheet]]). Worth it, but not free.

## What it cost

Kofi averaged 47 hours a week and Joel 41.5 ([[deputy-timesheet-export-summer-2025]]). Labour cost rose to about 36% of takings for the year ([[labour-cost-percentage]]).

## What followed

A fixed day off for Kofi (September 2025), wholesale paused (2025-10-20, [[why-did-we-pause-wholesale-to-cafes]]), the new oven (2025-11-14) and, too late, the [[assistant-baker-hiring]] in 2026.
