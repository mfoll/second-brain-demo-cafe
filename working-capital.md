# Working capital

## Summary

Defines working capital as current assets less current liabilities, a measure of a business's operating liquidity, and describes a working capital deficit when liabilities exceed assets.

## Why It Matters

Assigned reading in week 6 of the [[food-business-finance-course]]. [[farid-nasser]] used it to explain why a café can be profitable and short of cash at once. It helped me see the VAT pot and Christmas deposits as liabilities, not assets of ours ([[cash-buffer]]), and it is where the working capital line in the [[saltings-unit-7-fit-out-budget]] comes from.

## Caveats

A balance-sheet snapshot. Our problem is the worst week of the year, which a year-end figure does not show.
