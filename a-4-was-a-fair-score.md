# A 4 Was A Fair Score

## Stance

The 4 out of 5 we got on 2025-06-18 was fair, and treating it as fair, not arguing it, is what got the 5 back quickly.

## Why I think this

- The cooling was being done but could not be shown. An inspector has only the records and what she sees.
- The sink was blocked. It does not matter that the delivery had come an hour earlier.
- Imogen explained the score clearly and the letter matched what she said ([[inspection-report-letter-2025-06]]).
- Kofi was angry for a day; once the cooling sheet was in, he agreed it made the bakery's work visible.

## What would change my mind

Nothing about that visit. If a future rating seemed inconsistent with the letter, the scheme allows an appeal and I would use it.

## Implications

React to an inspection with fixes and photos within a week, request the re-rating once the records show three clean months ([[re-rating-visit-2025-10-07]]).
