# June Adeyemo

Regular customer since 2021 and author of a local food blog about Brackenmouth cafés and markets. Comes in most Tuesdays and Fridays for a flat white and whatever the seasonal bake is.

## Why she has a note

She is one of the few customers whose opinion reaches a lot of other people, and she agreed to be named here. She wrote kindly about the bakery in 2024 and critically about the lunch cut in March 2026: her post asked where people were supposed to eat a hot lunch on Hollowgate on a weekday. When the soup came back on 20 April 2026 she wrote a follow-up ([[june-adeyemo-blog-post-soup-return]]).

## How we work together

- No arrangement. She pays for everything and we do not give her anything free; we agreed that early on so her posts stay honest.
- I sometimes ask her opinion on a new bake before it goes on the counter. She tasted two rounds of the vegan croissant trials ([[vegan-bake-trials-log]]) and was blunt about the first.

## Current threads

- She has asked to write about the Christmas preorders in December.
- She is a source of what regulars say about lunch; her comments were one input to the [[menu-review-2026-04-09]].
