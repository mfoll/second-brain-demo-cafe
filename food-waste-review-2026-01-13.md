# Food Waste Review 2026-01-13

Year review of the [[end-of-day-waste-log]] after closing on Tuesday 13 January 2026, with Kofi and Lena.

## Results

- December 2025: 3.5% of bakery production by value, about 255 EUR a week at shelf price. March 2025 was 9.0%.
- Bread waste down by about two thirds; viennoiserie down by about half.
- Larder donations in 2025: about 2,150 kg over 8.5 months, 9 kg a trading day on average ([[larder-donation-record-2025]]).
- Yesterday's bags: about 45 a week, nearly all sold by 11:00 ([[yesterdays-bag-sales-summary]]).

## What did it

In order of effect, by our own estimate: dropping the weekday 13:00 bake and sizing the 11:30 bake from Square sales ([[late-bake-sizing]]); yesterday's bags for pastries; the smaller hired oven from August, which forced smaller batches; and simply looking at the sheet every day.

## Decisions

- Target for 2026: stay under 4% every month. No new target below that; Kofi says chasing 2% means empty shelves at noon.
- Keep the log daily but review quarterly instead of monthly.
- Lena to watch the 11:30 bake closely after any menu change. (Three weeks later the lunch cut proved the point: February rose to 4.4%.)
