# Allergen Counter Answers

What front-of-house staff say when asked about allergens. Practised at the [[allergen-training-2025-11-25]] and printed inside the cover of the counter folder.

- **"Does this contain nuts?"** Read the folder or the label, say what it contains, then: "We use nuts in the same bakery, so we can't promise anything is free from them."
- **"Is this gluten free?"** "No, we don't make anything gluten free; everything is baked in a room full of flour."
- **"Can you check with the kitchen?"** Yes, always. Check the [[allergen-matrix]] printout in the folder, then ask Priyanka, Kofi or Odile if anything is unclear. Never guess.
- **"What's in the soup today?"** The weekly soup sheet is in the front of the folder, updated on Mondays by Mateus.
- **A customer says they have a severe allergy.** Show the label or the folder page, offer a sealed prepacked item over an unwrapped one, and do not hurry them.
- **You are not sure.** Say so. "I don't know, let me check" is the right answer.

If a customer reports a reaction or a labelling error, tell Odile the same day; record the product and the fix, not the customer.
