# Price Board Wording 2026

The card by the till from 2026-03-10 to the end of April, and the line front of house used when asked. Drafted by me, cut down by Lena.

## The card

"Our prices have changed today, for the first time in a year. Butter, chocolate and eggs have all gone up a lot since last spring, and we would rather pay a fair price to the people who make our ingredients than use cheaper ones. Thank you for still choosing us. Odile and the team."

## The spoken line

"It's the butter, mostly. We changed everything once rather than bit by bit."

## What we left out

- Percentages. Lena thought any number would be the only thing people read.
- Names of suppliers. Fine on the bread board, odd on a price notice.
- An apology. Grace's view: "we're not sorry, we're honest."

## How it went

Two comments in the first week ([[new-prices-launch-2026-03-09]]). Reuse the format for January 2027.
