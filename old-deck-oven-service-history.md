# Old Deck Oven Service History

The service and repair record of the second-hand two-deck oven we bought in 2019, compiled from Colm Ashworth's job sheets when the oven failed.

## Key Details

| Date | Work | Cost (EUR) |
|---|---|---|
| 2019-03 | Installed second-hand, full service | 1,100 |
| 2021-10 | Bottom deck element | 640 |
| 2023-02 | Door seals both decks | 290 |
| 2024-06 | Steam generator descaled and valve replaced | 520 |
| 2025-01-09 | Top deck controller and two elements | 3,400 |
| 2025-05-20 | Bottom deck element | 710 |
| 2025-07-29 | Bottom deck controller burned out; no parts | Call-out 180 |

Total spent on the oven since 2019: 6,840 EUR, of which 4,290 EUR in 2025 alone.

## Why It Matters

The January repair coincided with the VAT payment and emptied the overdraft ([[cash-flow-forecast]]). Read together, the 2025 lines show the oven was finished before it stopped; I should have started replacing it in May ([[lessons-from-the-oven-failure]]).
