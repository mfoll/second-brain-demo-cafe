# Why Did We Pause Wholesale To Cafés?

## Why It Matters

People ask: Nadia and Ewan, the staff, Ruth at year end, and now anyone looking at the [[central-production-kitchen]] idea. Wholesale was our first attempt at growth without a second shop. If we restart it, or bake for a second site, the same constraints will be back, so the reasons need to be written down once and correctly.

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> We paused wholesale on 20 October 2025 because it was making too little and costing too much of the one thing we were short of. It ran at 33% gross margin against a 35% target, it took about 15 baker hours a week, and after the old deck oven failed on 29 July 2025 we were baking on a hired oven that could not do the shop and wholesale together without the early shift starting earlier and running longer.

The decision was mine, taken on 6 October 2025 after a conversation with [[kofi-asare]] and [[lena-marsh]], with two weeks' notice to both customers ([[wholesale-pause-letter-2025-10-06]]).

## Evidence

Margin ([[wholesale-margin-workbook-2025]]):

- About 60 loaves a day, Tuesday to Saturday: roughly 300 loaves and 780 EUR of sales a week.
- Prices were set at about 63% of shop prices. Gross margin averaged 33% from June to September 2025, below the 35% target for wholesale in [[gross-profit-margin]]. Flour moved to Tarnwick Mill on 1 July 2025, which improved the bread but added about 4 cents a loaf.
- Per oven hour, wholesale bread contributed about a third of what the shop's viennoiserie did ([[contribution-margin]], from the [[food-business-finance-course]]).

Oven and hours:

- The old three-deck oven failed on 29 July 2025. From early August we baked on a hired oven with less deck space until the new oven was commissioned on 14 November ([[deck-oven-replacement]]).
- With the hired oven, the wholesale bake had to go in first, at 04:30, pushing the shop's first croissant bake back and leaving the counter thin until about 08:15 on several days in September.
- Wholesale took about 15 baker hours a week, mostly Kofi and [[joel-whitcombe]]. The new Deputy rota made the overtime visible: Kofi averaged 47 hours a week in summer 2025.

What we gave up: about 260 EUR a week of gross profit, and goodwill with two good customers. Both took it well ([[nadia-volkova]], [[ewan-macrae]]).

## What Did Not Change The Answer

- The new oven (14 November 2025) restored capacity but not baker hours, so we did not restart.
- The assistant baker hire ([[hana-kobayashi]], from 24 August 2026) was meant to bring the early shift under 42 hours a week, not to free hours for wholesale.

## What Would Reopen It

Resumption is tied to the [[central-production-kitchen]] idea under the [[second-site-decision]], and to wholesale prices that clear 35% at current costs. That is tracked in [[when-could-wholesale-resume]].
