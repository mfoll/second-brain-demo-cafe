# Central Production Shift Pattern Options

Three ways the Hollowgate basement could bake for unit 7, written after the [[central-production-talk-with-kofi-2026-09-24]] and a call with [[anselm-ferreira]]. For the bakers to argue with, not a proposal.

## A: Earlier Start

Early shift starts 03:00 instead of 04:30 and bakes both sites' morning output. No new staff, no new kit.
**Rejected.** Kofi will not do it and I agree it is not fair ([[baker-hours-limit-growth]]).

## B: Afternoon Prep Shift

New baker works 12:00-20:00 five days: mixes, shapes, laminates and retards for the next morning. The early shift (still 04:30) bakes off and finishes. A second retarder (about 6,500 EUR) and more basement power.
- Extra cost: one baker at about 38 hours plus the retarder, roughly 3,300 EUR a month.
- Anselm ran his hotel pastry like this, with a written handover sheet signed by each team.
- Kofi said this could work if it is a new person.

## C: Frozen Viennoiserie

Hollowgate makes and freezes shaped viennoiserie; unit 7 proves and bakes in its own convection oven. Only bread travels.
- Needs a blast freezer and freezer space we do not have.
- Quality drop that [[priyanka-desai]] would notice; customers maybe not.
