# Paper Stamp Card Rules

The rules of the old paper coffee card, in use from 2019 until 31 July 2025. Captured on 6 May 2025 at the start of the [[loyalty-card-launch]]. Superseded by [[loyalty-scheme-rules-2025]]; kept because a few customers still asked about old cards until the end of 2025.

## Summary

- One stamp per hot drink; ten stamps for a free hot drink.
- Cards printed by Fold & Carry, 2,000 at a time, about 95 EUR a year.
- Stamped with a rubber stamp of a croissant kept under the till.

## Why It Matters

It explains the transition rules: stamps on old cards were honoured until 31 December 2025, and Amira converted up to 8 stamps to stars on request ([[loyalty-launch-day-2025-06-10]]). The rubber stamp was easy to copy; in spring 2025 Lena found three cards with a stamp that was not ours.

## Caveats

Nobody ever counted redemptions properly; the 4% estimate for 2024 came from counting returned cards in two sample weeks.

## Source Location

Last printed card and the rubber stamp in the office drawer, box "Old loyalty".
