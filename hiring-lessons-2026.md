# Hiring Lessons 2026

What I would keep and change from the assistant baker hire, written on 2026-08-30.

Keep:

- Saying the real start time in the advert ([[assistant-baker-job-advert-2026]]).
- Kofi on the panel and a paid trial shift from 04:30. The trial decided it ([[assistant-baker-interviews-2026-07-14]]).
- Starting induction with the hand-wash sink and the daily sheet, before the bread.

Change:

- Start sooner. The case was clear in September 2025 and I hired in August 2026, mostly from cash nerves after the oven.
- Budget for four to six months of training. Hiring someone keen rather than experienced was right at our pay, but it means the hours fall slowly ([[can-we-get-the-head-baker-under-42-hours]]).
- Ask Brackenmouth College for a placement student next time; two of the five interviewed came via the college.

Numbers: 23 applications, 5 interviews, 2 trials, 1 hire, 11 weeks from advert to first shift.
