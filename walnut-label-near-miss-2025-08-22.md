# Walnut Label Near Miss 2025-08-22

A Friday afternoon allergen near miss. No one ate the product and no one was harmed.

## What happened

- Priyanka had added chopped walnuts to the banana loaf from that week. The recipe card was updated; the handwritten counter label for the sliced, wrapped loaf was not.
- At 14:20 [[finn-calloway]] was restocking, noticed walnut pieces in a cut slice and checked the label. Walnuts were missing.
- He took all eleven wrapped slices off the counter and told Lena. They were relabelled by 14:50. Nothing had been sold from that batch since the new recipe, as far as the till showed.

## Fix

- That afternoon: every handwritten label on the counter checked against the recipe cards. One more error found (oats missing on a flapjack label, not an allergen group but still wrong).
- The following week: decision to make one matrix the only source of ingredient lists and to print labels from it ([[allergen-labelling-overhaul]], [[allergen-matrix]]).
- Finn was thanked at the September team meeting. The point I made there: report first, fix second, no blame.
