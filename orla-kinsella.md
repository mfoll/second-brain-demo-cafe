# Orla Kinsella

Reporter at the Brackenmouth Courier, the weekly local paper. Covers business and community stories on the high street.

## How we work together

- She interviewed me and [[bernard-osei]] in November 2025 for a piece on businesses giving surplus food to the Community Larder ([[courier-article-larder-donations-2025-11]]). The article brought two more bakeries into the Larder scheme and about a week of extra Saturday trade for us.
- She calls when something on Hollowgate changes; she asked about the lunch cut in February 2026 and I declined to comment until the menu review. In April she ran a short paragraph on the soup coming back.
- I give her facts and figures for the community side (waste, donations, the bake club with the school's agreement). I do not discuss staff, the second site or the hygiene rating with the press.

## Current threads

- She wants a Christmas piece on preorders in early December. Fine if it runs after 1 December so it does not push orders past what we can bake ([[how-many-christmas-preorders-can-the-basement-bake]]).
