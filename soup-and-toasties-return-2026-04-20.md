# Soup And Toasties Return 2026-04-20

First day of the restored lunch after the [[menu-review-2026-04-09]]. Strictly the first trading day was Tuesday 2026-04-21; the board, the Square items and Mateus's prep plan went live on the Monday.

## The day

- Mateus made leek and potato soup, 12 litres, and prepped 30 toasties of each kind by 11:20. Lena stood with him at the press for the first hour.
- Sold 34 soups, 22 cheese and leek and 17 ham and mustard toasties. Soup sold out at 13:50.
- Several regulars said "the soup's back" before they reached the till. June Adeyemo put a photo of it on her blog that evening.

## What we changed in week one

- Batch up to 15 litres on Tuesdays and Saturdays.
- Toasties pressed to order only; pre-pressed ones went soggy in the warmer.
- Soup cooling recorded in the cooling log from day one, following [[daily-food-safety-checks]].

## After four weeks

Takings 11:30 to 14:30 within 6% of the pre-cut level by 2026-05-22. Project closed: [[savoury-lunch-trim]].
