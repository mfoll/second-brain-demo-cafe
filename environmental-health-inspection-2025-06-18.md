# Environmental Health Inspection 2025-06-18

Unannounced routine inspection by [[imogen-hale]] of the Brackenmouth Borough Council environmental health team, 09:40 to 12:15. We were rated 4 out of 5 on the Borough Food Hygiene Rating, down from the 5 we had held since the 2022 visit.

## At a Glance

> [!warning]
> We lost the point on records and the premises, not on the food: cooling of the new cooked fillings was not recorded, and the basement hand-wash sink was blocked by flour sacks. Both were fixed within a week, the plan went to version 3.1 on 2025-07-11, and the re-rating visit on 2025-10-07 gave us 5 again.

## What happened

Imogen arrived during the mid-morning rush. Lena called me up from the basement; Kofi had already left. She looked at the kitchen and basement first, then the paperwork at the office table, then spoke to Mateus and Priyanka about cleaning and allergens.

Good findings, from her verbal feedback and the letter ([[inspection-report-letter-2025-06]]):

- Fridges and display all within our limits; probe calibrated in May.
- The new plan (version 3, [[food-safety-management-plan]]) was clear and the critical control points were sensible.
- Allergen information was available and staff knew where it was.
- Pest control contract in place, last report clean ([[pest-control-service-reports]]).

Shortfalls:

1. **Cooling records.** Since March 2025 we had been cooking ham and leek and spinach and feta fillings in the morning for the savoury bakes, and chicken for the lunch. The plan named cooling as a critical control point, but the daily sheet had no place for it. Kofi and Joel were cooling in shallow trays and could describe the method, but there were no times or temperatures for the past eight weeks. In her words, roughly, a control you cannot show did not happen.
2. **Hand-wash sink.** The basement hand-wash sink was blocked by four 25 kg flour sacks delivered that morning and stacked against it because the dry store was full. Staff were washing hands in the pot sink.
3. Minor: the cleaning schedule had gaps on two Sundays in May.

She explained the score: food handling and the structure were good; the confidence-in-management part was marked down because of the records. A 4 is "good" on the scheme, but it sits on the window sticker for months.

## Action plan

Agreed with Imogen before she left and confirmed in my reply of 2025-06-20:

- [x] Add a cooling log to the daily sheet: time and core temperature at the end of cooking, at 2 hours and at 6 hours ([[two-stage-cooling]], [[cooling-log-sheet]]). Done 2025-06-19.
- [x] Clear the sink; new rule that nothing is stored within a metre of any hand-wash sink, with a yellow floor marking. Done the same afternoon ([[basement-storage-changes-2025]]).
- [x] New racking in the dry store to take a full flour delivery off the floor. Fitted 2025-06-27, 380 EUR.
- [x] Sunday cleaning sign-off moved to the closing checklist so it cannot be skipped ([[cleaning-schedule]]).
- [x] Plan revised to version 3.1 with the cooling step and the sink rule written in. Done 2025-07-11.
- [x] Request a re-rating visit after three months of clean records. Requested 2025-09-15; visit on 2025-10-07 ([[re-rating-visit-2025-10-07]]).

## What I took from it

The rewrite in spring ([[haccp-plan-rewrite]]) was right in substance and wrong in one practical place: I wrote cooling into the plan without putting it on the sheet people fill in at 05:00. The inspection was fair ([[a-4-was-a-fair-score]]). Since then every new product is walked through the sheet, not only the plan, before it goes on sale. That rule caught the reheating step for Mateus's soup in April 2026.
