# Second Site Menu Ideas For Unit 7

Rough menu thinking for a counter in unit 7, July 2026, after a chat with [[priyanka-desai]] and [[amira-saleh]] about what would sell in a food hall. Not shared beyond them.

- Shorter than Hollowgate: 4 breads, 6 viennoiserie, 4 cakes, plus whatever is seasonal from the [[seasonal-bake-calendar]].
- No lunch. The hall has four hot counters. A savoury bun or two at most (the lunch saga at Hollowgate is reason enough, see [[savoury-lunch-trim]]).
- Coffee: same Ombrel espresso as Hollowgate, but expect the hall's kiosk to take a share ([[saltings-hall-tenant-mix-plan]]). Amira suggests a short filter menu to be different.
- Afternoon trade: the hall is busiest 12-14 and 17-19. We close at 17. Cakes and a "bread to take home" offer from 15:00 could work.
- Priyanka wants one pastry that only exists at the hall. Cardamom knot was her first idea.
- Prices same as Hollowgate. Do not discount to "launch".
- Bread finished in the convection oven from par-baked? Kofi would hate it. Ask him, do not assume ([[central-production-kitchen]]).
