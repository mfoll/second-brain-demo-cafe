# Why Did We Run Out Of Cash In January 2025?

Why did a profitable bakery spend 19 days on its full overdraft in January 2025?

## Current Answer

> [!success]
> Timing, not losses. Three known payments landed in one month of low takings: the rent quarter (9,750 EUR, 1 January), an oven repair (3,400 EUR, 14 January) and the Q4 VAT payment (11,800 EUR, 31 January). The VAT had been collected in December and spent on stock and wages, and our monthly budget hid all three.

Contributing causes:

- January takings run about 31% below December.
- The [[monthly-budget-2024]] spread rent evenly and netted VAT out of sales.
- The books were three weeks behind in the old spreadsheet ([[bookkeeping-spreadsheet-2019-2025]]).
- No cash buffer rule.

## What We Changed

- The [[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]], updated every Monday.
- A separate VAT pot, topped up weekly ([[should-we-set-aside-vat-weekly]]).
- A 15,000 EUR weekly floor ([[cash-buffer]]).
- 30% deposits on Christmas preorders, so December cash covers January ([[christmas-2025-deposits-reconciliation]]).
- Bookkeeping moved to Xero ([[bookkeeping-move-to-xero]]).

## Result

January 2026: low point 18,200 EUR, overdraft unused.
