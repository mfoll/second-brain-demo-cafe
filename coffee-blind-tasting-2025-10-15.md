# Coffee Blind Tasting 2025-10-15

Blind tasting of three house espressos after close on a Wednesday, set up by Amira Saleh. Eight tasters, including June Adeyemo as a regular customer.

## At a Glance

> [!success]
> Ombrel Roasters' Estuary blend (Y) won six of eight votes as espresso and all eight in a flat white. We chose Ombrel the next week.

## Set-up

- Three coffees, labelled X, Y and Z by Amira. Our old blend was not included; we already knew we were leaving it.
- Each dialled in that morning by Amira on our machine and grinder: 18 g in, yield to taste for each.
- Tasted as espresso, then as an 8 oz flat white with Glenvarrow whole milk. Water between rounds; ranked first to third.
- Scores in [[coffee-blind-tasting-scores-2025]].

## What people said

- Y: chocolate, sweet in milk, "the one I'd drink every day" (Lena).
- Z: fine in milk, thin and sour as espresso. Cheapest at 21.00 EUR/kg.
- X: fruity, divisive; Finn loved it, Kofi called it "tea".

## Afterwards

Amira wanted a second round with X given more time on the grinder. I said no: we sell mostly milk drinks ([[coffee-sales-by-drink-2025]]) and Y won those outright. Dev Sandhu's offer arrived on 2025-10-20 ([[ombrel-roasters-wholesale-offer-2025]]).
