# Allergen Training 2025-11-25

An hour of all-staff allergen training at 16:15 after close, led by Priyanka and me, to launch the printed labels and the matrix.

## Notes

- What the fourteen allergen groups are and where they turn up in our products (glazes, seeded mixes, pesto in the toasties).
- How to print a label ([[print-allergen-labels]]) and why nobody types an ingredient list.
- What to say at the counter, rehearsed in pairs ([[allergen-counter-answers]]). The hardest one: a customer asking if something is "nut free". We say what it contains and that nuts are used in the same room.
- Quiz at the end; everyone passed, signed and dated in the [[staff-training-matrix]].
