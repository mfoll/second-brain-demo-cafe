# Lunch Takings Six Week Review 2026-04

The comparison I pulled from the Square exports for the menu review: weekdays only, the six weeks before 2026-02-23 against the six weeks after.

## Summary

Between 11:30 and 14:30, average weekday takings fell from 815 to 636 EUR (-22%), transactions from 92 to 79 (-14%), coffee drinks from 61 to 48 and cake slices from 23 to 17. Takings outside that window were up 1%. Weekends barely moved, because weekend lunch was always mostly bakery.

## Why It Matters

The number that reversed the lunch cut at the [[menu-review-2026-04-09]]. It showed the cut was not just losing lunch sales but the drinks and cakes that came with them.

## Key Details

- Before: 2026-01-06 to 2026-02-21. After: 2026-02-24 to 2026-04-04. Same number of trading weekdays (30).
- The 2026-03-09 price rise falls inside the "after" window and would have lifted takings by about 6%, so the volume loss was larger than 22%.
- Daily loss about 179 EUR in takings; estimated 110 EUR in gross margin.

## Caveats

Six weeks in late winter, with Easter on the edge of the window. Aggregates only, no customer-level data, as for all Square exports ([[square-pos-sales-data]]).
