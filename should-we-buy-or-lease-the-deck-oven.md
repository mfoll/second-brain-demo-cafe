# Should We Buy Or Lease The Deck Oven

Should the new 41,500 EUR oven be bought from cash, bought with a bank loan, or leased?

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> Leased over five years at 812 EUR a month, signed 2025-09-26. Buying from cash would have taken the buffer to near zero before the January VAT quarter; a Pellow Mutual loan was cheaper on paper but needed a personal guarantee and three weeks to approve.

## Options compared (September 2025)

| Option | Cost over 5 years (EUR) | Cash now | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cash | 41,500 | 41,500 | We held about 23,000 |
| Bank loan, 5 years | about 47,600 | 0 | Personal guarantee asked |
| Lease (chosen) | 48,720 | 0 | Nominal 100 EUR purchase at the end |

## Who said what

[[ruth-marchbank]] preferred the loan for cost and suggested negotiating away the guarantee; I chose the lease for speed and because it left the Pellow Mutual relationship free for later borrowing ([[second-site-financing]]). The difference over five years is about 1,100 EUR.
