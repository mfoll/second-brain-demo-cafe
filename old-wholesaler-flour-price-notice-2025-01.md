# Old Wholesaler Flour Price Notice 2025-01

The flour wholesaler's notice of new prices from 2025-02-03: the third rise in thirteen months and the trigger for the [[flour-supplier-review]].

## Summary

Strong white roller-milled flour from 0.72 to 0.78 EUR/kg delivered; pastry flour from 0.80 to 0.86; wholemeal from 0.70 to 0.75. No change to delivery days.

## Why It Matters

Three rises in a year with no change in quality, plus Kofi's complaints about protein from batch to batch, made it worth looking for a mill that would at least give us something better for the money. The price gap to Tarnwick narrowed from 33% to 23% with this notice.

## Key Details

- Pastry flour still bought from them at 0.86, raised to 0.90 in January 2026.
- No batch analysis provided with deliveries.

## Caveats

Their prices are list prices; we never asked for a volume discount, which in hindsight we should have before switching.
