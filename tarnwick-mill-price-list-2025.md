# Tarnwick Mill Price List 2025

The trade price list Beatrix Coull gave me at the mill visit, with delivered prices for 25 kg sacks.

## Summary

Stone-ground strong white 0.96 EUR/kg delivered; wholemeal 0.92; light rye 1.10; spelt 1.65; a fine "pastry" grade 1.05 (not stone-ground, bought in by Tarnwick). Free delivery on orders over 500 kg; two deliveries a week.

## Why It Matters

The cost side of the [[flour-supplier-review]]. Against the old wholesaler's 0.78 for strong white, Tarnwick is 23% dearer: about 340 EUR a month on our 1,900 kg. The list made it clear the pastry grade was a resale, not their own milling, which settled that pastry flour would stay put.

## Key Details

- Protein for strong white quoted at 12.5 to 13%; moisture 14% or under.
- Prices fixed for 12 months from the start of an agreement, then reviewed.
- Pallet discount (40 sacks) of 3%, which we do not reach.

## Caveats

These are 2025 prices; the renewal in June 2026 added 4% ([[tarnwick-mill-supply-agreement-2025]]).
