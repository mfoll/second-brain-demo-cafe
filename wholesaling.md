# Wholesaling

English Wikipedia article on wholesaling or distribution.

## Summary

Wholesaling is selling goods to retailers and other business users rather than to the end consumer, typically in bulk. The wholesaler buys in bulk at a discount and the retailer sells on at a higher price to make a profit.

## Why It Matters

Read the day before I set the [[wholesale-price-list-2025]]. The basic logic, that the buyer needs room to sell on at a profit, is why I set wholesale bread at about 63% of our shop price. What the article's model does not cover is a maker who is also the retailer: every wholesale loaf used oven time and baker hours the shop needed. That gap is what [[wholesale-pricing-floor]] fixes and why we paused ([[why-did-we-pause-wholesale-to-cafes]]).

## Caveats

About distribution businesses in general, not bakeries supplying neighbours.
