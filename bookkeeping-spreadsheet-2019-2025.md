# Bookkeeping Spreadsheet 2019-2025

The spreadsheet I kept the books in from opening in 2019 until the move to Xero. Archived read-only on 2025-07-31; replaced by Xero ([[bookkeeping-move-to-xero]]).

## Summary

One workbook per year, one tab per month, bank lines typed in by hand from statements and coded with a drop-down. VAT returns were built from a summary tab.

## Why It Matters

Historic only. It holds the 2019-2024 figures used to seed the seasonal pattern in the [[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]] and the opening balances at 2025-03-31. Do not edit it; corrections go in Xero.

## Key Details

- About five hours a week to keep up; three weeks behind in January 2025.
- Known quirks: card fees netted off takings until 2023; wholesale was never a separate line before 2025.

Finance/Archive/Bookkeeping in the cloud drive, set to view-only.
