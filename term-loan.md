# Term loan

## Summary

Explains a term loan as money repaid in scheduled instalments over a fixed period, typically one to ten years, with interest included in the repayments at either a fixed rate or a variable rate linked to a benchmark.

## Why It Matters

Read the day after the [[pellow-mutual-second-site-loan-offer]] arrived, to check the vocabulary before calling [[devon-pryce]]. Our offer is a five-year fixed-rate term loan, which the article describes as the ordinary case.

## Caveats

No information on what rate is fair for a small business; for that I relied on [[ruth-marchbank]].
