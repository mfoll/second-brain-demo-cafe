# Bookkeeping

## Summary

Describes bookkeeping as the regular recording of a business's financial transactions, daily or at other intervals, as the basis for reliable financial statements.

## Why It Matters

Read on the first day of the [[bookkeeping-move-to-xero]]. The emphasis on recording transactions as they happen named what had gone wrong: the [[bookkeeping-spreadsheet-2019-2025]] was three weeks behind in January 2025.

## Caveats

Generic; nothing on software, bank feeds or point-of-sale imports.
