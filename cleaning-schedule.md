# Cleaning Schedule

The cleaning schedule in use since plan version 3.1, split into daily, weekly and monthly tasks with a named area owner.

## Key Details

- Daily: benches, mixers, sheeter, sinks, floors, display fridge, coffee machine, toilets. Signed on the closing sheet.
- Weekly: walk-in chiller shelves (Monday by Mateus), oven decks brushed and steam drains checked (Friday by the early shift), extractor filters (Saturday).
- Monthly: behind and under equipment, dry store emptied and wiped, high shelves.
- Products: one food-safe sanitiser at the stated dilution and contact time; dilution chart by each sink.

## Why It Matters

Two missing Sunday sign-offs in May 2025 were noted at the [[environmental-health-inspection-2025-06-18]]. Moving the sign-off onto the closing sheet stopped it being skipped.

## Source Location

Laminated on the basement wall and behind the counter; master in `Food safety / Cleaning`.
