# Butter

## Summary

General article on butter: made by churning cream, roughly 81% butterfat, the rest mostly water and milk solids. It covers production, cultured and sweet cream butters, and the uses of butter in cooking and baking.

## Why It Matters

Read at the start of the [[dairy-contract-renegotiation]] to check what we should compare between Glenvarrow and a second dairy beyond price. The fat and water content explain why one block laminates better than another and why "cheaper butter" can cost more in failed batches. It went into the questions I sent Nils before [[second-dairy-tasting-2026-10-14]].

## Key Details

- About 81% butterfat; the rest is mostly water and milk solids.
- Semi-solid at room temperature, which is the property lamination depends on; our working temperatures come from the dairies, not from here.

## Caveats

Nothing on prices or markets. For our own prices see [[butter-price-tracker]].
