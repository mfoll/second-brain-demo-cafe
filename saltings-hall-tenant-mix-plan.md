# Saltings Hall Tenant Mix Plan

The hall's plan of which kind of trader goes in each of its 14 units, shared by [[priscilla-adomako]] on 2026-04-02.

## Summary

Fourteen units: four hot-food counters (noodles, grill, pizza, a vegetarian kitchen), two bars (beer and wine), a fishmonger, a greengrocer, a deli, an ice-cream counter, a coffee kiosk run by the hall, and three units including unit 7 still being let. Unit 7 is earmarked for "bakery and café".

## Why It Matters

Competition for our coffee: the hall's own coffee kiosk sits 25 m from unit 7. I assumed coffee at 30% of our takings in the [[saltings-hall-three-scenario-model]], against 38% at Hollowgate, for that reason. No other bakery is planned, which is the main attraction.

## Key Details

- Hall opening hours 08:00-17:00 for daytime traders; bars open to 23:00.
- Common seating about 160 covers, no table service.
- Exclusivity: the hall will not let a second bakery while we trade, written into the heads of terms.

## Caveats

The mix may change before opening; three units are unlet.
