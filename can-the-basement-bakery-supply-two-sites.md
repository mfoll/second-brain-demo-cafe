# Can The Basement Bakery Supply Two Sites?

Can the Hollowgate basement bake for Hollowgate and unit 7 at Saltings Market Hall, on a shift pattern the bakers accept and at a cost the unit can carry?

## Why It Matters

It is the second condition on [[should-we-open-a-second-site]]. It also decides whether [[wholesale-to-cafes]] can ever restart ([[why-did-we-pause-wholesale-to-cafes]]).

## Current Answer

Oven: yes. The three-deck oven is idle after 11:00 ([[central-production-capacity-study]]).

People: not on the current pattern. Unit 7's morning output would need a 03:00 start, which [[kofi-asare]] has refused ([[kofi-email-on-central-production]]). An afternoon shift with a new baker plus a second retarder (about 6,500 EUR) looks feasible on paper ([[central-production-shift-pattern-options]]).

Cold storage and the lease: a second retarder needs more power in the basement and [[cyril-ashdown]]'s consent ([[hollowgate-lease-summary]]).

## What Would Answer It

- The bakers' own proposal, due 2026-11-16.
- A costing of the afternoon shift: one baker, about 38 hours a week, plus the retarder.
- Possibly a two-week trial in January 2027.

## Owner

Odile with Kofi. Needed by 2026-12-15.
