# Flour Bake Trial Scores 2025

The score sheet from the blind bread trial of 2025-05-14: three flours, the same recipe adjusted for water, six tasters.

## Summary

Loaves A (old wholesaler), B (Tarnwick strong white) and C (a second regional mill we did not pursue) were scored 1 to 5 on crust, crumb, taste and keeping (tasted again on day 2). B won on every line with every taster. Totals out of 120: A 71, B 104, C 83.

## Why It Matters

The decision evidence for the [[flour-supplier-review]]. It also recorded the water: B took 76% hydration against 72% for A to reach the same dough feel, which became the new house recipe ([[house-sourdough-recipe-card]], [[dough-hydration]]).

## Key Details

| Flour | Hydration | Crust | Crumb | Taste | Day 2 | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 72% | 19 | 18 | 17 | 17 | 71 |
| B | 76% | 26 | 25 | 28 | 25 | 104 |
| C | 75% | 21 | 22 | 21 | 19 | 83 |

Tasters: Odile, Kofi, Joel, Priyanka, Lena, Amira.

## Caveats

Six people who all work here and one bake each (plus a repeat of B and A a week later that matched). Not a panel, but enough for a bread we sell to our own customers.
