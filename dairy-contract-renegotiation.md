# Dairy Contract Renegotiation

Getting a fixed butter price for the busy half of the year, from Glenvarrow Dairy or a second dairy, before the Christmas bakes start eating 200 kg a month.

## Outcome

A fixed-price butter contract for November 2026 to April 2027 signed with Glenvarrow Dairy or a second dairy.

## Current State

- Butter is 8.90 EUR/kg in September 2026, up 44% from 6.20 in January 2025 ([[butter-price-tracker]]).
- On 2026-10-06 [[moira-fenwick]] offered a fixed 8.40 EUR/kg for November 2026 to April 2027 against a 900 kg commitment ([[glenvarrow-fixed-price-offer-2026-10]]). She wants an answer by 2026-11-06.
- [[nils-jorgensen]] brought Coldbeck Creamery samples on 2026-10-14 ([[second-dairy-tasting-2026-10-14]]); quote promised by 2026-10-30.
- Milk and cream stay with Glenvarrow whatever happens to butter.

At 8.40 against today's 8.90 the fixed price saves about 450 EUR over six months if the market stays flat, and costs us if butter falls. The 900 kg is close to our real use, so the risk is in the price, not the volume.

## Scope and Boundaries

Butter only, both the block butter for lamination and the dough butter. Milk, cream and the café's dairy order are outside this project. Recosting the menu after the decision belongs to [[menu-refresh-2026]].

## Working Context

We used about 1,980 kg of butter in the twelve months to September 2026, 150 to 210 kg a month, heaviest in November and December. Butter is the single biggest line in the croissant ([[croissant-cost-breakdown]]): about 45 g a piece, 0.40 EUR at today's price.

Options:

1. Sign Glenvarrow's 8.40 for six months. Simple, keeps the relationship, and Moira has never missed a delivery.
2. Take Coldbeck if its quote is clearly lower and the butter laminates as well. Priyanka's first test was good but one test is not a season ([[priyanka-email-butter-sheet-trial-2026-10]]).
3. Stay on the monthly price and accept the swings. Ruth's view was that a small business should not speculate on butter in either direction.

What would settle it: Coldbeck's price per kg on a similar commitment, a second lamination test, and whether Coldbeck can deliver twice a week. See [[should-we-sign-the-fixed-price-butter-contract]] and the general idea in [[forward-buying]].
