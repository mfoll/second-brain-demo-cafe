# Situational Leadership Theory

English Wikipedia article on the situational leadership model.

## Summary

The model holds that good leaders adapt their style to the situation, that no single style fits every case, and that a leader may use different styles with the same team or person at different times.

## Why It Matters

[[margot-elling]] mentioned it in the March 2026 session, after the lunch cut, when I complained that some of the team wanted more say and others wanted clearer instructions. It gave me a way to think about the people on the early shift: [[kofi-asare]] needs almost no direction and wants to be consulted; a new assistant baker would need close instruction for weeks. I used that framing to plan Hana's induction with Kofi before the hire ([[assistant-baker-hiring]]).

## Caveats

A management model, not evidence. Useful as words for something I half knew.
