# Tarnwick Mill Visit 2025-03-19

Kofi and I drove out to Tarnwick Mill on a Wednesday afternoon; Beatrix Coull showed us round for two hours.

## What we saw

Two pairs of stones running, a small roller line for the white flours they sift, and a storeroom that was cleaner than our basement. They mill about 40 tonnes a week, mostly for bakeries and farm shops within an hour's drive. Wheat comes from four farms they name on the sacks.

## What we learned

- Each batch is tested for protein, moisture and falling number; Beatrix showed us the batch sheets they would send with each delivery.
- Stone-ground strong white keeps more of the germ and fine bran, so it takes more water and ferments faster. Kofi asked twice whether that was "a feature or a problem"; Beatrix said both, depending on the baker.
- They hold stock for regular customers. She offered two weeks of our use as a buffer before I asked.

## What we decided

Take trial sacks of strong white and wholemeal for a side-by-side bake in May ([[flour-bake-trial-2025-05-14]]). Price list in hand ([[tarnwick-mill-price-list-2025]]): 0.96 EUR/kg delivered for strong white.

Kofi on the drive back: "If the bread's better, the 18 cents is nothing." He has said the same about everything that makes the bread better, so I wrote it down.
