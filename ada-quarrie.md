# Ada Quarrie

Runs Quarrymoor Farm, which supplies all our free-range eggs, about 1,080 a week.

## Role

Delivers herself on Tuesday and Saturday mornings before 07:00. Eggs come graded, in trays of 30, with the laying date on each tray.

## How we work together

- Fixed weekly standing order; changes by phone by Friday for the following week.
- In late winter laying drops and she warns us a fortnight ahead. We buy liquid egg for the egg wash only during those weeks.
- Price went from 0.24 to 0.29 EUR an egg in January 2026, with a month's notice and an honest explanation about feed ([[quarrymoor-farm-egg-supply-terms]]).
