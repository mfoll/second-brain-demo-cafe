# Bake Club Pilot Review 2026-06-03

Last session of the pilot year and a half-hour review with [[samir-belkacem]] afterwards, Wednesday 3 June 2026.

## The year in numbers

- Eight sessions, October 2025 to June 2026 (no session in December).
- Average attendance 11 children of 12 places.
- Ingredient cost about 300 EUR for the year; staff about 40 hours.
- Products: focaccia, soda bread, pizza bianca, scones, flatbreads, shortbread, fruit buns, and a final "bake your own" session.

## What Samir reported

- The class teacher survey ranked it the best enrichment activity of the year.
- Two children who rarely spoke in class were the most confident in the food room.
- The school wants it to continue in 2026-27 with the new Year 4.

## What we agreed

- Same format next year, first Wednesday of the month from 7 October 2026.
- No yeasted dough made at school; we bring it.
- One session on grains in spring, using Tarnwick spelt ([[heritage-grains]]).
- Bread rolls for the Community Larder's Christmas lunch at the December session if the date works.
