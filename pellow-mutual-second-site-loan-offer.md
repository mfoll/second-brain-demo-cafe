# Pellow Mutual Second Site Loan Offer

The formal offer letter from Pellow Mutual for a term loan to fit out and open unit 7 at Saltings Market Hall, received from [[devon-pryce]] on 2026-09-08.

## Summary

85,000 EUR over five years at 7.9% fixed, repayable monthly, offer valid until 2026-12-08. Secured by a debenture over the company's assets. No personal guarantee.

## Why It Matters

It is the main financing option in [[second-site-financing]], and it covers 85,000 of the 120,000 EUR needed ([[saltings-unit-7-fit-out-budget]]). Its expiry a week before the 2026-12-15 decision date is a problem ([[should-we-take-the-pellow-mutual-loan]]).

## Key Details

| Term | Value |
|---|---|
| Amount | 85,000 EUR |
| Term | 60 months, first repayment one month after drawdown |
| Rate | 7.9% fixed |
| Monthly repayment | about 1,720 EUR (my calculation; the letter quotes 1,719.43 EUR) |
| Total interest | about 18,200 EUR |
| Arrangement fee | 850 EUR |
| Early repayment | Allowed after 12 months, 2% fee on the amount repaid |
| Security | Debenture |
| Drawdown | In up to three tranches against fit-out invoices, by 2027-06-30 |
| Conditions | See [[pellow-mutual-loan-conditions-email]] |

## Caveats

The offer assumes the business plan sent in August, which used the base case. Devon said the credit team would want to see the low case before drawdown.
