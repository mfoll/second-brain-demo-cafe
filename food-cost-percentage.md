# Food Cost Percentage

The cost of the ingredients and packaging in a product divided by its selling price. The mirror of [[gross-profit-margin]]: a 29% food cost is a 71% gross margin.

## How I use it

- **In the costing sheet:** against the price ex VAT, so it lines up with Xero ([[recipe-costing-sheet]]).
- **At the counter and in the butter tracker:** against the shelf price, because that is the number I can do in my head. The two differ by a few points; I always say which one I mean.
- **Targets by section** (ex VAT): viennoiserie 27%, bread 25%, cakes 28%, coffee 20%, lunch 32%. Set in the [[price-review-2026]].

## Worked example

Croissant, October 2026: food cost 0.86 EUR, shelf price 2.95 EUR, so 29% of the shelf price; ex VAT the price is about 2.68 and the food cost 32% ([[croissant-cost-breakdown]]).

## Limits

Labour, energy and rent are not in it. A product can have a fine food cost and still lose money if it takes a cook's morning, which is what the [[lunch-margin-analysis-2026-01]] had to add by hand. A low food cost on a product nobody buys is worth nothing; see [[menu-engineering]].
