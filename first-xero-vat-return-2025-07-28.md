# First Xero VAT Return 2025-07-28

Filed the Q2 2025 VAT return from Xero for the first time, three days before the deadline. The last step of the [[bookkeeping-move-to-xero]].

## What Happened

- Liability 11,350 EUR for April to June 2025, paid from the VAT pot, which held 11,900 EUR.
- [[ruth-marchbank]]'s team reviewed the Xero return against my parallel spreadsheet version. They differed by 41 EUR, traced to a Fold & Carry invoice coded with the wrong tax rate in the spreadsheet.
- Filing took 20 minutes, against most of a Sunday the old way.

## Notes

This was the week before the old oven failed on 2025-07-29. If the oven had gone a week earlier, I would have been filing a return and hiring an oven on the same day, with the books half in two systems. Wrote the steps up as [[file-the-quarterly-vat-return]].
