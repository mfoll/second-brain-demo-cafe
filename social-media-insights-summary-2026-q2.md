# Social Media Insights Summary 2026 Q2

Quarterly summary of our social media account statistics for April to June 2026, compiled by [[grace-oyelaran]] on 6 July 2026. Aggregate figures only.

## Key Details

- Followers: 4,860 at 30 June (4,310 at 31 March).
- Posts: 49. Average reach per post about 1,900 accounts.
- Top three posts by reach: the basement at 05:00 with steam coming off the deck (6,400), the soup returning on 20 April (5,100), Talia's spring pastry photo (4,700).
- Lowest: two "offer" posts about yesterday's bags (under 600 each).
- Most followers within 15 km, per the platform's location report.

## Why It Matters

It led to the July revision of the [[social-media-posting-plan]]: more basement and process content, no offers. The soup post confirmed what the [[menu-review-2026-04-09]] had heard from regulars.

## Caveats

Reach figures are the platform's estimates and are not comparable across platforms or with earlier quarters before we switched to a business account in January.
