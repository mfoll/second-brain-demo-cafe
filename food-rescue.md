# Food Rescue

English Wikipedia article on food rescue, also called food recovery or surplus food redistribution.

## Summary

Defines food rescue as collecting edible food that would otherwise be wasted, from farms, markets, shops, restaurants and canteens, and passing it to local emergency food programmes.

## Why It Matters

It names what our Larder collection is: we are a small source of rescued food, and the Larder is the programme it goes to. Useful words when [[orla-kinsella]] interviewed me for the [[courier-article-larder-donations-2025-11]] and when explaining to staff why the closing routine matters. Read alongside [[community-fridge]] in June 2026.

## Caveats

Short article; nothing on labelling or food safety for donors, which is covered by our own [[community-larder-donation-agreement]].
