# Allergen Matrix

The single spreadsheet that lists every product we sell, its full ingredient list and which of the fourteen allergen groups it contains or may contain. Labels and the counter folder are printed from it and nothing else.

## Summary

64 products in October 2026, one row each, with ingredient lists built from sub-recipes (doughs, glazes, fillings) so a change in one sub-recipe updates every product that uses it. Each ingredient line links to a dated supplier specification ([[supplier-allergen-specifications]]). Columns for the fourteen allergen groups show "contains" or "may contain" with the reason.

## Why It Matters

It is the fix for the [[walnut-label-near-miss-2025-08-22]]. The rule: no product goes on sale, and no recipe changes, until its row is updated and checked by a second person (Priyanka or me). It also made the [[allergen-labelling-overhaul]] finishable.

## Key Details

- Most common allergens: cereals containing gluten (61 products), milk (52), eggs (44), nuts (17), sesame (9), soya (6).
- "May contain" is used only where a supplier declares it or where we cannot separate, per [[precautionary-allergen-labelling]].
- Last full check: 2026-10-14 for the autumn menu; soup recipes rotate weekly and are checked by Mateus and me each Monday.
