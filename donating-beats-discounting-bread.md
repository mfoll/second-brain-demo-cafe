# Donating Beats Discounting Bread

## Stance

Unsold bread should go to the Community Larder, not be sold off cheaply at the end of the day. Pastries are different and can go in yesterday's bags the next morning.

## Scope

Bread at Hollowgate, at current volumes (about 7 to 9 kg a day). Not a view on other bakeries, and not on pastries.

## Why I Think This

- An end-of-day bread discount teaches regulars to come at 15:30. We saw it on the Saturday trial on 26 April 2025: full-price sales after 14:00 fell.
- Day-old bread at half price sells badly; people buy bread to eat today. Pastries keep their appeal better for one morning.
- The Larder needs bread and the volume is small enough that it costs us nothing but a label and ten minutes.
- It keeps the pressure on baking less rather than selling cheaper. The [[end-of-day-waste-log]] counts donated bread as waste on purpose.

## What Would Change My Mind

If waste rose back above 6% for two months, donating would be covering an over-baking problem and we would need something sharper. Also if the Larder stopped being able to take it.

## Implications

Keep the [[late-bake-sizing]] rule, renew the Larder agreement in 2027, and never put a discount sign on bread.
