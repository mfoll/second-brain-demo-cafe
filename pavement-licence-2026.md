# Pavement Licence 2026

Our current pavement seating licence from the Brackenmouth Borough Council licensing team, valid 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31.

## Key Details

- Area: 1.6 m deep along the 7 m shop front on Hollowgate; 3 tables, 12 covers.
- Hours: furniture out from 07:30, in by 18:00.
- Conditions: 1.8 m clear walkway at all times; no fixed barriers; furniture stored inside overnight; no amplified music; litter swept at close; public liability cover of at least the council's stated minimum.
- Fee paid: 380 EUR.

## Why It Matters

The 2027 application keeps these terms and adds the side-street section ([[pavement-licence-application-2027]]). Breaking the 18:00 condition would put the renewal at risk, so the closing checklist includes bringing the furniture in.
