# Deck Oven Quotes 2025

Three quotes for a replacement deck oven, received between 2025-08-15 and 2025-09-10, with Kofi's notes from the demonstration bakes.

## Key Details

| | Option A | Option B (chosen) | Option C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decks | 3, electric | 3, electric | 2, electric, wider |
| Steam | Top two decks | Every deck | Every deck |
| Installed price (EUR) | 33,900 | 41,500 | 46,800 |
| Lead time | 14 weeks | 8 weeks | 6 weeks |
| Local service | None in Brackenmouth | Colm Ashworth certified | Supplier's own engineer, 90 km away |
| Kofi's test bake | Uneven bottom deck | Good on all decks | Good, less capacity |

## Why It Matters

Option B cost 7,600 EUR more than A, but steam on every deck lets us bake bread on three decks at once, and Colm can service it. Option A would have kept us on the hired oven into December. Option C had less capacity than the oven it replaced.

## Caveats

Prices exclude the three-phase supply (2,300 EUR) and VAT. Option C's price was a first offer; we did not negotiate it.
