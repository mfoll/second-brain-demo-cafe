# VAT Return Calendar

The VAT quarters and payment dates for Hollowgate Bakehouse Ltd, written on 2025-03-10 when the VAT pot was opened, and pinned above the office desk.

## Summary

Calendar quarters, return and payment due by the last day of the month after the quarter ends. I file three days early and pay from the VAT pot.

## Why It Matters

These four dates are the spine of the [[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]]. The January one is the dangerous one ([[overdraft-squeeze-january-2025]]).

## Key Details

| Quarter | Months | Due | Typical liability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Jan-Mar | 30 April | 8,000-9,000 EUR |
| Q2 | Apr-Jun | 31 July | 11,000-12,000 EUR |
| Q3 | Jul-Sep | 31 October | 12,000-13,000 EUR |
| Q4 | Oct-Dec | 31 January | 11,800-13,500 EUR |

- Weekly set-aside: 9% of gross takings into the VAT pot every Monday ([[should-we-set-aside-vat-weekly]]).
- Ruth's team reviews the Q2 and Q4 returns before filing.

## Caveats

Liabilities shift with the mix of standard-rated drinks and reduced-rate food. Typical ranges from 2024-2026.
