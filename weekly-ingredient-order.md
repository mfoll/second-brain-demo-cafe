# Weekly Ingredient Order

How to put together the week's dry goods, flour, coffee and chocolate orders on Monday evening, and the daily dairy and produce orders. Written so Lena or Kofi can do it when I am away.

## Inputs

- Last week's [[square-pos-sales-data]] item sales (top 20 items) and the bake plan for the week.
- Stock count sheet from Saturday close (walk-in, dry store, basement).
- [[supplier-order-calendar]] for cut-offs; [[supplier-contacts-list]] for numbers.
- Any specials on the [[seasonal-bake-calendar-sheet]] for the coming week.

## Steps

1. Count. On Saturday at close, fill the stock sheet: flour sacks, sugar, butter blocks, chocolate, nuts, coffee bags, eggs, packaging.
2. Work out need. For each line: next week's planned use minus stock, plus the safety stock written on the sheet (flour 3 days, butter 2 days, coffee 5 days).
3. Round up to the supplier's unit: 25 kg sacks, 5 kg butter blocks, 1 kg coffee bags, trays of 30 eggs.
4. Tarnwick: email Beatrix by Monday 18:00 with Tuesday and Friday quantities.
5. Ombrel: email Dev on Monday for Wednesday. Do not order more than two weeks of coffee.
6. Old wholesaler: portal by Tuesday 12:00 for Thursday.
7. Kestrin: portal by Thursday for Monday.
8. Dairy and produce are daily: dairy by 14:00 the day before, Brook & Daughters by text by 20:00.
9. File the order confirmations in the `Orders` folder and write the totals on the sheet.

## Checks

- Does the order total look like last week's, within about 15%? If not, check for a typo before sending.
- Any item on the [[supplier-price-rises-2026]] list this month? Flag it for [[recost-a-recipe]].

## If something goes wrong

- Short delivery: call the supplier before 08:00; note it on the invoice for a credit.
- Butter short: Lena may buy up to 10 kg at the cash-and-carry, keep the receipt.
- Flour short: Tarnwick holds two weeks of our stock; ring Beatrix.
