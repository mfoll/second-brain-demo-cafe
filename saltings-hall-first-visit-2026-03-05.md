# Saltings Hall First Visit 2026-03-05

First walk round Saltings Market Hall, with [[simone-ward]] and the hall manager [[priscilla-adomako]], in hard hats. The building was scaffolded inside.

## What I Saw

- The old fish market: one big hall with steel trusses, fourteen unit shells along three walls, seating in the middle. Estuary on one side, the new car park on the other.
- Unit 7: corner unit on the east side, by the main door from the car park. About 28 m2 plus a store. Morning sun.
- The hall's extraction runs behind every unit. A small oven in our unit would connect to it, no flue of our own.

## What Priscilla Said

- Opening "spring 2027". Units on offer at 1,300-1,600 EUR a month plus service charge.
- They want one bakery and will not let a second.
- Footfall projection to follow (arrived 2026-03-20: [[saltings-hall-footfall-projections]]).

## My Reaction

Unit 7 does what Tanner's Row could not: an oven without a 38,000 EUR flue. The risk moves from the building to the footfall. Started the [[saltings-hall-feasibility]] project three days earlier on paper; this made it real. Questions I did not ask on the day went into [[things-to-ask-priscilla-about-the-hall]].
