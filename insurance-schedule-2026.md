- Covers: buildings contents and stock, equipment breakdown (including the leased oven, noted as financed), business interruption up to 12 months, public and products liability, employers' liability.
- Breakdown cover excess: 250 EUR per claim. Stock spoilage cover up to 5,000 EUR after a refrigeration failure.
