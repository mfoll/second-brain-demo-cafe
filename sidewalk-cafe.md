# Sidewalk cafe

## Summary

Short Wikipedia article on the sidewalk or pavement café: the part of a café or bar served on or next to a public pavement. It covers how such seating is usually permitted by the local authority, the common conditions (clear walkway, furniture removal, hours) and the role outdoor seating plays in street life.

## Why It Matters

Read at the start of the [[pavement-seating-licence]] project in September 2026, mostly to check whether there was anything general about pavement seating I had missed beyond our own council's conditions. The useful reminder was that the conditions are about the public's use of the pavement first; that is why the plan leads with the 1.8 m walkway rather than the number of covers ([[pavement-seating-plan]]).

## Key Details

- Pavement seating is normally licensed by the local authority, often seasonally or yearly.
- Conditions usually protect pedestrian access and limit hours.

## Caveats

Very general, and it describes practice in real cities; our rules are Brackenmouth Borough Council's ([[pavement-licence-2026]]).
