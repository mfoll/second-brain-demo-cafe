# Price Review 2026

The once-a-year price review, done properly this time: every recipe recosted, every price checked against its target margin, one change on one day.

## Outcome

New prices from 2026-03-09 based on recosted recipes, including croissants from 2.60 to 2.95 EUR.

## Current State

- Completed 2026-03-06; prices live in Square from opening on Monday 2026-03-09 (we were closed, so Tuesday was the first day customers saw them).
- 41 of 63 items went up; average rise across items sold was 6.8%. Nothing went down.
- Croissant 2.60 to 2.95; pain au chocolat 2.90 to 3.25; sourdough loaf 4.80 to 5.20; flat white 3.30 to 3.50; slice of cake 4.20 to 4.50. Full list: [[price-list-2026-03-09]].
- Six weeks later, unit sales of croissants were down 3% on the same weeks of 2025 while croissant takings were up 10%.

## Scope and Boundaries

Counter, coffee and the lunch items that survived the trim. Wholesale prices were not reviewed because wholesale is paused ([[wholesale-to-cafes]]). Christmas prices are set separately each October.

## Working Context

The trigger was the January costing run in the [[recipe-costing-sheet]]: the croissant's food cost had gone from 24% of the selling price in January 2025 to 31%, mostly butter ([[butter-price-tracker]], [[croissant-cost-breakdown]]). Chocolate was up 20% from January ([[kestrin-chocolate-price-notice-2026-01]]) and eggs 21%.

Method: recost every recipe with February invoice prices ([[recost-a-recipe]]), set a target food cost per section (viennoiserie 27%, bread 25%, cakes 28%, coffee 20%), round to the nearest 0.05 EUR, then look at each price as a customer would. Lena pushed back on the flat white going to 3.60; we settled on 3.50. Kofi wanted the sourdough at 5.50; I held it at 5.20 because the loaf is what people compare with the supermarket.

Rejected: small rises twice a year (see [[price-rises-once-a-year-not-in-dribs]]) and a separate "takeaway price" (confusing and the till would need two item sets).
