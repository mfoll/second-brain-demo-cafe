# Early Shift Production Schedule

Kofi's timetable for the basement bakery from 04:30 to 12:30, the version in use since the new oven arrived, last revised for the autumn menu on 2026-10-12.

## Key Details

| Time | Bakery |
|---|---|
| 04:30 | Opening checks ([[daily-food-safety-checks]]); oven on, decks to 250 °C |
| 04:45 | Shape retarded sourdough from the walk-in; croissants out to prove |
| 05:30 | First bread bake: sourdough, three decks |
| 06:15 | Croissants and pains au chocolat, decks at 190 °C |
| 07:00 | Baguettes; first bread to the shop by 07:20 |
| 08:00 | Second viennoiserie bake to sales by day of week |
| 09:00 | Mix tomorrow's sourdough; laminate on Tuesdays and Fridays |
| 10:30 | Savoury bakes; fillings cooked and cooled ([[two-stage-cooling]]) |
| 12:00 | Small late bake on Saturdays only; clean down; cooling log handover |
| 12:30 | Shift ends; handover to Priyanka |

Daily quantities come from the day-of-week averages in [[square-pos-sales-data]] and are cut on rainy forecasts. Since March 2025 the late bake is small and planned against sales after 14:00 to keep waste down ([[end-of-day-waste-log]]).

## Why It Matters

This is where the hours go. Any idea of baking for a second site has to fit on this sheet, which is why Kofi reads [[central-production-kitchen]] as a 03:00 start.
