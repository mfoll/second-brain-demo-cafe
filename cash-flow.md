# Cash flow

## Summary

Broad article on cash flow as money moving into or out of a business or project. It treats each cash flow as defined by when it happens, how much, in which currency and through which account, and notes that future cash flows are uncertain and have to be estimated.

## Why It Matters

The useful idea for me was that a cash flow is defined by its date as much as its amount. That is the January 2025 lesson in one line ([[why-did-we-run-out-of-cash-in-january-2025]]): the VAT and the rent were never surprises in amount, only in timing.

## Caveats

Much of the article concerns finance and valuation (discounting, cash flow statements), which is more than a bakery needs. Read alongside [[cash-flow-forecasting]].
