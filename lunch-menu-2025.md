# Lunch Menu 2025

The nine-item lunch board from September 2025 to the cut on 2026-02-23, as printed for the window.

## Summary

Served 11:30 to 14:30, Tuesday to Saturday, cooked by Dominic Fairley. Soup of the day with bread 6.50; cheese and leek toastie 7.20; ham and mustard toastie 7.50; focaccia sandwich 7.90; sausage roll plate with slaw 8.50; grain bowl 10.50; roast vegetable bowl 10.50; shakshuka with sourdough 10.50; quiche plate with salad 10.90.

## Why It Matters

The baseline for the [[savoury-lunch-trim]]: what was cut, and which three items came back on 2026-04-20.

## Key Details

- The hot plates (shakshuka, quiche) were 31% of lunch units but the most waste and the worst margins ([[lunch-margin-analysis-2026-01]]).
- Sunday had no lunch menu even then.

Prices as of September 2025; the costing sheet has the 2026 versions of the three surviving items.
