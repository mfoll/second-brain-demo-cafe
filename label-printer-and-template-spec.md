# Label Printer And Template Spec

What we bought and set up to print allergen labels, October 2025.

## Key Details

- Direct thermal label printer, 58 mm labels, USB to the office laptop and a second cable run to the pass. 289 EUR.
- Label stock from Fold & Carry (Yusuf Karimi): 58 x 60 mm, 1,000 per roll, about 9 EUR a roll; we use about two rolls a month, four in December.
- Template: product name, full ingredients with allergens in bold, "may contain" line where relevant, use-by or best-before date, storage line, our name and address.
- The template reads the matrix export, so staff choose a product and a date; they never type ingredients.

## Why It Matters

Typing was the failure point. One printer covers the volume ([[do-we-need-a-second-label-printer]]).

## Caveats

Direct thermal labels fade in sunlight after a few weeks; fine for our shelf lives, not for anything kept longer.
