# End-of-Day Waste And Donation Routine

What whoever closes does with everything left on the counter. Takes about 15 minutes. Do it before the closing food safety checks ([[daily-food-safety-checks]]).

## When

Tuesday to Saturday from 16:00. Sunday from 14:00.

## Steps

1. Count what is left of every bakery product. Write the numbers on the waste sheet by the till, next to the baked numbers from the production sheet ([[end-of-day-waste-log]]). Count before anything is bagged.
2. Sort into three piles:
   - Bread, rolls, baguettes, plain focaccia: for the Larder.
   - Plain and fruit viennoiserie: for yesterday's bags.
   - Anything with cream, custard, cream cheese or a cooked filling, and anything that has been on a table: bin. Write "binned" on the sheet.
3. Larder bread: bag in brown paper sacks. Stick a donation label on each sack from the label printer, template "Larder". Check the label shows product, baked-on date and allergens. Weigh each sack and write the kilos on the Larder sheet.
4. Yesterday's bags: three pastries per bag, mixed is fine. Label "baked yesterday" with today's date. Price is half the shelf price of the three, rounded down to 5 cents. Into the dry store, top shelf.
5. 16:15: hand the Larder sacks to the volunteer at the shop door. They sign the Larder sheet.
6. If nobody has come by 16:45: freeze the sacks in the basement chest freezer (bottom basket, marked "Larder") and text Bernard Osei.
7. Sundays: no collection. Take the sacks to the community fridge on the way home, before 15:00, and note it on the sheet.
8. Leave the waste sheet in the folder. Lena types it up on Saturday.

## Do not

- Do not give the Larder anything unlabelled or anything with a filling.
- Do not put yesterday's bags out a second day. If they are left at 13:00 next day, bin and log them.
- Staff may take one item each at closing. Nothing else leaves free.

## If something is unclear

The rules come from the [[community-larder-donation-agreement]] and [[what-should-we-do-with-unsold-pastries]]. Ask Lena.
