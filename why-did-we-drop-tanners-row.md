# Why Did We Drop Tanner's Row?

Why did we stop pursuing the Tanner's Row shop as a second site in April 2026?

## Current Answer

Because a café that bakes or cooks there needs a kitchen extract and flue that would cost 38,000 EUR ([[tanners-row-extraction-and-flue-quote]]), the landlord would not contribute, and planning consent for the flue was uncertain. That pushed the cost over 130,000 EUR before working capital, against 120,000 EUR all-in for unit 7 at Saltings Market Hall, where the hall provides extraction ([[saltings-market-hall-unit-7]]).

The alternative, a cold-only café fed entirely from Hollowgate, was rejected because it would have forced the [[central-production-kitchen]] question a year early and put the whole load on the early shift.

Decided on 2026-04-16 ([[tanners-row-dropped-2026-04-16]]).

## What Would Reopen It

A landlord contribution to the flue, or a change of use already granted. Neither is likely.
