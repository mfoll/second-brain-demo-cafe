# Loyalty Programme First Year Figures

My review of the first year of Square Loyalty, 10 June 2025 to 9 June 2026, written on 21 July 2026 from the Square Loyalty reports and Square sales. Aggregates only; no member data leaves Square.

## Figures

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Members at 9 June 2026 | 1,840 |
| Members active in the last 90 days | about 1,020 |
| Share of hot drinks scanned | 38% |
| Rewards redeemed | about 1,950 |
| Cost of rewards at selling price | about 6,300 EUR, 3.5% of hot drink takings |
| Visits per week, scanned customers | 2.6 on average |

## What I take from it

- The scheme is mostly used by people who were already regulars. Fine: it is a thank-you, not a recruitment tool.
- The scan rate dips on Saturdays (about 29%) when the queue is long and the counter forgets to ask.
- Cost is about what I estimated for the paper cards including forged stamps, but now we can see it.

## Caveats

- "Visits per week" counts days with a scanned purchase. Unscanned regulars cannot be counted, so any comparison is rough.
- The cost uses selling price; at food cost it is about a third.
