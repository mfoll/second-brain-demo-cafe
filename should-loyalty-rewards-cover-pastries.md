# Should Loyalty Rewards Cover Pastries?

## Why It Matters

The scheme rewards hot drinks only. Pastry regulars, especially at weekends, ask why their croissant does not count. Adding pastries would make the scheme fairer to them but could cost real margin on our most butter-exposed product.

## Evidence So Far

- First-year figures ([[loyalty-programme-first-year-figures]]): rewards cost about 3.5% of hot drink takings. A hot drink costs us about 0.55 EUR to make; a croissant now costs about 0.91 EUR in food alone at 8.90 EUR/kg butter ([[butter-price-tracker]]).
- Amira's tally over two weeks in August 2026: about one in five scanned customers also bought a pastry.
- Options discussed with Lena: (a) leave as is; (b) a star for a pastry bought with a drink; (c) a separate pastry reward at 12 stars, redeemable Tuesday to Thursday only.

## Current Answer

Leaning to (c), because it pulls pastry trade into the quieter days without touching Saturday, but I have not costed it properly.

## What Would Settle It

A cost estimate for (c) from Square sales by day, decided before the 2027 price review. If the estimate is over 0.5% of pastry takings, leave the scheme alone.
