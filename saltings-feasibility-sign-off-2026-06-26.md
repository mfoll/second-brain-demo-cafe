# Saltings Feasibility Sign-Off 2026-06-26

Closed the [[saltings-hall-feasibility]] project with the final version of the [[saltings-hall-three-scenario-model]], after [[ruth-marchbank]]'s review on 2026-06-22.

## Results

- Base case: cumulative break-even in month 14.
- High case: month 9.
- Low case: not within 36 months, losing about 1,100 EUR a month at steady state.
- Investment 120,000 EUR ([[saltings-unit-7-fit-out-budget]]).

## What I Concluded

The unit can work if the hall delivers two-thirds of its own footfall projection. The study cannot tell me whether it will. The questions now are money and production, so I opened [[second-site-financing]] for July and noted the production question for later; it became [[central-production-kitchen]] in September.
