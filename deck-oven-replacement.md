# Deck Oven Replacement

Replacing the old two-deck oven, bought second-hand in 2019, after it failed for good on 2025-07-29. Everything we sell starts in that oven, so this ran the whole autumn of 2025.

## Outcome

A new three-deck electric oven installed and commissioned on 2025-11-14, financed on a five-year equipment lease of 41,500 EUR. Reached.

## Current State

- Completed 2025-11-14. The oven runs every day; first annual service due November 2026 under [[colm-ashworth]]'s contract.
- Lease payments of 812 EUR a month run to October 2030 ([[deck-oven-lease-agreement]]).
- The hired oven used from 2025-08-06 to 2025-11-14 has gone back ([[oven-hire-agreement-2025]]).
- Wholesale to cafés, which the hired oven could not carry, stayed paused after the new oven arrived because baker hours, not oven space, became the limit ([[why-did-we-pause-wholesale-to-cafes]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

The oven, its installation, the electrical upgrade it needed and how we financed it. The wholesale decision belongs to [[wholesale-to-cafes]]; the cash effect is tracked in [[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]].

## Working Context

The old oven had a 3,400 EUR repair in January 2025 (the repair that collided with the VAT payment, see [[cash-flow-forecast]]) and a second element failure in May. On 2025-07-29 the bottom deck controller burned out mid-bake, and Colm said parts were no longer made ([[deck-oven-failure-2025-07-29]], [[old-deck-oven-service-history]]).

For two days we baked in the top deck only and cut the bread range to six loaves. From 2025-08-06 we ran a hired two-deck electric oven at 1,150 EUR a month. It had less capacity than the old one: roughly 70% of the old daily output. We dropped two sourdough varieties and kept the shop supplied; wholesale took the cut, and on 2025-10-20 I paused it.

Three quotes came in during August and September ([[deck-oven-quotes-2025]]). We chose the mid-priced three-deck model at 41,500 EUR installed, mainly for steam injection on every deck and a local engineer. The cheapest option had a 14-week lead time and no service cover in Brackenmouth. Buying outright would have taken the whole cash buffer, so we leased ([[should-we-buy-or-lease-the-deck-oven]]). The building needed a new three-phase supply, agreed with [[cyril-ashdown]] and paid by us (2,300 EUR).

Install was 2025-11-10 to 2025-11-12, commissioning and test bakes 2025-11-13 and 2025-11-14 ([[new-deck-oven-commissioned-2025-11-14]], [[oven-commissioning-checklist]]).
