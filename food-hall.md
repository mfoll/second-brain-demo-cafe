# Food hall

## Summary

A very short article defining a food hall as a large standalone space, or a section of a department store, selling food and drink.

## Why It Matters

Looked up the day after the [[saltings-hall-first-visit-2026-03-05]]. It says almost nothing useful, which was a lesson in itself: the evidence for the hall model has to come from the hall's own figures and our own thinking ([[a-food-hall-unit-beats-a-standalone-shop]]).
