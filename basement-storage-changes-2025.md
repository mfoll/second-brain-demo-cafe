# Basement Storage Changes 2025

What we changed in the basement after the flour sacks were found against the hand-wash sink on 2025-06-18.

- **Sink rule**: nothing stored within one metre of any hand-wash sink. Yellow floor tape marks the zone. Written into plan version 3.1.
- **Racking**: four-tier galvanised racking in the dry store, fitted 2025-06-27 for 380 EUR, holding 24 sacks of 25 kg off the floor. It stands 15 cm from the wall so Callum Reid can inspect behind it.
- **Delivery size**: flour orders cut from 30 sacks a fortnight to 20 sacks every ten days, so a delivery fits the racking. Easier from July with Tarnwick Mill, who deliver weekly ([[flour-supplier-review]]).
- **Rotation**: new sacks bottom, open sacks top ([[first-in-first-out]]).

The basement is the same size it always was. Any plan to bake more here ([[central-production-kitchen]]) has to answer where another fortnight of flour goes.
