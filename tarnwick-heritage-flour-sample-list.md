# Tarnwick Heritage Flour Sample List

A one-page list of heritage and speciality flours from Tarnwick Mill, left by [[beatrix-coull]] with four 2.5 kg sample bags on 27 October 2025. Prices per kg excluding VAT, 25 kg sacks, delivered with our normal order.

## Key Details

| Flour | EUR/kg | Notes from Beatrix |
|---|---|---|
| Wholemeal spelt | 1.85 | Regular supply, grown within the region |
| White spelt | 2.05 | Sieved; better for enriched doughs |
| Wholemeal emmer | 2.60 | Small harvest, order by January for the year |
| Einkorn, wholemeal | 3.40 | Limited; minimum 2 sacks |
| Khorasan, white | 2.90 | Bought in; supply varies |
| Our white bread flour, for comparison | 0.92 | Current contract price |

## Why It Matters

The sample bags were the start of the [[heritage-grain-tasting-notes]] and the costs behind [[could-we-use-heritage-grain-flour-in-a-signature-loaf]]. A 30% spelt loaf adds about 0.25 EUR of flour.

## Caveats

Prices valid to 31 March 2026; Beatrix said spelt would hold at about the same price for 2026 but emmer may rise.
