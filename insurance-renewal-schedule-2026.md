# Insurance Renewal Schedule 2026

The policy schedule for the bakehouse's combined insurance from 2026-03-01, placed by [[petra-szabo]].

## Summary

Annual premium 4,860 EUR including insurance tax, up from 4,050 EUR, mainly because of the new equipment breakdown section (640 EUR) and a higher contents value after the new oven.

## Why It Matters

The July 2025 oven failure showed a gap: breakdown was not covered, so the hired oven and lost wholesale came out of our own cash. The breakdown cover closes that gap for the next failure. Premium is a fixed March line in the [[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]].

## Key Details

- Contents and stock 145,000 EUR; business interruption 12 months, 260,000 EUR gross profit.
- Equipment breakdown: oven, mixers, refrigeration, espresso machine; 500 EUR excess.
- Public liability 5 million EUR; employers' liability as required.
- Paid in one instalment to avoid the 8% charge for monthly payments.

## Caveats

Business interruption only follows insured damage or breakdown; a closure ordered by the council would not be covered.
