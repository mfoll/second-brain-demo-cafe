# Plant-Based Baking

Exploration cluster for vegan and egg-free baking. Customers ask for a vegan pastry two or three times a week, mostly on Saturdays; at the moment our only fully plant-based items are the sourdough loaves, the rye and the oat bars.

## Why I am looking at this

A vegan croissant would be the obvious product, but lamination with plant fats is hard and the butter in our croissant is the reason people buy it. Cakes are easier: aquafaba meringue and egg-free sponges are well understood. The allergen side matters too: a plant-based product baked in the same basement is not "free from" anything, and our labels must say so ([[allergen-management]]).
