# Debenture

## Summary

Describes debentures as a way companies raise medium- or long-term money at a fixed rate of interest. Originally the word meant a document creating or acknowledging a debt; in some places it now means a bond or loan note. Debenture holders rank ahead of shareholders if the company fails.

## Why It Matters

Read after [[devon-pryce]]'s email listed a debenture as security for the loan ([[pellow-mutual-loan-conditions-email]]). The article's main sense, a security issued by larger companies, is not what the bank means; [[ruth-marchbank]] explained that for us it is a charge over the company's assets. Kept as a reminder that the same word means different things.

## Caveats

Mostly about debentures as tradable securities. Does not describe a bank's charge over a small company's assets.
