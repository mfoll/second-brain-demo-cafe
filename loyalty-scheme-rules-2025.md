# Loyalty Scheme Rules 2025

The rules of our Square Loyalty scheme, set up on 2 June 2025 and live from 10 June. Still current in October 2026.

## Summary

- Join with a phone number at the till. No app for the customer.
- One star per hot drink of any size, including takeaway. Maximum one star per drink, any number of drinks per visit.
- Nine stars: one free hot drink of any size, any milk.
- Stars do not expire while the member visits at least once in 12 months.
- Not counted: pastries, cakes, bread, lunch, yesterday's bags, Christmas preorders.
- No marketing texts. Square sends only the reward notification.

## Why It Matters

These are the rules the counter applies every day and the base for the pastry question ([[should-loyalty-rewards-cover-pastries]]). Nine rather than ten stars was deliberate: one stamp fewer than the paper card, so nobody felt worse off.

## Key Details

- Staff drinks are not scanned.
- A reward can be used on the same visit it is earned.
- Lena can add a star by hand for a missed scan if the customer shows a receipt from the same day.
