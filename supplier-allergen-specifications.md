# Supplier Allergen Specifications

The folder of product specification sheets from every supplier, one per ingredient, with the date received.

## Summary

About 110 sheets from Tarnwick Mill, the pastry flour wholesaler, Glenvarrow Dairy, Kestrin Chocolate, Quarrymoor Farm, Brook & Daughters and the dry goods wholesaler. Each declares allergens contained and any "may contain" risk from the supplier's own site.

## Why It Matters

The [[allergen-matrix]] is only as good as these. Two surprises during the overhaul: the fondant glaze contained milk powder, and the seeded flour blend carried a sesame "may contain". When a supplier changes, the new sheet is requested before the first delivery, as with the move to Tarnwick in July 2025 ([[flour-supplier-review]]).

## Caveats

Sheets older than two years are re-requested each October. Twelve were renewed in October 2026.
