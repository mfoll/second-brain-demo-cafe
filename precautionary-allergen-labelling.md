# Precautionary Allergen Labelling

The "may contain" line: a warning that an allergen not in the recipe could be present because of shared equipment, shared space or a supplier's process. It is voluntary and only useful when it is specific.

## The tension

Too little, and someone with an allergy trusts a product that carries a real risk. Too much, and the label becomes noise: if everything may contain everything, people with allergies stop reading, or stop buying from us at all.

## How we apply it

- Used when a supplier declares it, or when we use the allergen in the same step and cannot separate (nuts on the pastry bench, sesame on the seeded loaves).
- Not used as blanket cover. Instead, every label and the counter folder say we bake in one room with the main allergens ([[do-we-need-may-contain-labels]]).
- Recorded with a reason in the [[allergen-matrix]], so we can explain each one to a customer.
