# Loyalty Launch Day 2025-06-10

Square Loyalty went live at the counter on Tuesday 10 June 2025, replacing the paper stamp cards.

## What happened

- 07:30 start with the counter script ([[loyalty-launch-poster-and-counter-script]]): ask "Do you want to join the coffee card on your phone number?" at payment for every hot drink.
- 87 sign-ups on the first day; 412 by the end of the first week.
- Paper card holders could bring their cards in until 31 December 2025 and have their stamps honoured; [[amira-saleh]] transferred stamps as stars for anyone who asked, up to 8.
- One problem: two customers did not want to give a phone number. We kept a small stack of paper cards for them until the end of the year.

## What we learned

- Asking works; the poster does not. On Tuesday afternoon, when Finn was alone at the till and forgot to ask, sign-ups dropped to almost nothing.
- The queue was slower by about 10 seconds a transaction for the first week, then back to normal.

## Follow-up

Membership reached 1,104 by 25 July, when the project closed ([[loyalty-card-launch]]).
