# Finance Course Syllabus

The syllabus of "Finance for Food Businesses", a 12-week evening course at Brackenmouth College taught by [[farid-nasser]], Mondays 18:30 to 20:30, 15 September to 8 December 2025. Fee 480 EUR.

## Summary

| Week | Subject |
|---|---|
| 1 | Reading a profit and loss account |
| 2 | Fixed, variable and semi-variable costs |
| 3 | Gross margin and food cost by product |
| 4 | Contribution margin and break-even |
| 5 | Pricing: cost-plus, menu engineering, price rises |
| 6 | Cash flow and the 13-week forecast |
| 7 | Working capital: stock, deposits, supplier terms, VAT timing |
| 8 | Labour cost and rotas |
| 9 | Borrowing: overdrafts, loans, leases |
| 10 | Raising equity, franchising |
| 11 | Planning a second site or a new line |
| 12 | Presentations of each participant's model |

## Why It Matters

Weeks 2, 4 and 7 changed how I run the numbers ([[break-even-takings]], [[customer-deposits-as-working-capital]]). Week 11 came a year too early for the second site but I kept the handout. Weeks 10 and 11 were least relevant for a nine-person bakery.
