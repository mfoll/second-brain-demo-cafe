# Taking Christmas Preorders

How to take a Christmas order at the counter or by phone, from 1 October until orders close on 12 December 2026 or a product reaches its cap.

## Before you start

- Check the caps board in the back corridor. If a product is crossed out, it is sold out; offer another.
- Use the 2026 form only ([[christmas-preorder-form-2026]]), the green one. The 2025 white forms are in the bin.

## Steps

1. Fill in the form with the customer: name, phone number, products and quantities, collection day (23 or 24 December) and half-hour slot.
2. Add up the total from the price list on the form. Deposit is 30% of the total, rounded to the nearest 50 cents.
3. Take the deposit on Square using the item "Christmas deposit" and type the amount. Card or cash. Write the Square receipt number on the form.
4. Read the refund rule to the customer: full refund if cancelled by 12 December, no refund after that, and orders not collected by 14:00 on 24 December are released.
5. Tear off the customer copy and give it to them. Their copy is their receipt for collection.
6. Put the shop copy in the box file "Christmas 2026" in date order.
7. Tick the products on the tally sheet next to the box file.

## Phone orders

Same form. Take the deposit by card over the phone only on the Square card-not-present screen. Never write card numbers anywhere.

## Cancellations

Before 12 December: refund the deposit on Square against the original receipt, write "cancelled" and the date on both copies, and untick the tally sheet. After 12 December: no refund; refer to Lena or Odile if the customer disagrees.

## On the late-night shopping evening

Take orders at the separate table by the window, not at the till.

Lena copies the tally into the [[christmas-preorder-tracker-2026]] every Saturday.
