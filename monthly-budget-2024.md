# Monthly Budget 2024

The monthly profit budget I used for 2024 and January 2025. Archived on 2025-03-28 and superseded by the [[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]].

## Summary

Twelve columns of expected sales and costs per month. It showed January 2025 as a profitable month with about 2,100 EUR of profit.

## Why It Matters

Kept as the example of why a profit budget is not a cash plan. It had VAT netted out of sales and the rent spread evenly across months, so the 11,800 EUR VAT payment and the 9,750 EUR rent quarter in January did not appear ([[why-did-we-run-out-of-cash-in-january-2025]]).

## Key Details

- Built in December 2023 from 2023 actuals plus 5%.
- 2024 actual turnover came within 3% of budget; the budget was not wrong about profit.
