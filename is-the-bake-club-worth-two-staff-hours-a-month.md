# Is The Bake Club Worth Two Staff Hours A Month?

## Why It Matters

Each session costs about 5 staff hours in total (me plus Priyanka or Grace, 2.5 hours each with preparation and travel) and about 38 EUR of ingredients. In a month with a short rota, those hours come out of the shop. I asked this in March 2026, in the middle of the lunch cut, when every hour was being counted.

## Current Answer

Yes, for now. Kept for 2026-27 with the same format. Reasons:

- Cost is about 1,100 EUR a year including staff time at average rates; less than a single print advertising campaign we ran in 2023.
- Families come in: Lena counted 23 bake club families in the shop on the Saturday after sessions over the pilot year, and Christmas 2025 orders included at least 9 from school families (they said so on the form).
- [[priyanka-desai]] and [[grace-oyelaran]] both asked to keep doing it. Staff liking a task is worth something in a year with a lot of change ([[team-culture]]).
- Samir's class teacher survey rated it the best enrichment activity of the year.

## Limits

If a session would fall in a December week or the week of a big event, we skip it. If it ever needs more than one staff member besides me, we cut it to termly.

## What Would Reopen It

A rota so tight that the session hours come from the early shift, or the school asking for more than monthly.
