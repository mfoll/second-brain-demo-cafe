# Point of sale

## Summary

Wikipedia's article on the point of sale: the time and place where a retail transaction is completed, and by extension the hardware and software that calculate the amount, take payment and issue a receipt. It describes how modern systems also record item-level sales, manage stock and produce reports, and the shift from standalone tills to tablet and cloud systems.

## Why It Matters

I looked it up in March 2025 when deciding how much to rely on our till data for the bake plan. The point I took: a modern till is a sales database, not just a cash drawer, and we had been using about a tenth of it. From April 2025 Lena and I exported takings by hour and by item every month ([[square-pos-sales-data]]), which later fed the waste work, the lunch decision and the Christmas planning.

## Key Details

- Item-level records make it possible to compare sales by hour, day and product.
- Cloud systems keep historical data available for export.
- Reporting quality depends on how consistently items are named and set up.
