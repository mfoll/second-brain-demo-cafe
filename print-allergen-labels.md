# Print Allergen Labels

How to print labels for anything we prepack on site. Labels come from the allergen matrix only. Never handwrite an ingredient list and never type one into the template.

## Before you start

- Is the product in the matrix? If it is new or the recipe changed this week, stop and ask Odile or Priyanka. Nothing new is labelled until its row is checked by two people.
- Printer on, label roll loaded (spare rolls in the drawer under the pass).

## Steps

1. On the office laptop, open the label template from the desktop shortcut "Labels". It loads the latest matrix export automatically.
2. Choose the product from the list.
3. Choose the date type: use-by for anything with a filling, cream or meat; best-before for bread, cookies, granola.
4. Enter the date. Shelf lives are printed on the sheet taped beside the printer.
5. Enter the number of labels and print.
6. Check the first label against the product: right name, right date, allergens in bold, "may contain" line where expected.
7. Label each pack on the back, flat, not over the seal.

## If something looks wrong

Do not use the labels. Put the product aside, tell Odile or Priyanka, and note it on the daily sheet. A wrong label is a food safety problem, not a printing problem.

## If the printer fails

Print the same template on A4 label sheets from the office printer (sheets in the top drawer). Tested November 2025.

## Verification

Odile checks a random five labels against the matrix every Monday with the sheets ([[daily-food-safety-checks]]).
