# Emmer

English Wikipedia article on emmer wheat.

## Summary

Emmer is a hybrid wheat species and, with einkorn, one of the first crops domesticated in the Near East. Widely grown in the ancient world, it survives today as a relict crop in mountain regions of Europe and Asia, and is one of the three grains sold as farro in Italy.

## Why It Matters

Read after [[beatrix-coull]] left the Tarnwick samples in October 2025 ([[tarnwick-heritage-flour-sample-list]]). It explained why emmer is the most expensive of the regular heritage flours (2.60 EUR/kg against 0.92 for our white): a small, relict crop. The 100% emmer loaf at the first tasting was dense and split the team ([[heritage-grain-tasting-notes]]).
