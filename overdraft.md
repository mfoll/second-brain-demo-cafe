# Overdraft

## Summary

Explains an overdraft as withdrawing more than an account holds, so the balance goes below zero. With an arranged limit the bank charges interest at the agreed rate; going beyond the arrangement brings fees and higher charges.

## Why It Matters

Looked up on the Saturday we sat at the limit ([[overdraft-squeeze-january-2025]]) to understand what happens if we went past 6,000 EUR. Background for [[do-we-still-need-the-overdraft]].
