# Marchbank Ochoa Year-End Accounts 2025

The statutory accounts of Hollowgate Bakehouse Ltd for the year to 2025-12-31, prepared by Marchbank Ochoa Accountants and signed by me on 2026-04-22.

## Summary

Turnover 609,800 EUR, gross margin 69.4%, profit before tax 38,600 EUR, after the costs of the oven failure and the hired oven. Corporation tax of about 9,300 EUR due in autumn 2026.

## Why It Matters

The baseline for the second-site discussion: a business that makes money but does not throw off much cash. It also gave the overall figure against the 68% target in [[gross-profit-margin]].

- The new deck oven is on the balance sheet as a leased asset from November 2025 ([[deck-oven-replacement]]).
- Cash at year end: 52,300 EUR, inflated by Christmas deposits not yet earned.

## Caveats

Year-end cash flatters the position because of December takings and deposits; the forecast's January low point is the better guide ([[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]]).
