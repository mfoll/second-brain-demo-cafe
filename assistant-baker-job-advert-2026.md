# Assistant Baker Job Advert 2026

The advert for the assistant baker post, published 2026-06-08.

## Summary

Assistant baker, early shift, 30 hours a week over four mornings, 04:30 to 12:30, with one Saturday a month. Hand-shaping bread, mixing and baking under the head baker, cleaning down, the cooling log and opening checks. Experience in any bakery welcome; training given on sourdough and the deck oven. Level 2 food safety within the first month, paid. Closing date 2026-06-29.

## Why It Matters

We wrote it with Kofi to be honest about the hours. A first draft said "early starts"; Kofi changed it to the actual time, which probably cost us applicants and saved us a bad hire. 23 applications came in ([[assistant-baker-hiring]]).
