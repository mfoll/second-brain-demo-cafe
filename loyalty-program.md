# Loyalty Program

English Wikipedia article on loyalty or rewards programmes.

## Summary

Describes a loyalty programme as a marketing strategy meant to encourage customers to keep buying from one business, or from a group of businesses that share a programme.

## Why It Matters

Read at the start of the [[loyalty-card-launch]] in May 2025, mostly to be clear what we were for and against. The definition is about encouraging customers to keep coming, which is how I framed our scheme: one star per hot drink, rewarding visits rather than spend ([[loyalty-points-should-reward-habits-not-spend]]).

## Caveats

General background only. The design choices are ours and are recorded in [[loyalty-scheme-rules-2025]].
