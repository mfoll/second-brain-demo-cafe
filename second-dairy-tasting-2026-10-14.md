# Second Dairy Tasting 2026-10-14

Nils Jorgensen of Coldbeck Creamery came at 14:30 with samples: 5 kg of lamination plates and 2 kg of dough butter.

## What happened

- Tasted both butters plain against Glenvarrow's, with Priyanka and Kofi. Coldbeck slightly saltier on the palate even though both are unsalted; Kofi thought it was the culture. Not a problem in pastry.
- Talked delivery: twice a week, Tuesday and Friday, 40 kg minimum. We would need about 30 kg more chiller space on a Friday than now; the walk-in can take it if we clear the lunch shelf.
- Price: "around 8.20" for a six-month commitment like Glenvarrow's. Written quote promised by 2026-10-30.
- Spec sheets left with us ([[second-dairy-product-specifications-2026-10]]); reading list I had sent him: [[laminated-dough]] and [[butter]].

## Next day

Priyanka laminated one batch with the plates on 2026-10-15. Promising, not proven ([[priyanka-email-butter-sheet-trial-2026-10]]). Second test booked for the week of 2026-10-27, on a warm morning if we get one.

## Where it leaves us

Two credible options and one week of slack before Moira's deadline of 2026-11-06. See [[dairy-contract-renegotiation]].
