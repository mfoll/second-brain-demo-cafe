# Cash Buffer Calculation 2026-09

The calculation [[ruth-marchbank]] and I did before the [[second-site-review-2026-09-30]] of what a three-month cash buffer means for Hollowgate with a second site.

## Summary

About 95,000 EUR: three months of fixed costs and payroll at Hollowgate, plus the second site's fixed commitments, plus an amount standing in for the overdraft. We held 41,000 EUR in September 2026.

## Why It Matters

It is the number behind Ruth's advice and the first condition in [[should-we-open-a-second-site]].

## Key Details

| Monthly line | EUR |
|---|---|
| Hollowgate payroll including employer costs (rota average) | 19,600 |
| Rent | 3,250 |
| Deck oven lease | 812 |
| Utilities | 1,450 |
| Insurance, software, fees, other fixed | 1,100 |
| Unit 7 loan repayment | 1,720 |
| Unit 7 rent and service charge | 1,760 |
| **Monthly total** | **29,692** |

Three months: 89,076 EUR. Plus 6,000 EUR so the overdraft is not part of the plan. Total 95,076 EUR, rounded to 95,000.

Held on 2026-09-21: 41,000 EUR above the 15,000 EUR weekly floor ([[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]]).

Gap: 54,000 EUR. At the 2025-26 rate of cash build (about 2,400 EUR a month after tax, outside December), closing it would take until 2028 without the second site's own takings.

## Caveats

Ingredient purchases are excluded because they scale with takings. Ruth's rule is a rule of thumb, not a bank requirement ([[how-big-a-cash-buffer-do-we-need]]).
