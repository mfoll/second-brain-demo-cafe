# Christmas 2025 Preorder Debrief

Written on 29 December 2025 with Lena's notes. The starting point for [[christmas-preorders-2026]].

## Orders

- 214 orders (151 in 2024). 7 cancelled before 12 December, 5 not collected.
- Average order about 76 EUR. Total order value about 16,300 EUR.
- Product mix: 142 stollen, 88 bûches, 196 boxes of mince pies, 120 fruited sourdough loaves.
- Best day for orders: the late-night shopping evening, 31 orders ([[traders-association-late-night-shopping-2025-12-04]]).
- Turned away about 25 people after 19 December when we stopped taking bûche orders.

## Cash

> [!success]
> 30% deposits brought in about 4,900 EUR between October and mid-December, and the balances on 23 and 24 December. With that, January 2026 never touched the overdraft, the first January since 2022.

The detail, with refunds and kept deposits, is in [[christmas-2025-deposit-reconciliation]]. The idea behind it: [[customer-deposits-as-working-capital]].

## What worked

- Half-hour collection slots.
- Deposits through a Square item, so the till and Xero both show them.
- Stollen made early (from 15 December), which spread the load.

## What did not

- Bûche moulds: we needed 10 more. Priyanka worked until 19:00 on the 22nd.
- Walk-in customers on the 23rd found too little bread. Next year: a fixed counter allowance.
- The order form had no box for the collection slot; Lena added it by hand on 80 forms.
- No waste at all on 23 and 24 December, which means we under-baked for walk-ins.

## For 2026

Target growth to about 300, caps per product, a collection table by the window, and order deposits counted in the forecast only once received.
