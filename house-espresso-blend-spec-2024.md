# House Espresso Blend Spec 2024

The old roaster's spec sheet for the house blend we used until 2025-11-28. Kept for the record; superseded by [[ombrel-roasters-wholesale-offer-2025]] and the [[coffee-roaster-change]].

## Summary

A three-origin blend at 19.80 EUR/kg, medium-dark roast, recommended recipe 18 g in, 36 g out, 25 to 28 seconds. Roast dates printed on bags; delivery fortnightly.

## Why It Matters

The baseline we compared against in the blind tasting ([[coffee-blind-tasting-2025-10-15]]). The blend changed twice in 2025 without a new spec sheet, which is the reason we started looking.

## Key Details

- Price 19.80 EUR/kg, unchanged since 2023.
- Free grinder servicing twice a year.

## Caveats

Out of date and no longer accurate even for the old roaster.
