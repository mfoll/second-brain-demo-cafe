# Lease

## Summary

Describes a lease as a contract in which a lessee pays the owner of an asset, the lessor, for its use, with typical subjects being property, vehicles and business equipment, and with conditions on how the asset may be used.

## Why It Matters

Read while going through the unit 7 heads of terms ([[saltings-market-hall-unit-7]]). We would hold three leases at once if unit 7 goes ahead: Hollowgate ([[hollowgate-lease-summary]]), the deck oven, and the hall. The article's split between property and equipment leases is the same split as in [[second-site-financing]].

## Caveats

Not legal advice and not specific to any country's commercial lease law. The hall's solicitor and ours would govern the actual terms.
