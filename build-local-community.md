# Build Local Community

Everything that ties Hollowgate Bakehouse to the people of Brackenmouth: regulars, the loyalty scheme, social media, the Christmas preorders, the bake club at Saltings Lane Primary, the Community Larder and the traders on our street. It has no end date because the shop lives on regulars; about two thirds of weekday takings come from people we see at least once a week.

## Scope

- Marketing, social media posts and photography, the loyalty scheme, local press.
- Seasonal preorders (Christmas, and since 2026 a small Easter run) and the deposits that come with them.
- The [[school-bake-club]], the [[community-larder-donation-agreement]] and our part in the [[food-waste]] work.
- The Brackenmouth Traders' Association and neighbours on Hollowgate.
- Not here: wholesale customers as a business line (that is [[plan-business-growth]] through [[wholesale-to-cafes]]), staff matters ([[run-daily-operations]]), and the hygiene rating ([[keep-food-safe-and-licensed]]).
- Privacy: no customer names apart from the few people with their own notes, no loyalty member data, only aggregates from [[square-loyalty-monthly-report-summary]].

## Current State

- [[christmas-preorders-2026]] opened on 1 October 2026. 61 orders by 22 October against a target of 320; deposits stay at 30%.
- [[school-bake-club]] started its second year on 7 October 2026 with [[samir-belkacem]]'s new Year 4 class.
