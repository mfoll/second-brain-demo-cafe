# Run Daily Operations

Everything that has to happen for the shop to open at 07:30 and close clean at 16:00, six days a week: the early bake, the production schedule, equipment, the staff rota, hiring and training. It never ends, so it lives here rather than in a Project.

## Scope

- Opening and closing, the production schedule in the basement bakery and the front-of-house routine.
- Equipment: ovens, mixers, sheeter, chillers, coffee machine, and who services them ([[equipment-register]]).
- The rota in Deputy, timesheets, overtime and holiday cover.
- Hiring, induction and training for the nine staff ([[staff-training-matrix]]).
- Food safety checks are done by the same people but owned by [[keep-food-safe-and-licensed]]. Supplier ordering lives under [[manage-suppliers-and-costs]]; the menu under [[develop-menu-and-products]].
