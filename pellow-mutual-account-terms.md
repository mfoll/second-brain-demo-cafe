# Pellow Mutual Account Terms

The current terms for the bakehouse's business current account, overdraft and savings account at Pellow Mutual, from the annual review letter of 2026-02-17.

## Summary

Current account at 32 EUR a month plus per-transaction fees; 6,000 EUR overdraft renewed for 12 months at 11.4% with a 120 EUR arrangement fee; instant-access business savings account (the VAT pot) at 1.6%.

## Why It Matters

The overdraft has not been used since February 2025. The question of keeping or dropping it is in [[do-we-still-need-the-overdraft]]. Ruth counts it as part of the buffer only as a last resort ([[cash-buffer-calculation-2026-09]]).

## Key Details

- Cash deposit fee 0.6%, which is why Lena banks cash weekly, not daily.
- Overdraft renewal date 2027-02-17.
- The savings account allows up to six withdrawals a month.
