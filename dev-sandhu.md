# Dev Sandhu

Head roaster and co-owner of Ombrel Roasters in the next town. Our espresso supplier since 2025-11-28.

## Role

Roasts the Estuary blend we use as house espresso and the decaf. Calibrates our two grinders and trains the baristas twice a year, which was part of his offer ([[ombrel-roasters-wholesale-offer-2025]]).

## How we work together

- Amira is his main contact for quality; I deal with him on price and volume.
- Orders every Monday for Wednesday delivery, roasted the Monday or Tuesday before. He prefers 7 to 21 days off roast for espresso and asked us not to stockpile.
- Straight talker. When we asked for a cheaper price he said no, and offered the training day instead.
