# Heritage Grains

Exploration cluster for older wheats (emmer, spelt, einkorn, khorasan) and whether one of them could carry a signature loaf. It started when [[beatrix-coull]] brought sample bags from Tarnwick Mill after the [[flour-supplier-review]] in 2025.

## What I know so far

- These flours make weaker doughs than modern bread wheat; spelt in particular over-mixes easily and wants shorter fermentation.
- Price: Tarnwick quoted 1.85 EUR/kg for wholemeal spelt and 2.60 EUR/kg for emmer against 0.92 EUR/kg for our main white bread flour (October 2025 sample list).
- None of them is gluten-free, and all must be labelled as cereals containing gluten. Some customers believe spelt is safe for coeliacs; it is not.
