# Value-added tax

## Summary

General article on value-added tax as a consumption tax charged on the value added at each stage of production and distribution. Sellers collect it from buyers and pass it to the state, and many systems exempt or reduce the rate on some goods.

## Why It Matters

Read before opening the VAT pot in March 2025. The mechanism, that the seller collects the tax for the state, is the basis of [[vat-money-is-not-our-money]] and [[should-we-set-aside-vat-weekly]].

## Caveats

General and international; it describes no country's rates or rules. Our rates and dates come from our own registration and [[ruth-marchbank]], not from this article.
