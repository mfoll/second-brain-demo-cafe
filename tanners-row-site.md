# Tanner's Row Site

Assess the empty shop on Tanner's Row as a second Hollowgate café. Superseded by [[saltings-hall-feasibility]].

## Outcome

Superseded by the Saltings Market Hall option after the extraction and flue quote came to 38,000 EUR.

## Current State

- Superseded on 2026-04-16 ([[tanners-row-dropped-2026-04-16]]). The answer to "why not Tanner's Row" is in [[why-did-we-drop-tanners-row]].
- Nothing is owed: we paid only for the ventilation survey (450 EUR).

## Scope and Boundaries

One property, offered through [[simone-ward]]. Did not include any lease negotiation beyond asking the rent and the incentives.

## Working Context

The shop ([[tanners-row-shop-particulars]]): 74 m2 ground floor, former phone repair shop, 2,100 EUR a month, ten-year lease, landlord offering four months rent-free. Ten minutes' walk from Hollowgate, on the route from the station car park.

What killed it: it had never been a food premises. Any hot food or baking would need a kitchen extract and a flue up the back of a three-storey building, and the landlord would not pay for it. The quote from Harrowby Ventilation came to 38,000 EUR ([[tanners-row-extraction-and-flue-quote]]), on top of a fit-out I had estimated at 70,000 EUR. That pushed the total past 130,000 EUR before working capital. A cold-only café (no ovens, everything baked at Hollowgate) would avoid the flue, but would put the whole production question onto the basement and Kofi a year earlier than I wanted.

Simone mentioned Saltings Market Hall at the second viewing; that is how the hall came in ([[saltings-hall-first-visit-2026-03-05]]).
