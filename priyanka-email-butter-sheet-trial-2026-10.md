# Priyanka Email Butter Sheet Trial 2026-10

Priyanka's email after laminating one batch of croissants with Coldbeck's butter plates on 2026-10-15, summarised.

## Summary

One batch of 48 croissants each with Glenvarrow block and Coldbeck plates, same dough, three single folds. Coldbeck's plates were faster to start (no pounding), stayed pliable at 13 °C and gave a clean honeycomb. Glenvarrow's layers were slightly more even. On the shelf, nobody at the counter could tell them apart.

## Why It Matters

The first evidence that switching butter would not cost us quality in the [[dairy-contract-renegotiation]]. Priyanka asked for a second test on a warm morning before she would trust it.

## Key Details

- Time saved with plates: about 10 minutes per batch, roughly 40 minutes a week at our volume.
- One Coldbeck sheet cracked at the second fold when it was too cold (straight from the fridge at 4 °C); fine after 20 minutes at room temperature.

## Caveats

One batch, one morning, one person. Priyanka's own words: "promising, not proven".
