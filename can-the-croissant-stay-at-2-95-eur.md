# Can The Croissant Stay At 2.95 EUR?

## Why It Matters

The croissant is our best seller by units. It went from 2.60 to 2.95 EUR on 2026-03-09, and its food cost has crept from 27% after the rise back to 29% of the selling price with butter at 8.90 ([[croissant-cost-breakdown]]). Another rise within a year would break my own rule ([[price-rises-once-a-year-not-in-dribs]]).

## Current Answer

Probably yes until the January 2027 review, if butter is fixed at 8.40 or lower for the winter. At 8.40 the croissant's food cost falls to about 0.84 EUR (28%). If we stay on the monthly list and butter reaches 9.20, it goes to about 0.87 (30%), and I would look at 3.10 in the January review.

## What Would Settle It

- The butter decision ([[should-we-sign-the-fixed-price-butter-contract]]).
- Croissant unit sales through the Christmas period against 2025.
- The January 2027 costing run in the [[recipe-costing-sheet]].

## Options considered

- Smaller croissant at the same price: rejected, customers notice and it is the product we are known for.
- Cheaper butter blend: rejected ([[all-butter-croissants-are-not-where-we-save]]).
