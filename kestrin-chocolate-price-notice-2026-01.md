# Kestrin Chocolate Price Notice 2026-01

Kestrin's letter of November 2025 announcing new prices from 2026-01-05, handed over by Rafaela Montoya with the samples.

## Summary

Chocolate products up about 20% from January 2026, nuts up 6 to 8%. Dark couverture 12.80 to 15.40 EUR/kg; chocolate batons for pain au chocolat 13.50 to 16.20 EUR/kg; hazelnuts 11.20 to 12.10 EUR/kg; almonds unchanged until March.

## Why It Matters

Rafaela's early warning let us buy six weeks of batons (40 kg) at the old price in December, about 108 EUR saved. The new prices fed straight into the [[price-review-2026]]: pain au chocolat from 2.90 to 3.25 EUR. Summarised with the other rises in [[supplier-price-rises-2026]].

## Key Details

- Reason given: cocoa bean prices; Kestrin quotes its own costs up by more than it passed on.
- Prices held until 2026-06-30, then reviewed; the July list was unchanged.
- A lower-cocoa baton was offered at 14.10 EUR/kg. Tested by Priyanka in September 2026 and rejected: waxy and too sweet.

## Caveats

Letter prices exclude delivery; orders under 150 EUR pay a 12 EUR delivery charge.
