# Saltings Hall Three-Scenario Model

My spreadsheet model of a Hollowgate counter café in unit 7 at Saltings Market Hall: 36 months of monthly profit and cash in low, base and high cases. Final version signed off on 2026-06-26 after [[ruth-marchbank]]'s review.

## Summary

Base case reaches cumulative cash break-even in month 14, the high case in month 9, and the low case not within 36 months. The low case loses about 1,100 EUR a month at steady state.

## Why It Matters

It is the main evidence for the [[saltings-hall-feasibility]] and the [[second-site-review-2026-09-30]]. The low case also runs in the second tab of the [[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]] as a stress test for Hollowgate.

## Key Details

| Assumption | Low | Base | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visitors a week | 4,500 | 6,000 | 9,000 |
| Transactions a week | 530 | 760 | 990 |
| Average spend | 7.40 EUR | 7.40 EUR | 7.40 EUR |
| Weekly takings by month 6 | 3,900 EUR | 5,600 EUR | 7,300 EUR |
| Gross margin | 64% | 66% | 67% |
| Staff cost a month | 9,800 EUR | 9,800 EUR | 10,900 EUR |

Fixed costs a month: rent 1,450 EUR, service charge 310 EUR, loan repayment 1,720 EUR, utilities 450 EUR, extra accountancy and software 160 EUR, insurance 175 EUR. Three rent-free months at opening. Opening investment 120,000 EUR ([[saltings-unit-7-fit-out-budget]]).

Ruth's change on 2026-06-22: the loan repayments and extra fees were moved from Hollowgate's costs into the unit's. Base case break-even moved from month 12 to month 14.

Sensitivity: each 0.50 EUR on average spend moves base-case break-even by about two months; losing one weekday of trade to the hall closing for events moves it by about one month.

## Caveats

- Footfall is the hall's projection, discounted ([[saltings-hall-footfall-projections]]).
- Production cost assumes Hollowgate bakes for the unit at marginal ingredient cost plus 8 extra baker hours a day, which is the unresolved [[central-production-kitchen]] question.
