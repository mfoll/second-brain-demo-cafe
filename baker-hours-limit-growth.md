# Baker Hours Limit Growth

## Stance

> [!info]
> At Hollowgate the binding limit on growth is the bakers' hours, not the oven, the money or the demand. Any growth plan that adds output by starting the early shift earlier or stretching it longer is the wrong plan.

Written on the evening of the [[second-site-review-2026-09-30]].

## Scope

Wholesale, a second site, central production, larger Christmas volumes. Applies to the people on the early shift: [[kofi-asare]], [[joel-whitcombe]], [[hana-kobayashi]], and me on my two pastry mornings.

## Why I Think This

- Kofi averaged 47 hours a week in summer 2025, visible once Deputy logged it ([[rota-system-switch]]).
- Wholesale was paused partly because it ate baker hours ([[why-did-we-pause-wholesale-to-cafes]]).
- The new oven restored capacity but not people ([[deck-oven-replacement]]).
- The capacity study found the oven idle after 11:00 ([[central-production-capacity-study]]).
- Kofi's objection was fair ([[kofi-email-on-central-production]]).

## What Would Change My Mind

A shift pattern the bakers propose themselves that adds output without longer or earlier mornings, for example an afternoon prep shift ([[central-production-shift-pattern-options]]).

## Implications

Growth plans are costed with extra bakers from the start, not as an afterthought. The bakers see production plans before they are drafted, not after.
