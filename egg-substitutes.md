# Egg Substitutes

English Wikipedia article on egg substitutes in cooking and baking.

## Summary

Covers products used to replace eggs, and the reasons people choose them: egg allergy, vegan or egg-free vegetarian diets, animal welfare or environmental concerns, and worries about salmonella from raw egg. It notes that confusing labelling in the egg industry leads some consumers to avoid eggs altogether.

## Why It Matters

Two uses for us. In the vegan trials, the egg-free chocolate sponge with apple sauce worked as a traybake ([[vegan-bake-trials-log]]). In the [[school-bake-club]], egg allergy is the exclusion the school sends most often, so the scone session has an egg-free version ([[bake-club-session-plans]]). The allergy reason in the article's list is the one that matters most at our counter.
