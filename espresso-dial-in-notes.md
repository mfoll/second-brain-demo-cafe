# Espresso Dial-In Notes

Amira's one-page routine after the [[ombrel-barista-training-2025-11-25]], taped inside the cupboard above the grinders.

## Recipe

Estuary blend, 18.0 g in (weighed every time at dial-in, then by eye with spot checks), 38 g out, 28 to 30 seconds from pump on. Water 93 °C.

## When to dial in

- At opening, before the first customer: two test shots, tasted.
- After the 11:00 lull, when the hopper has been refilled.
- When a new bag is opened that is more than a week further from roast.

## What to change

Grind only. Fast and sour: finer by one step. Slow and bitter: coarser by one step. Never change dose to fix time ([[espresso-brew-ratio]]).

## Log

Write the grind setting and shot time in the notebook by the machine at each dial-in. Amira reads it weekly. Typical setting moves two or three steps across the life of a bag.

## Milk

Whole milk to 60 to 65 °C; oat to 55 to 60 °C, or it splits.
