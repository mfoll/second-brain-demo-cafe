# Ombrel Barista Training 2025-11-25

Dev Sandhu's half-day training for front of house, Tuesday 14:00 to 18:00 with the café closed early at 14:00 (signs up a week before).

## Who

Dev Sandhu; Amira Saleh, Lena Marsh, Finn Calloway, Grace Oyelaran; Odile for the first hour.

## What we covered

- Calibrating both grinders for Estuary and setting the new recipe: 18 g in, 38 g out, 28 to 30 seconds ([[espresso-brew-ratio]]).
- Tasting under- and over-extracted shots on purpose so everyone knows what sour and bitter mean on this coffee.
- Milk: steaming to 60 to 65 °C, not hotter; Dev was blunt about our flat whites being too hot.
- Daily dial-in routine at opening and after the 11:00 lull; written up by Amira in [[espresso-dial-in-notes]].
- A short session on pouring basic shapes ([[latte-art]]); Grace was the best of us.

## Cost

Included in the supply deal. Closing two hours early cost about 260 EUR in takings on a quiet Tuesday.

## Follow-up

Old blend finished on 2025-11-28 and Estuary on both grinders from then. Refresher with Dev in May 2026 (done 2026-05-19); next due late November 2026, when the newer weekend staff need the full session.
