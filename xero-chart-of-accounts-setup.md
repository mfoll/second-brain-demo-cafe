# Xero Chart Of Accounts Setup

The chart of accounts [[ruth-marchbank]] and I agreed for Xero on 2025-04-24, at the start of the [[bookkeeping-move-to-xero]].

## Summary

Xero's default chart, trimmed, with sales and purchases split so that gross margin can be read by menu section without a spreadsheet.

## Why It Matters

The split is what makes [[margin-by-menu-section-2025]] and the [[gross-profit-margin]] targets possible. Without it, the lunch cut debate in early 2026 would have been an argument about feelings.

## Key Details

- Sales accounts: Bakery counter, Savoury lunch, Drinks, Wholesale, Preorders and celebration cakes, Deposits received (a liability until the order is collected).
- Purchase accounts: Flour and grains, Dairy, Eggs, Fruit and veg, Chocolate and nuts, Coffee and tea, Other ingredients, Packaging.
- Square card fees in their own account, not netted off sales (the June 2025 mistake).
- Tracking category "Site" added in September 2026 with one value, Hollowgate, so a second site could be added without restructuring.
- Mixed purchases (one Glenvarrow invoice for butter used in bakery and lunch) are coded to the main use; Ruth accepts the error.

## Caveats

Food used for staff meals and donations is not separated; it sits in purchases.
