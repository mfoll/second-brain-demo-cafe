# Ombrel Roasters Wholesale Offer 2025

Dev Sandhu's emailed offer after the blind tasting, summarised. Accepted on 2025-10-24.

## Summary

Estuary blend at 24.50 EUR/kg in 1 kg bags, decaf at 26.00, delivered every Wednesday, roasted within the previous four days. Included: grinder calibration on day one, a half-day training session for all baristas, and a refresher every six months. No minimum term; 30 days' notice either way. No equipment loan.

## Why It Matters

The cost case for the [[coffee-roaster-change]]: about 420 EUR a month more than the old roaster at our 90 kg. The training clause was the deciding extra, because the old roaster's service was a technician, not a teacher.

## Key Details

- Price held for 12 months from first delivery (to 2026-11-28); Dev has said the 2027 price will move with green coffee costs.
- Minimum order 10 kg per delivery.
- Unused sealed bags can be returned within 14 days of roast.

## Caveats

We gave up the old roaster's free grinder servicing; burrs now cost us about 140 EUR a year.
