# Rota Principles

The rules Lena and I agreed when we moved the rota into Deputy in 2025, updated September 2026.

1. The rota for the next fortnight is published by Thursday 16:00.
2. Fixed days off stay fixed. People plan their lives on them.
3. Swaps go through Deputy and Lena approves them. A swap agreed by phone on the day is entered before the shift starts.
4. Nobody works more than five early shifts in a row, and nobody is rostered for an early shift after a closing shift.
5. Leave requests answered within a week. Summer leave requests by 31 March, Christmas leave is not available from 10 December to 24 December.
6. Overtime is offered first to whoever has had least of it this month, not to whoever always says yes.
7. Team meetings are rostered and paid.
8. Students' shifts follow their published timetable; exam weeks are requested by the start of term.

Rule 6 came from the summer 2025 export ([[summer-2025-early-shift-pressure]]): the person who always said yes was Kofi.
