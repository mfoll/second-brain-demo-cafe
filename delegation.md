# Delegation

English Wikipedia article on delegation.

## Summary

Defines delegation as handing work to another person. In management it means distributing work, decisions and responsibility to people in the organisation, which can create a clear chain of authority and responsibility. The article notes that poor delegation can lead to micromanagement.

## Why It Matters

Read after the May 2026 mentoring session, when Margot told me my 500 EUR sign-off limit on orders made me the bottleneck ([[mentor-sessions-with-margot]]). The micromanagement point landed. From July the limit went to 1,000 EUR and [[lena-marsh]] owns the ordering calendar; I have not had to overrule anything since.
