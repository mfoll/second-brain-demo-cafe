# Walk-In Chiller Failure 2026-01-27

At 04:35 on a Tuesday, Joel's opening check found the walk-in chiller at 9 °C. The compressor had stopped overnight.

## What happened

- Joel followed the sheet: probed the dairy and the cooked fillings. Butter and milk at 8 °C, fillings at 7 °C. He could not tell how long they had been warm, so the fillings (about 6 kg) went in the bin and were recorded.
- Butter and retarded dough moved into the two under-counter fridges and the café display, which Lena cleared at 07:00.
- Refrigeration engineer called at 05:10, on site 07:40; compressor replaced by 15:00 the next day ([[walk-in-chiller-repair-invoice-2026]]).
- For two days the savoury bakes were made without fillings made ahead; we sold a smaller range.

## What it showed

The sheet worked at 04:35 without anyone calling me first, which is the point of writing it for that hour ([[daily-food-safety-checks]]). Loss was about 140 EUR of food. The chiller went on the watch list in the [[equipment-register]], and I asked Petra whether stock spoilage cover would have paid (it would, above the excess, [[insurance-schedule-2026]]).
