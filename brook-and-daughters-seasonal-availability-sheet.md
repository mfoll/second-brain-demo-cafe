# Brook And Daughters Seasonal Availability Sheet

Stellan Brook's monthly sheet, emailed in the last week of each month since May 2026: what will be good in four to six weeks, rough prices, and what to avoid.

## Summary

One page, three columns: "good and cheap", "good but dear", "not yet / finished". The October 2026 sheet for November: pears, apples, squash, leeks and celeriac good and cheap; chestnuts and cranberries good but dear; plums finished.

## Why It Matters

The input that makes the [[seasonal-bake-calendar]] work. A fruit goes on the calendar only when it is in Stellan's first column and under the price in the [[recipe-costing-sheet]].

## Key Details

- Pears 2.40 EUR/kg (November), squash 1.60, leeks 2.10.
- The May sheet warned June strawberries would be 7.50 EUR/kg after a wet spring; we ran the tart anyway and it made 58% margin.

## Caveats

Prices are Stellan's estimates, typically within 10% of the invoice.
