# School Bake Club Risk Assessment

Our risk assessment for running bake club sessions in the Saltings Lane Primary food room. First written 30 September 2025, revised 21 September 2026 for the second year. The school has its own assessment for the room and supervision; ours covers what we bring and do.

## Key Details

| Hazard | Control |
|---|---|
| Burns from hot trays and oven | Adults only at the oven; trays cool 10 minutes on racks out of reach |
| Oven running hot | Probe check on arrival; set 20°C below recipe |
| Allergen exposure | School sends exclusions a week ahead; recipe checked against them; every ingredient labelled; nothing from outside the plan used |
| Cross-contamination from our basement | Dough carried in lidded, labelled tubs; tubs cleaned in the basement, not at school |
| Knives | Table knives only for children |
| Slips | Floor wiped during the session; aprons, no loose sleeves |
| Dough held too long out of the fridge | Chilled until 14:45; used within 2 hours |

## Why It Matters

It is what the school asked for before the first session and what [[running-a-bake-club-session]] is built on. The oven line was added after the first session, when a focaccia tray caught ([[school-bake-club-first-session-2025-10-08]]).
