# Should Christmas Deposits Be Refundable?

## Why It Matters

The 30% deposit is there to fund January and to stop no-shows. A strict rule protects both; a soft one keeps goodwill with regulars. Staff need one clear answer at the counter.

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> Deposits are fully refundable if the order is cancelled by 12 December, and not refundable after that. An uncollected order is kept until 14:00 on 24 December, then the products go on sale or to the Larder and the deposit is kept.

Same rule in 2025 and 2026. It is printed on the [[christmas-preorder-form-2026]].

## Reasons

- In 2025 only 7 of 214 orders cancelled before 12 December (refunds of 163 EUR) and 5 were not collected (deposits kept, 118 EUR) ([[christmas-2025-deposit-reconciliation]]). The refund window did not cost us much cash.
- Most deposits arrive in late November and early December, after the refund window narrows, so the January cash is safe either way.
- A no-refund rule from day one was rejected: Lena expected arguments at the counter in October, when people change plans.

## Caveat

Refunds go back to the card through Square and show in the forecast week they happen; Lena records them on the tracker.
