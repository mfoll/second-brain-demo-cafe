# Food contaminant

## Summary

A short Wikipedia article defining a food contaminant as a harmful chemical or microorganism present in food that can make the consumer ill, with pointers to the main kinds of contamination.

## Why It Matters

Priyanka and I looked it up in October 2025 while arguing about wording for the "may contain" lines ([[do-we-need-may-contain-labels]]). It helped us keep two ideas apart: contamination in this sense is about something harmful to everyone, while an allergen that gets into a product is harmless to most people and dangerous to a few. That is why our labelling treats allergen carry-over as an information problem for the customer, handled through the [[allergen-matrix]] and [[precautionary-allergen-labelling]], not as a hazard we claim to remove.
