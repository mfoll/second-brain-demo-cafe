# April 2026

The month I undid half of a decision and dropped a shop. Both were the right calls, and both came from numbers I should have looked for earlier.

## Lunch, partly restored

The six-week [[menu-review-2026-04-09|menu review]] showed that cutting lunch to bakery only had cost us more in midday coffee, cake and goodwill than it saved. We brought back soup and two toasties, made by Mateus, and kept the plated hot dishes off. They went on sale on 21 April, the first trading day after the Monday change, and the soup sold out before two o'clock. This partly reverses the February decision ([[team-meeting-2026-02-05|5 February]]); the whole story is in the [[savoury-lunch-trim|lunch trim project]]. See [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-04-09|9 April]].

## Tanner's Row dropped, the hall stays

The flue and extraction quote made Tanner's Row more expensive than unit 7 before a single bake, so I let it go on 16 April ([[why-did-we-drop-tanners-row|why]]). The second-site work now runs entirely through the [[saltings-hall-feasibility|Saltings Hall study]], and Priscilla sent the hall's tenant mix early in the month. See [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-04-16|16 April]].

## Money and paperwork

The 2025 accounts were signed on 22 April ([[marchbank-ochoa-year-end-accounts-2025|year-end accounts]]), the engagement letter with Marchbank Ochoa was renewed, and the new insurance year started on 1 April. Croissant takings were up on last year after the March price rise even with slightly fewer sold.

## Carried into May

The four-week lunch check, the three-scenario model for unit 7, and the seasonal bake calendar planning on 4 May.
