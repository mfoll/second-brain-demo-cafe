# Wednesday 30 September 2026

## The second-site review

I went into the [[second-site-review-2026-09-30|review]] meaning to decide on unit 7 and came out without a decision. Ruth, Kofi and Lena were there after close; Margot could not come.

Ruth thinks the unit looks reasonable and the Pellow Mutual offer is fairly priced. Her objection is timing: in the low case Hollowgate would carry the unit's losses through its first winter. She wants us to hold three months of fixed costs and payroll in cash first, and we hold well under half of that ([[cash-buffer-calculation-2026-09|the buffer calculation]]). A bigger loan does not help, it makes the low case worse.

Kofi repeated what he told me last week: no 03:00 starts to bake for two sites from our basement. He is not against a second site, only against building it on the early shift. Lena backed him and added the Saturday rota worry for a seven-day hall unit.

## The decision not to decide

Deferred to 15 December, four days before the option on unit 7 runs out. Two things have to move before then: the buffer and a production plan from the bakers. The open question and its conditions are in [[should-we-open-a-second-site|should we open a second site]]; the project is [[second-site-decision|Second Site Decision]].

## Afterwards

More relieved than disappointed, which I am trying to take seriously. Wrote [[baker-hours-limit-growth|an opinion note]] in the evening.
