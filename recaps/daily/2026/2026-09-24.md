# Thursday 24 September 2026

## Kofi on central production

Half an hour with Kofi in the basement after the morning bake, the day after his [[kofi-email-on-central-production|email]]. He agrees with the oven numbers in the [[central-production-capacity-study|capacity study]]; his problem is the morning, not the oven. Baking for unit 7 from here on the current pattern means 03:00 starts, and he will not do that, will not ask Joel to, and certainly will not ask Hana a month into her job. The conversation is in the [[central-production-talk-with-kofi-2026-09-24|meeting note]].

## Where he left a door open

An afternoon shift that mixes, shapes and retards dough for the next morning could work, if it is a new baker and the morning team bakes off rather than starting from scratch. He wants the bakers to propose the pattern themselves rather than react to my draft. Fair. I should have asked them before drafting [[central-production-shift-pattern-options|the options]] at all.

## What we agreed

Kofi, Joel and Hana bring a pattern by 16 November. Kofi says his piece at the review next Wednesday in his own words. Nothing goes to the wider team yet. This is now the second condition on the [[central-production-kitchen|central production idea]], next to the money.
