# Thursday 9 April 2026

## Soup comes back

Today we partly reversed the lunch cut I made at the [[team-meeting-2026-02-05|team meeting of 5 February]]. At the six-week [[menu-review-2026-04-09|menu review]] the Square figures showed weekday takings between 11:30 and 14:30 down by about a fifth, with coffee and cake falling alongside lunch, exactly as Lena had said they would. The [[lunch-takings-six-week-review-2026-04|six-week review]] has the table. What we saved on cook hours and lunch food did not make up for what we lost, and regulars kept asking for the soup ([[lunch-feedback-from-regulars|what they said]]).

## What we decided

Soup and two toasties return on Monday 20 April, made by Mateus, who asked to do it. Shakshuka, the quiche plate and the salad bowls stay off: they need a cook at the hob and had the worst margins. We did not go back to nine items, and we did not stay at bakery only. The reasoning in short is in [[why-did-we-bring-soup-back-after-the-lunch-cut|the answered question]].

## What I owe Lena

She was right about the coffee and the cake, and I should have tested her smaller menu before cutting everything. I told her so after the meeting. Dominic had already left on 27 March, which is the part I cannot undo.
