# Thursday 16 April 2026

## Letting Tanner's Row go

Rang Simone Ward in the morning to say we will not take the Tanner's Row shop, and confirmed it by email. The flue quote settled it: once [[tanners-row-extraction-and-flue-quote|extraction and a flue]] were added, the shop cost more than unit 7 at the hall before we had baked anything, with a planning risk on top. The reasons are in [[why-did-we-drop-tanners-row|the answered question]] and the day itself in [[tanners-row-dropped-2026-04-16|this note]]. I marked the [[tanners-row-site|Tanner's Row project]] as superseded by the [[saltings-hall-feasibility|Saltings Hall feasibility study]].

## The lesson

I should have asked about previous food use and extraction before the viewing. It is now the first line of my site checklist, and I used it straight away on the list of [[things-to-ask-priscilla-about-the-hall|questions for Priscilla]].
