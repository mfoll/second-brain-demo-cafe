# Thursday 5 February 2026

## Cutting the lunch menu

At the team meeting after close I put the January lunch figures on the table and decided to take the savoury lunch down to bakery only from 23 February. Lunch runs well below the bakery on margin and ties up a cook for 30 hours a week; the numbers are in the [[lunch-margin-analysis-2026-01|January analysis]] and the decision, with everyone's objections, is in the [[team-meeting-2026-02-05|meeting note]].

## Who disagreed

Lena disagreed and said so clearly: lunch customers also buy the coffee and the cake, and she asked for a trial with a smaller menu rather than nothing. Dominic said this was his job being cut, which it is. I offered him counter and porter hours and he will answer next week. Kofi was for it; Amira was worried about the midday coffee trade. I made the decision against Lena's advice and wrote that down so I cannot pretend otherwise later.

## What happens next

Lena prepares the [[lunch-cut-staff-briefing|staff briefing]] and the answer for customers. Kofi and Priyanka add two savoury buns to the morning bake. We look at takings between 11:30 and 14:30 again after six weeks, from the Square data. The work sits in the [[savoury-lunch-trim|lunch trim project]].

## How I feel about it

Uneasy. Right on the numbers, maybe wrong on the people.
