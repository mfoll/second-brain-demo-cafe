# Tuesday 29 July 2025

## The oven

Kofi rang at five to six: the bottom deck of the old oven had burned out mid-bake. He moved everything to the top deck and we opened on time with half the bread and no baguettes until half nine. Colm came at 10:30 and confirmed the controller is no longer made. The details of the morning are in the [[deck-oven-failure-2025-07-29|failure note]].

## What I decided

Not to nurse the old oven any longer. By lunchtime I had opened the [[deck-oven-replacement|replacement project]], asked Colm for names of suppliers, and started looking for an oven to hire in the meantime. Savoury bakes go into the small convection oven in the café kitchen until then.

## The wholesale customers

Rang Nadia and Ewan at seven to say they would get half their order. Both were kind about it. Tonight I am less sure than I was in June that we can keep [[wholesale-to-cafes|wholesale]] going on one deck and a stretched early shift; nothing decided, but I wrote the worry down.

## The VAT return

Yesterday's [[first-xero-vat-return-2025-07-28|first VAT return from Xero]] went through without a problem, which felt like a long time ago by 06:00 this morning.
