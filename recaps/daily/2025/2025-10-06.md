# Monday 6 October 2025

## Pausing wholesale

Spent the morning in the basement with Kofi and Lena going through September's Deputy hours and the [[wholesale-margin-workbook-2025|wholesale margin workbook]]. The bakers are working too many hours to bake for two other cafés on a hired oven, and the margin was never at our 35% target anyway. We looked at raising prices and at dropping one customer; neither fixes the hours. I decided to pause both from 20 October and look again once the new oven is in. The options and the reasoning are in the [[wholesale-pause-decision-2025-10-06|decision note]], and the short answer for anyone who asks later is [[why-did-we-pause-wholesale-to-cafes|here]].

## Telling Nadia and Ewan

Wrote the [[wholesale-pause-letter-2025-10-06|letters]] in the afternoon and phoned them both. Nadia was disappointed but not surprised. Ewan asked whether he could still buy at retail on Saturdays, which of course he can.

## Kofi

He asked for the freed hours to go into a proper break and an earlier finish, not new products. Agreed.

## Course

Missed the finance course tonight, the first one I have missed. Too tired after all that.
