# Wednesday 18 June 2025

## The inspection

Imogen Hale came unannounced in the middle of the morning rush and we came out of it with a 4, not the 5 we have had since 2022. The food itself was fine. We lost the point on two things she could see with her own eyes: no cooling records for the cooked fillings we started in March, and the basement hand-wash sink hidden behind that morning's flour delivery. Everything she said, and the action plan we agreed before she left, is in the [[environmental-health-inspection-2025-06-18|inspection note]].

## What I did in the afternoon

Cleared the sink and taped the yellow line on the floor before closing. Talked to Kofi on the phone about the cooling log; he will start filling it from tomorrow on a draft sheet until the proper [[cooling-log-sheet|cooling log]] is printed. Ordered racking for the dry store so a full delivery never sits on the floor again.

## What I keep thinking about

The spring rewrite of the [[food-safety-management-plan|food safety plan]] named cooling as a control point. I just never put it on the sheet people fill in at 05:00. Lena thinks the score is harsh; I think it is fair and said so in [[a-4-was-a-fair-score|a note to myself]]. Re-rating can be asked for after three months of clean records.
