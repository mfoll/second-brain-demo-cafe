# Should We Sell Ombrel Beans Retail?

## Why It Matters

Customers ask what coffee we use and where to buy it about twice a week. A shelf of 250 g bags could add some easy margin and a Christmas gift line.

## Current Answer

Open. Dev offered 250 g retail bags at 6.80 EUR to us, recommended retail 10.50; that is about 35% gross margin, half our usual. On about 15 bags a week it would be roughly 55 EUR a week of margin, plus shelf space by the till we do not really have.

## What Would Settle It

- A four-week trial in December with the Christmas preorders, 60 bags, to see real demand.
- Whether Dev would sell us bags at a better price for a minimum of 40 a month.
- Where the shelf would go without blocking the queue.

Owner: Odile, with Amira. Revisit after Christmas.
