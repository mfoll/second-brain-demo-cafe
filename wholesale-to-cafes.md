# Wholesale To Cafés

Daily bread for two other cafés in town, [[nadia-volkova]]'s Pier Street Coffee and [[ewan-macrae]]'s Lantern Books café. Started in June 2025 as a first step towards growth without a second shop; paused since 20 October 2025.

## Outcome

Daily bread for Pier Street Coffee and Lantern Books café at 35% gross margin or better; paused since 2025-10-20.

## Current State

- Paused since 2025-10-20. No deliveries, no current actions.
- While it ran (agreements from 2025-06-02; collections 2025-06-03 to 2025-10-18): about 60 loaves a day, Tuesday to Saturday; Pier Street about 35, Lantern about 25. Gross margin averaged 33%, below the 35% target ([[wholesale-margin-workbook-2025]]).
- Both customers took the pause well and still buy from us occasionally at retail. Nadia asked about resuming in May 2026; I said not before the second-site decision ([[wholesale-resumption-request-2026-05-12]]).
- Resume condition: a decision on the [[central-production-kitchen]] that gives the basement enough baker hours, and wholesale prices that clear 35% at 2026 ingredient costs.

## Scope and Boundaries

Bread only: white sourdough, seeded rye and baguettes. We never wholesaled pastries: they do not travel, and the croissant margin depends on selling at our own counter. Customers collected from the basement door between 06:45 and 07:15; we never ran deliveries. The supply terms are in [[wholesale-supply-agreement-pier-street-coffee]] and [[wholesale-supply-agreement-lantern-books]].

## Working Context

Why we started: in spring 2025 the basement had spare oven time after the 07:00 bake and Kofi wanted to grow the bread side. Wholesale looked like growth with no new premises. Prices were set at about 63% of retail (white sourdough 2.90 EUR against 4.60 EUR in the shop).

Why we paused, in one line: when the old deck oven failed on 29 July 2025 we baked on a hired oven with less capacity until the new one came on 14 November, and the 15 or so baker hours a week that wholesale took were the hours the shop needed. The full answer, with the numbers, is in [[why-did-we-pause-wholesale-to-cafes]].

What the pause did not fix: the new oven restored capacity but not baker hours. [[kofi-asare]] averaged 47 hours a week in summer 2025, and the assistant baker hire in August 2026 was meant to bring the early shift under 42 hours, not to add a product line.

The lesson I took, written as a stance: [[wholesale-only-at-the-target-margin]]. The price floor method is [[wholesale-pricing-floor]].
