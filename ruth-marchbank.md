# Ruth Marchbank

Partner at Marchbank Ochoa Accountants and the bakehouse's accountant since 2019. She does the year-end accounts, reviews the VAT returns twice a year and gives tax advice. She is the most cautious voice I listen to about money, and usually the right one.

## How We Work Together

- Engagement letter renewed each April ([[marchbank-ochoa-engagement-letter]]): year-end accounts, corporation tax, two VAT reviews a year, and up to six hours of ad hoc advice.
- Adviser access to Xero since the [[bookkeeping-move-to-xero]]. She prefers a short email with the question and the numbers, then a call. She does not like being shown a decision already made.
- Year-end meeting each March ([[year-end-meeting-with-ruth-2026-03-18]]).
