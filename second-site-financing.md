# Second Site Financing

Find a way to pay for the 120,000 EUR fit-out and working capital of unit 7 without putting Hollowgate at risk.

## Outcome

A financing option chosen for the 120,000 EUR fit-out and working capital.

## Current State

- Offer on the table: Pellow Mutual, 85,000 EUR over five years at 7.9% fixed, received 2026-09-08, valid to 2026-12-08 ([[pellow-mutual-second-site-loan-offer]]). Repayments about 1,720 EUR a month.
- The remaining 35,000 EUR would come from our own cash. We hold 41,000 EUR set aside, so taking the loan would leave almost no buffer.
- [[ruth-marchbank]] advised at the [[second-site-review-2026-09-30]] to wait until the bakehouse holds a three-month buffer of about 95,000 EUR, independent of the second site ([[cash-buffer-calculation-2026-09]]).
- No option chosen. The project waits on [[should-we-open-a-second-site]].

## Scope and Boundaries

How the money would be raised and what it does to the Hollowgate forecast. Not whether to open; that is the umbrella [[second-site-decision]]. Odile's personal money is out of scope; the company borrows or it does not.

## Working Context

Options considered so far:

| Option | Amount | Cost | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pellow Mutual term loan | 85,000 EUR | 7.9%, 5 years, debenture | Offered |
| Equipment lease for ovens, coffee machine and fridges | about 32,000 EUR | about 9% equivalent, 5 years | Quote requested from the oven lessor |
| Own cash | 35,000-41,000 EUR | Leaves no buffer | Available |
| Delay a year and save | 0 | Lose unit 7 | Fallback |

The loan conditions came by email from [[devon-pryce]] ([[pellow-mutual-loan-conditions-email]]): a debenture over company assets, our own contribution of at least 25%, and quarterly management accounts. No personal guarantee was asked for, which matters to me.

How it sits in the forecast: I run a second tab in the [[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]] with the loan drawn in March 2027, fit-out paid March-April 2027 and the unit trading from late April 2027, using the low case from [[saltings-hall-three-scenario-model]]. In that tab Hollowgate's end-of-week balance drops below the 15,000 EUR floor in January 2028. That is the picture Ruth reacted to.
