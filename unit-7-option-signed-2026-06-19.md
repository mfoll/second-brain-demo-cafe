# Unit 7 Option Signed 2026-06-19

Signed a six-month option on unit 7 at Saltings Market Hall, holding it for us until 2026-12-19, for a 2,900 EUR deposit.

## Why An Option And Not A Lease

The feasibility study was nearly done and the base case looked workable, but the financing and the production question were not. [[priscilla-adomako]] had told me another trader had asked about unit 7. An option kept the choice open for six months at a small, mostly refundable cost (500 EUR is lost if we withdraw in time).

[[ruth-marchbank]] agreed by phone the day before: "an option is cheap, a lease is not."

## Terms

Recorded in [[saltings-market-hall-unit-7]]: deposit credited against the lease deposit if we sign, refundable minus 500 EUR if we withdraw by 2026-12-19, lost after that.

## Afterwards

Put 2026-12-19 in the forecast, the calendar and on the fridge in the office. The decision date of 2026-12-15 later came from working backwards from it ([[second-site-review-2026-09-30]]).
