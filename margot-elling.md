# Margot Elling

Retired hotelier who ran two hotels and a restaurant group. My business mentor through the Brackenmouth Traders' Association mentoring scheme since autumn 2024.

## How We Work Together

- Coffee once a month at Hollowgate, Monday mornings when the shop is closed, about 90 minutes. No agenda; I bring one problem.
- She never tells me what to do. She asks what I would advise another owner, then asks whether I would take my own advice.
- Unpaid; I give her a box of viennoiserie, which she says is the only fee she accepts.

## Current Threads

- The second site. Her question at our 2026-10-06 session was whether I want a second site or a second Priyanka and a second Amira, meaning more responsibility for the team without a new lease ([[mentor-session-margot-2026-10-06]]).
- Growth and the bakers' hours: she was blunt that her second hotel nearly broke her head chef ([[baker-hours-limit-growth]]).
