# Seasonal Calendar Planning 2026-05-04

Monday planning session with Priyanka, 10:00 to 13:00, with Stellan Brook on the phone for the first half hour.

## Agreed rules

1. A fruit goes on the calendar only when it is in season and its price is under the line in the [[recipe-costing-sheet]].
2. Every special uses at least one ingredient we already stock, so a slow week does not leave odd stock.
3. Every special is test-baked on a Saturday and sold as "new" before it earns a calendar slot.
4. Plan four weeks ahead, on the first Monday of the month ([[how-far-ahead-should-we-plan-specials]]).

## What we filled in

June to September: strawberry tart and elderflower bun; apricot Danish and courgette and feta scone; blackberry and apple cake and tomato focaccia; plum and frangipane tart and apple and cheddar scone. Soup rota for Mateus aligned with Stellan's vegetables. Sheet: [[seasonal-bake-calendar-sheet]].

## Disagreement

Priyanka wanted three specials a month. I held at two: each extra line is another tray to waste at 15:30. We said we would look again after the summer; we did not change it.

## Stellan's warning

June strawberries would be dear after the wet spring. We kept the tart and it made 61% for the month; the note for 2027 is in [[seasonal-bake-calendar]].
