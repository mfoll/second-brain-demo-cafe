# Break-even point

## Summary

Describes the break-even point as the level at which a firm's costs and revenues are equal, so it makes neither profit nor loss, and notes that economists use a wider sense that also covers the return investors could get elsewhere.

## Why It Matters

Background for the [[saltings-hall-feasibility]]. The textbook gives a turnover level; for unit 7 I needed a date, which is why I use the [[break-even-month]] instead. Ruth's point that the owner's time and the cash tied up have a cost is the economists' sense in the article.

## Caveats

Static: it does not deal with opening costs paid back over time, which is the main question for a new site.
