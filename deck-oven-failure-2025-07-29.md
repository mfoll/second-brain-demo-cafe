# Deck Oven Failure 2025-07-29

The bottom deck of the old oven died at 05:50 on a Tuesday, halfway through the first sourdough bake. Kofi smelt burning, shut it down and called me at 05:55.

## What happened

- The bottom deck controller burned out. The top deck still worked.
- Kofi moved the bread to the top deck and baked in four rounds instead of two. The shop opened on time with six loaves instead of eleven and no baguettes until 09:30.
- Wholesale: Pier Street Coffee and Lantern Books got half their order that day; I phoned Nadia and Ewan at 07:00.
- [[colm-ashworth]] came at 10:30 and confirmed the controller was no longer made. A second-hand part might turn up in weeks, or not.

## Decisions that day

- Do not try to keep the old oven alive; replace it ([[deck-oven-replacement]]).
- Hire an oven as soon as one can be delivered (it came on 2025-08-06, [[oven-hire-agreement-2025]]).
- Until then: reduced bread range, viennoiserie in the top deck after bread, savoury bakes moved to the café kitchen's small convection oven.

## Afterwards

The week on one deck pushed Kofi to 54.5 hours, his highest week of the summer ([[deputy-timesheet-export-summer-2025]]). The failure is also where the [[wholesale-to-cafes]] pause started, though the decision came in October ([[why-did-we-pause-wholesale-to-cafes]]). Notes on what I would do differently: [[lessons-from-the-oven-failure]].
