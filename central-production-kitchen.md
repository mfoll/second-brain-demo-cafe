# Central Production Kitchen

The idea that the Hollowgate basement bakery bakes for both Hollowgate and a second site, and possibly for wholesale again.

## Outcome

Decide whether the Hollowgate basement bakery can bake for both sites, and on what shift pattern.

## Current State

- Idea. Nothing tested in practice.
- Oven capacity looks sufficient since the new three-deck oven ([[central-production-capacity-study]]): the oven is idle after about 11:00 and could take a second bake.
- Labour is the problem. Baking unit 7's morning bread and viennoiserie on top of Hollowgate's means either a 03:00 start or a second shift. [[kofi-asare]] objected to 03:00 starts in writing ([[kofi-email-on-central-production]]) and at the [[second-site-review-2026-09-30]].
- Kofi averaged 47 hours a week in summer 2025; [[hana-kobayashi]] joined on 2026-08-24 to bring that under 42. Central production would undo that.

## Scope and Boundaries

Production only: ovens, shifts, transport between sites, and what it would mean for [[wholesale-to-cafes]]. Not the staffing of the second site's counter ([[second-site-staffing-plan]]). Any shift change goes through [[lena-marsh]]'s rota and is discussed with the bakers before it is costed, not after.

## Working Context

The shift options are in [[central-production-shift-pattern-options]]. Short version:

- A: everything earlier, 03:00 start. Rejected by Kofi; I agree it is not fair to ask it.
- B: an afternoon shift 12:00-20:00 bakes retarded doughs and shapes viennoiserie for the next morning; the early shift only bakes off. Needs one more baker and a second retarder (about 6,500 EUR).
- C: unit 7 finishes viennoiserie on site in a small convection oven from frozen pieces made at Hollowgate; only bread travels. Needs freezer space we do not have.

[[anselm-ferreira]] ran the hotel pastry kitchen with a split day and night team; I asked him in October how he organised the handovers. His answer favoured B, with a written handover sheet.
