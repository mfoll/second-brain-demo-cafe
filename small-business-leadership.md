# Small Business Leadership

Exploration cluster for how I run a team of nine without becoming either the bottleneck or absent. Most of this comes from mentoring with [[margot-elling]], from reading, and from mistakes.

## Threads

- Matching how much direction to give to how experienced someone is: [[situational-leadership-theory]] gave me words for why Kofi needs almost none and Hana a lot.
- Listening: the [[staff-pulse-survey-2026-05]] (anonymous, aggregate only) said people want to hear about big decisions before customers do. That came straight back at the lunch cut ([[team-meeting-2026-02-05]]).
- Early starts and long weeks: the early shift is the hardest part of the job ([[team-culture]]).
