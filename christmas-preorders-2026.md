# Christmas Preorders 2026

Christmas preorders for collection on 23 and 24 December 2026, with a 30% deposit at ordering. It is a community job (most orders come from regulars) and a cash job: the deposits carry us through the January dip.

## Outcome

320 Christmas preorders taken with 30% deposits by 2026-12-12 (214 in 2025), collected on 23 and 24 December without overtime beyond what is on the December rota.

## Current State

- Orders opened on 1 October 2026. 61 orders and 1,452 EUR of deposits by 22 October ([[christmas-preorder-tracker-2026]]). Last year we had 38 by the same date.
- Products and prices are on [[christmas-preorder-form-2026]]: stollen 24.00 EUR, bûche de Noël (8 portions) 34.00 EUR, mince pies box of 6 13.50 EUR, fruited Christmas sourdough 9.50 EUR, gingerbread tin 16.00 EUR. Prices rose about 6% on 2025, mostly butter ([[butter-price-tracker]]).
- Capacity is the open risk. [[kofi-asare]] thinks 320 is the ceiling for the basement with the new oven and [[hana-kobayashi]] on the early shift; stollen is the bottleneck ([[how-many-christmas-preorders-can-the-basement-bake]]).
- Deposits are 30%, non-refundable after 12 December, refundable before ([[should-christmas-deposits-be-refundable]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

Covers the order form, the counter process, the production plan for 21 to 24 December, collection days and the deposit money. Order details (names, phone numbers) live only on the paper forms and in Square; the tracker holds counts and totals by product. Not covered: the Christmas menu in the café (that is [[seasonal-bake-calendar]]).

## Working Context

Why deposits. In January 2025 the VAT payment and an oven repair arrived together and we sat on the full 6,000 EUR overdraft for 19 days. For Christmas 2025 I took 30% deposits on 214 preorders, about 4,900 EUR, which covered most of the January 2026 dip; the overdraft was not used ([[christmas-2025-preorder-debrief]], [[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]]). The idea behind it is in [[customer-deposits-as-working-capital]].

Why 320. 2025 orders grew from 151 in 2024 and we turned people away on 19 December. 320 would bring in about 7,400 EUR of deposits at the 2025 average order of 76 EUR plus the price rise. [[ruth-marchbank]] asked me not to count on more than 5,000 EUR in the forecast until the orders are in.

What 2025 taught us ([[christmas-2025-preorder-debrief]]): collection slots by half hour stopped the queue on the 24th; five orders were not collected; the bûche moulds were the real limit on the 23rd. This year we bought 10 more moulds (86 EUR).
