# Food Safety Management Plan

Our written food safety management plan, built on HACCP principles. Version 3 was signed off on 2025-04-11 at the end of the [[haccp-plan-rewrite]]; version 3.1 of 2025-07-11 is current and followed the [[environmental-health-inspection-2025-06-18]].

## Summary

A 22-page plan in two parts. Part one is the hazard analysis for each product family and the four critical control points with their limits, monitoring and corrective actions. Part two covers the prerequisites: cleaning, pest control, personal hygiene and hand washing, allergen control, supplier approval, training, and stock rotation. The daily records that prove the plan is followed are the sheets in [[daily-food-safety-checks]].

## Why It Matters

This is the document an environmental health officer asks for first, and the one Kofi, Joel and Mateus actually work from. Version 3 replaced a bought template from 2021 ([[food-safety-management-plan-version-2]]) that described a bakery we no longer were. The difference between 3 and 3.1 is the lesson of 2025: a control written into the plan but missing from the sheet does not exist for an inspector.

## Key Details

Critical control points and limits (our own, set during the rewrite):

| CCP | Step | Critical limit | Monitoring | Corrective action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Receipt of chilled goods | At or below 8 °C on arrival | Probe one item per delivery, record | Reject delivery, tell supplier, record |
| 2 | Cooking high-risk fillings and lunch chicken | Core at least 75 °C | Probe each batch, record | Keep cooking until reached |
| 3 | Cooling cooked fillings, soup | Below 20 °C within 2 h, below 5 °C within a further 4 h | Time and core temperature at end of cook, 2 h, 6 h | Discard if either stage missed |
| 4 | Cold holding: fridges and display | Fridges at or below 5 °C; display at or below 8 °C | Opening and closing readings | Move stock; discard high-risk items above limit for over 2 h |

Version 3.1 changes, 2025-07-11:

- CCP 3 moved onto the daily sheet with its own section ([[cooling-log-sheet]]); before that it existed only in the plan.
- Prerequisite added: nothing stored within one metre of a hand-wash sink; floor marking in yellow.
- Sunday cleaning sign-off moved into the closing check.
- Reheating of soup (added 2026-04-20 as an annex, not a new version): core at least 75 °C, reheat once only.

Prerequisites worth knowing:

- Allergen control is a prerequisite, not a CCP, because no single step removes an allergen. It points to the [[allergen-matrix]].
- Stock rotation by first in, first out with day dot labels ([[first-in-first-out]]).
- Pest control every eight weeks by contract ([[pest-control-service-reports]]).
- Probe thermometers calibrated quarterly in boiling and iced water.

## Caveats
