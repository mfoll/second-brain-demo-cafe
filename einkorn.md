# Einkorn

English Wikipedia article on einkorn wheat.

## Summary

Einkorn names both a wild wheat and its domesticated form. It is a diploid hulled wheat whose husks hold the grain tightly; the name refers to each spikelet containing a single grain. It was one of the first wheats to be cultivated.

## Why It Matters

Read before the second tasting in April 2026, where we tried 20% einkorn in a white loaf. The hulled grain and one grain per spikelet go some way to explaining why it is the dearest flour on the Tarnwick list (3.40 EUR/kg, minimum two sacks; [[tarnwick-heritage-flour-sample-list]]). Joel liked the loaf; Kofi disliked the sticky dough. Not a candidate while the price is that high.

## Caveats

The article does not cover dough handling; that is our own observation.
