# Early Shift Overtime Review 2025-09-02

A Tuesday meeting at 12:45 with Kofi, Joel and Lena after the first quarter of Deputy timesheets came out ([[deputy-timesheet-export-summer-2025]]).

## Notes

- Kofi averaged 47 hours a week over June to August, Joel 41.5. Kofi's highest week was 54.5, the week of the oven failure.
- Causes as Kofi saw them: wholesale bread (60 loaves a day, about an hour of shaping and packing), the hired oven's longer bakes, and covering my absences in August.
- Options discussed: hire a third baker now, cut wholesale, cut the bread range, move Saturday to a later start.

## Decisions

- No hire yet: cash was tight with the oven and I wanted the new oven in first. With hindsight this was a mistake that cost Kofi another winter.
- Kofi to have one fixed day off a week from mid-September.
- Revisit wholesale if the hired oven stayed longer than October. It did, and wholesale was paused on 2025-10-20 ([[why-did-we-pause-wholesale-to-cafes]]).

The hire came eight months later ([[assistant-baker-hiring]]).
