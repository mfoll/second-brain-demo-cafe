# Management Accounts Monthly Pack

The four Xero reports I run on the first Monday of each month and save as one PDF. Latest: September 2026, run on 2026-10-05.

## Summary

Profit and loss for the month and year to date against last year, gross margin by sales account, aged payables, and the balance sheet. Ten minutes to run once the month is reconciled.

## Why It Matters

Pellow Mutual's loan conditions would require quarterly management accounts ([[pellow-mutual-loan-conditions-email]]); this pack already does it. It is also where the margin by menu section is read every month.

## Key Details

September 2026:

- Takings 52,900 EUR (September 2025: 51,600 EUR, with wholesale; like-for-like up 6%).
- Gross margin 68.6%, down from 69.8% in September 2025 because of butter at 8.90 EUR/kg ([[butter-price-tracker]]).
- Lunch section margin 58% since the soup and toasties returned, better than the 51% before the trim.
- Aged payables: nothing over 30 days.

## Caveats

Monthly margin moves with stock counts, which are rough; trust the quarter more than the month.
