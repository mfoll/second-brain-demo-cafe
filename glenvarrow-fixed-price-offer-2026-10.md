# Glenvarrow Fixed Price Offer 2026-10

Moira Fenwick's written offer, handed over at the meeting on 2026-10-06 ([[glenvarrow-meeting-2026-10-06]]) and confirmed by email the next day.

## Summary

Block butter (lamination) and dough butter at a fixed 8.40 EUR/kg from 2026-11-01 to 2027-04-30, against a commitment of 900 kg over the period. Volume above 900 kg at the same price; shortfall below 900 kg charged at 1.00 EUR/kg on the difference. Answer wanted by 2026-11-06.

## Why It Matters

The central document of the [[dairy-contract-renegotiation]]. Against September's 8.90 it is 0.50 EUR/kg cheaper, about 450 EUR over six months on 900 kg, if the monthly price stays where it is. If butter falls below 8.40 we pay over the market.

## Key Details

- Our usage November to April last year: about 1,020 kg, so 900 kg is safe even with a quiet spring.
- Milk and cream not included; they stay on the monthly list.
- Delivery days unchanged.

## Caveats

Moira would not say whether a 12-month fix was possible ("not this year"). The shortfall charge is new; she says other bakeries asked for the fix and then bought elsewhere.
