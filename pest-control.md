# Pest control

## Summary

Wikipedia's article on pest control: managing animals, plants or fungi that harm human activities, with responses ranging from tolerance and deterrence to eradication. It describes biological, physical and chemical methods and integrated pest management, which combines prevention, monitoring and targeted treatment rather than relying on poison alone.

## Why It Matters

Read in February 2025 after Callum Reid found a mouse in the basement dry store ([[pest-control-service-reports]]). The idea I took from integrated pest management was that proofing and housekeeping do most of the work: the fix was sealing a gap around a pipe and keeping stock off the floor and away from walls, not more bait. It is why the new racking in June 2025 stands 15 cm from the wall ([[basement-storage-changes-2025]]).

## Key Details

- Prevention and exclusion come first: block entry points, remove food and shelter.
- Monitoring shows whether a problem exists before treating it.
- Chemical control is targeted and a last step.
