# Glenvarrow Dairy Price Notices

The monthly price notices from Glenvarrow, filed together since January 2025. Fourteen notices so far that changed the butter price, two that changed milk.

## Summary

Each notice gives the new price per kg or litre, the start date (always the first of the month) and a short reason. Butter rose in 14 of 21 months; it never fell. Milk rose twice, by 4 cents a litre each time (April 2025, January 2026).

## Why It Matters

The paper trail behind the [[butter-price-tracker]]. Moira's reasons give context the numbers do not: feed costs in winter 2025, a dry summer, strong export demand for butterfat in 2026.

## Key Details

- Largest single rise: 2026-01-01, 7.90 to 8.30 EUR/kg.
- Notice period always a month, as agreed in 2019.
- The August 2026 notice (8.80 from 2026-08-01, 8.90 from 2026-09-01) triggered the [[dairy-contract-renegotiation]].

## Caveats

The reasons are Glenvarrow's account and not checked against any market data.
