# Food Loss And Waste

English Wikipedia article on food that is discarded or lost uneaten. I searched for "food waste" and it redirects here.

## Summary

The article treats loss and waste across the whole food system, from production and processing through distribution and retail to the consumer. Its lead says most waste happens after consumers have bought the food, and gives global figures: roughly a third of the world's food thrown away, about 931 million tonnes a year, split between households (61%), food service (26%) and retail (13%).

## Why It Matters

I read it in the first week of the [[end-of-day-waste-log]], in March 2025. What I took from it was simple: we are the food service and retail slices, and those are the parts a business can actually measure. It pushed me to log waste daily rather than guess, and to think about redistribution as well as baking less ([[community-larder-donation-agreement]]).

## Caveats

Global statistics, nothing specific to bakeries or to a shop of our size. The percentages are from the article's lead as read in 2026 and may change with edits.
