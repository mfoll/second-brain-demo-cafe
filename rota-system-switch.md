# Rota System Switch

Replacing the paper rota on the basement door with Deputy for all nine staff, so that shifts, swaps, leave and timesheets live in one place and payroll stops depending on Lena reading my handwriting.

## Outcome

Every shift published, swapped and clocked in Deputy, with timesheets exported to payroll each fortnight. Reached on 2025-05-30.

## Current State

- Completed 2025-05-30. All staff use the Deputy phone app; the kiosk tablet by the back door handles clock-in.
- Timesheets are approved by Lena on alternate Mondays and exported to the payroll file Marchbank Ochoa run for us.
- The paper rota template is kept only as a record ([[paper-rota-template]]).
- The biggest result was not planned: overtime became visible. Kofi averaged 47 hours a week over June to August 2025 ([[deputy-timesheet-export-summer-2025]]), which led to [[assistant-baker-hiring]].

## Scope and Boundaries

Scheduling, leave requests, clock-in and timesheets. Payroll itself stays with the accountants ([[manage-money-and-cash-flow]]). Individual pay rates are in Deputy and nowhere in this vault.

## Working Context

Before March 2025 Lena drew the rota on an A3 sheet every Thursday, photographed it into the staff group chat and kept hours in a notebook. Swaps happened by text and often never reached the sheet. Twice in early 2025 the café opened one person short on a Saturday because a swap was agreed but not written down.

We compared Deputy with one other scheduling app and with simply moving the paper rota to a shared spreadsheet ([[deputy-and-alternatives-comparison]]). Deputy won because the clock-in kiosk and leave approvals came in the base plan, and the export matched the payroll columns Marchbank Ochoa asked for. The spreadsheet option was free but fixed nothing about swaps.

Rollout ran in three steps: Lena and I built the shift templates in March, front of house went live on 2025-04-22, the bakery on 2025-05-13, and the paper rota came down on 2025-05-30 ([[deputy-go-live-2025-05-30]]). Kofi was the most sceptical; he started trusting it once it showed his real hours. The rules we agreed for publishing and swapping are in [[rota-principles]].

## Active Workflows

- Lena publishes the next fortnight by Thursday 16:00.
- Swaps go through the app and need Lena's approval; same-day swaps can be agreed by phone but must be entered before the shift.
- Fortnightly timesheet approval and export.
