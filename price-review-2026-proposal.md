# Price Review 2026 Proposal

The four-page proposal I wrote for Lena and Kofi on 2026-02-27, with the reasoning for each price change. Agreed with two changes on 2026-03-06.

## Summary

Target food cost by section (viennoiserie 27%, bread 25%, cakes 28%, coffee 20%), every product checked against it with February invoice prices, rounded to the nearest 0.05 EUR. Proposed rises on 43 items; 41 went ahead. Average rise weighted by units sold 6.8%.

## Why It Matters

The record of why each price is what it is, for the next review and for anyone asking at the counter. It is the bridge between the [[recipe-costing-sheet]] and the [[price-list-2026-03-09]].

## Key Details

- Croissant 2.60 to 2.95: food cost at 31% of the old price, 27% at the new.
- Flat white proposed at 3.60; Lena argued for 3.50 because the shop up the road charges 3.40. Agreed 3.50.
- Sourdough: Kofi wanted 5.50; kept at 5.20 as the loaf people compare with the supermarket.
- Two proposed rises dropped: the plain scone and the children's hot chocolate.

## Caveats

Written before the lunch cut's effect was known; lunch items were left out because they were off the menu.
