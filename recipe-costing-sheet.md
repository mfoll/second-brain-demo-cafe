# Recipe Costing Sheet

Every product we sell, costed from its recipe: ingredients by weight, the latest invoice price for each, packaging, food cost, food cost percentage and gross margin at the current selling price. Recosted for the 2026 price review and kept live since.

## Summary

> [!abstract]
> 63 products costed on one sheet with a shared ingredient price tab. In January 2026 bakery averaged about 71% gross margin and the nine-item lunch 51%; after the March prices and the lunch trim the counter averages 72% and the restored lunch 64%.

## Why It Matters

It is the basis for the [[price-review-2026]], the lunch decision at the [[team-meeting-2026-02-05]], the margin estimate for the restored lunch at the [[menu-review-2026-04-09]], and every special on the [[seasonal-bake-calendar]]. It is the one place where a butter rise turns into a croissant price.

## Key Details

Structure:

- **Ingredients tab:** one row per ingredient, price per kg or unit from the latest invoice, supplier, date updated. Butter links to the [[butter-price-tracker]].
- **Recipes tab:** one block per recipe, batch yield, waste allowance (5% for viennoiserie, 3% for bread, 8% for cakes), packaging.
- **Summary tab:** food cost, food cost % ex VAT, gross margin %, target and a red flag when a product is more than 3 points off its target.

## Caveats

- Labour is not in the food cost. The lunch decision needed the cook hours added by hand ([[lunch-margin-analysis-2026-01]]).
- Waste allowances are averages; the real waste by product is in the [[end-of-day-waste-log]].
- Prices update only when someone enters an invoice. Before the price review half the ingredient rows were more than three months old. Rule since then: follow [[recost-a-recipe]] when a key ingredient moves more than 5%.
