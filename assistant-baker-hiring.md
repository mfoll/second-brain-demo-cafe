# Assistant Baker Hiring

Hiring a third baker for the early shift so that Kofi Asare stops working 47-hour weeks and the bakery can cover holidays without me on the bench four mornings in a row.

## Outcome

One assistant baker hired for the early shift. Reached: Hana Kobayashi accepted on 2026-08-07 and started on 2026-08-24.

## Current State

- Completed 2026-08-21 with paperwork and induction booked. Hana works four early shifts a week, 04:30 to 12:30, Tuesday to Friday, plus one Saturday a month.
- Kofi's average week over September and October 2026 was 43.5 hours, down from 47 in summer 2025 but still over the 42-hour aim ([[can-we-get-the-head-baker-under-42-hours]]).
- Hana is trained on shaping, the mixer and the oven. She does not yet run the bake alone; that is planned for December 2026.

## Scope and Boundaries

The hire itself: budget, advert, interviews, trial shifts and induction. Ongoing rota decisions belong to [[run-daily-operations]]. Candidates' details are kept in the Deputy onboarding file and not in this vault.

## Working Context

The case came from the Deputy hours ([[deputy-timesheet-export-summer-2025]], [[summer-2025-early-shift-pressure]]) and from Kofi telling me at his review in May 2026 that he could not do another summer of six-day weeks. Budget: 30 hours a week at the baker rate, about 27,000 EUR a year with employer costs, checked against the cash forecast with Ruth in May.

The advert went out on 2026-06-08 ([[assistant-baker-job-advert-2026]]) through Brackenmouth College, the traders' association newsletter and two job boards. 23 applications; we interviewed five on 2026-07-14 and 2026-07-15 with Kofi on the panel ([[assistant-baker-interviews-2026-07-14]]) and ran two paid trial shifts from 04:30. We scored against the rubric ([[assistant-baker-interview-rubric]]); Kofi weighted the trial more than the interview, which was right.

We did not hire a fully qualified baker. Nobody qualified applied at what we can pay, and Kofi preferred someone keen who would learn his way. That trade-off means another four to six months before the hours fall as far as planned.
