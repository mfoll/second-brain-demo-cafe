# HACCP Plan Rewrite

Rewriting our food safety management plan from the 2021 binder into a plan built properly on HACCP principles, with critical limits we can actually measure at 05:00 and check sheets that fit on one page.

## Outcome

The food safety management plan rewritten as version 3 with new critical limits and daily check sheets. Signed off on 2025-04-11. The June inspection then showed a gap the rewrite missed, fixed in version 3.1 on 2025-07-11.

## Current State

- Completed 2025-04-11. Version 3.1 (2025-07-11) is current: [[food-safety-management-plan]].
- The daily sheets are in use and reviewed every Monday ([[daily-food-safety-checks]]).
- The weak point found afterwards: we had added cooked fillings (ham and leek, spinach and feta, the lunch chicken) in March 2025, and the plan named cooling as a step but the sheets had nowhere to record it. That cost a point on 2025-06-18 ([[environmental-health-inspection-2025-06-18]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

The written plan, its hazard analysis, the critical control points and the record sheets. Allergen labelling became its own project ([[allergen-labelling-overhaul]]). Premises repairs went to [[run-daily-operations]].

## Working Context

Version 2 ([[food-safety-management-plan-version-2]]) was a bought template filled in during 2021. It listed hazards for products we no longer make, had no cooling step, and nobody had read it since the 2022 inspection. In January 2025 I started again with Kofi, using the HACCP steps from the course binder and the background in [[hazard-analysis-critical-control-point]] and [[iso-22000]].

We mapped every product family on the basement wall (bread, viennoiserie, cakes, savoury bakes, lunch), walked each from delivery to the counter, and asked where a hazard could be removed or controlled. Four critical control points came out: chilled delivery, cooking of high-risk fillings, cooling, and cold holding. Everything else (cleaning, pest control, allergen segregation, hand washing, training) is a prerequisite with its own record ([[critical-control-point]]).

The limits we chose are ours, set from the course guidance and checked with [[imogen-hale]] informally in March: chilled deliveries at or below 8 °C, fillings cooked to a core of 75 °C, cooling from cooking to below 20 °C within 2 hours and below 5 °C within a further 4 hours ([[two-stage-cooling]]), fridges at or below 5 °C and display at or below 8 °C.

What I would do differently: test the sheets for a full fortnight before signing. The cooling line existed in the plan but the March pilot sheets came from the old binder.
