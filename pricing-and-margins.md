# Pricing And Margins

Where to look when the question is "are we making money on this?": a product, a menu section, a wholesale customer or a whole site. The working definitions live in [[gross-profit-margin]], [[prime-cost]] and [[fixed-and-variable-costs]]; the numbers live in the [[recipe-costing-sheet]], the [[butter-price-tracker]] and [[margin-by-menu-section-2025]].

## Targets I use

| Line | Target gross margin | Latest |
|---|---|---|
| Whole shop | 68% | 69.4% (2025) |
| Bakery counter | 70% | 71% |
| Savoury lunch | 60% | 51% before the trim |
| Coffee and drinks | 75% | 78% |
| Wholesale bread | 35% | 33% when paused |

## Main threads

- Ingredient rises: butter from 6.20 to 8.90 EUR/kg ([[butter-price-tracker]]) pushed the croissant food cost from 24% to 31%; the [[price-review-2026]] raised it from 2.60 to 2.95 EUR.
- The lunch reversal: margin was the reason for the cut at [[team-meeting-2026-02-05]], takings were the reason for bringing soup back at [[menu-review-2026-04-09]].
- Wholesale ran under target ([[why-did-we-pause-wholesale-to-cafes]]).
- A second site has to clear the same targets with less control: [[saltings-hall-three-scenario-model]].
