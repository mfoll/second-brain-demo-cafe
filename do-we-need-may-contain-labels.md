# Do We Need May Contain Labels

Should our labels carry "may contain" warnings, and if so, for what?

## Current Answer

Yes, but sparingly. We add a "may contain" line only when a supplier's specification declares it, or when we use an allergen in the same production step and cannot separate it (nuts on the pastry bench, sesame on the seeded loaves). We do not add a blanket "may contain all allergens" line: it tells customers nothing and stops people with allergies from buying anything. All labels and the counter folder say that we bake in one room with nuts, sesame, milk, eggs and gluten.

## Why

Agreed by Priyanka and me during the [[allergen-labelling-overhaul]] in October 2025, after reading on [[precautionary-allergen-labelling]] and [[food-allergy]]. The question returns whenever a new nut product comes in.

## Caveats

This is our policy, not a legal reading. If guidance from the council changes, it gets re-checked.
