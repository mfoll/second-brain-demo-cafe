# Should Pastry Flour Move To Tarnwick?

## Why It Matters

We buy from two flour suppliers for one reason: pastry flour stayed with the old wholesaler after the [[flour-supplier-review]]. One supplier would mean one order, one invoice and one relationship.

## Current Answer

Deferred to spring 2027. Priyanka's test on 2025-05-28 with Tarnwick's finer grade gave a tighter honeycomb and visible bran specks; that grade was also bought in by Tarnwick, not milled by them ([[tarnwick-mill-price-list-2025]]). Beatrix is looking at milling a lower-extraction pastry flour herself.

## What Would Revive It

- Tarnwick milling its own fine, low-extraction flour ([[flour-extraction-rate]]).
- The old wholesaler raising pastry flour above 1.00 EUR/kg (it is 0.90 now).
- A central production kitchen needing simpler ordering ([[central-production-kitchen]]).
