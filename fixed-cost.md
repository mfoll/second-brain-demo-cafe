# Fixed cost

## Summary

Defines fixed costs as expenses a business pays regardless of output, often recurring at set intervals such as rent or loan interest, and contrasts them with variable costs that move with production.

## Why It Matters

From week 9 of the [[food-business-finance-course]]. It is the basis of [[fixed-and-variable-costs]], and of the buffer rule, which counts only fixed costs and payroll ([[cash-buffer-calculation-2026-09]]).

## Caveats

In a café, payroll sits awkwardly between fixed and variable; the article does not settle that.
