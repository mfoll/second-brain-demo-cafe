# How Big A Cash Buffer Do We Need?

Before opening a second site, how much cash should Hollowgate hold, and is Ruth's three-month rule the right one for us?

## Why It Matters

It is the first of the two conditions on [[should-we-open-a-second-site]]. At about 95,000 EUR against 41,000 EUR held, it is the condition furthest from being met.

## Current Answer

[[ruth-marchbank]]'s rule: three months of payroll and fixed costs, including the second site's commitments, plus the overdraft amount, about 95,000 EUR ([[cash-buffer-calculation-2026-09]]). For Hollowgate alone, the weekly floor of 15,000 EUR has worked since 2025 ([[cash-buffer]]).

My doubts about the 95,000 EUR:

- It counts three months of the unit's rent, but the first three months are rent-free.
- It treats all payroll as fixed; in a real crisis I would cut hours.
- It ignores the loan being drawn in tranches.

Ruth's reply on the 2026-10-19 call ([[notes-from-ruth-call-2026-10-19]]): those points take it to perhaps 80,000 EUR, still double what we hold, and January is when buffers get tested.

## What Would Answer It

- A version with the equipment leased instead of bought, which keeps about 30,000 EUR more cash ([[second-site-financing]]).
- Agreement with Ruth on a number below 95,000 EUR I can defend, or acceptance of hers.
