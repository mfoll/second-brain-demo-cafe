# Food Safety Management Plan Version 2

The plan we used from 2021 until 2025-04-11, built from a bought template. Superseded by version 3 ([[food-safety-management-plan]]).

## Summary

A 40-page generic template with our name added. It listed hazards for products we had stopped making (fresh cream eclairs, a hot breakfast), had no step for cooling cooked food, and treated nine steps as critical control points, most with no measurable limit. Records were a separate fridge sheet and cleaning sheet.

## Why It Matters

Kept for history and because an inspector may ask what changed. Reading it again in January 2025 was the start of the [[haccp-plan-rewrite]]: nobody on the team could say which steps were critical or why.

## Source Location

Old green binder on the top shelf of the office, marked "superseded 2025-04-11".
