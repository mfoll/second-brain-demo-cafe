# Glenvarrow Meeting 2026-10-06

Moira Fenwick's autumn visit. A Tuesday at 15:00 in the office downstairs, an hour.

## At a Glance

> [!note]
> Glenvarrow offered a fixed 8.40 EUR/kg for butter from November 2026 to April 2027 against 900 kg, with a 1.00 EUR/kg charge on any shortfall and an answer wanted by 2026-11-06. I said I would take a second quote first; Moira said that was fair.

## Discussed

- Where butter is going: Moira expects 9.00 to 9.20 by spring on the monthly list, but would not promise.
- The fixed offer ([[glenvarrow-fixed-price-offer-2026-10]]). This is new for customers our size; she said several bakeries asked for it last winter.
- I showed her the [[butter-price-tracker]]: 6.20 to 8.90 in 21 months. She did not dispute it.
- 12-month fix: "not this year".
- Milk and cream unchanged for now.

## My notes afterwards

- 900 kg is below our November to April use last year (about 1,020 kg), so the commitment is safe.
- If the list price does go to 9.10, the fix saves about 630 EUR over six months; if butter falls back to 8.00, it costs us 360 EUR.
- Rang Harriet Lowe for names of other dairies; she gave me Nils Jorgensen at Coldbeck Creamery. Booked him for 2026-10-14 ([[second-dairy-tasting-2026-10-14]]).
- Decision framed in [[should-we-sign-the-fixed-price-butter-contract]].
