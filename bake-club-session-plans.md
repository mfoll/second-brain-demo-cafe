# Bake Club Session Plans

One product per session, chosen to bake in under 25 minutes in a domestic oven and to work around common allergen exclusions. Updated September 2026 for the second year.

| Month | Product | Allergens (base recipe) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| October | Focaccia | Gluten | Dough from the basement; dimple and top |
| November | Soda bread | Gluten, milk (buttermilk) | No yeast, so mixed at school; swap buttermilk for oat drink with lemon if milk is excluded |
| December | Bread rolls for the Larder's Christmas lunch | Gluten | If the date works with [[bernard-osei]] |
| January | Pizza bianca | Gluten, milk (mozzarella) | Cheese optional |
| February | Flatbreads with spelt | Gluten | The grains session, Tarnwick spelt ([[heritage-grains]]) |
| March | Scones | Gluten, milk, egg | Egg-free version tested in the pilot |
| May | Fruit buns | Gluten, milk, egg | Dough from the basement |
| June | Bake your own | Depends | Children choose from the year |

## Lessons from the pilot

- Laminated dough: never. Too slow, too much butter.
- Shortbread worked but needs 30 minutes' chilling; only if the dough comes ready.
- Children like shaping more than mixing. Give them dough, not flour.
- Cost about 38 EUR of ingredients a session.
