# Flour Extraction Rate

The share of the grain that ends up in the flour. Wholemeal is close to 100%; a white flour sifted from it keeps much less, depending on how much bran and germ the miller removes.

## Why I care

Beatrix explained it at the mill visit ([[tarnwick-mill-visit-2025-03-19]]): Tarnwick's stone-ground "white" keeps more of the fine bran and germ than roller-milled white. That is why it tastes of more, takes more water ([[dough-hydration]]) and ferments faster, and why it showed bran specks in Priyanka's croissant test.

## Practical rule

- Bread: a little higher extraction is a gain.
- Lamination: low extraction, very fine flour. That is why pastry flour stayed with the old wholesaler ([[should-pastry-flour-move-to-tarnwick]]).

## Caveat

Mills label extraction differently; ask for the batch sheet rather than trusting the sack name.
