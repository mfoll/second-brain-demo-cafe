# Mentor Session Margot 2026-10-06

Monthly coffee with [[margot-elling]] at Hollowgate, Tuesday, 10:00-11:30. The subject was the deferred second-site decision.

## What We Talked About

- I described the [[second-site-review-2026-09-30]] and said I was relieved not to decide. She asked why relief, and whether relief was information.
- Her question: do I want a second site, or more room for [[priyanka-desai]] and [[amira-saleh]] to grow? Those can be had without a lease: a pastry-led Sunday menu, Amira training other cafés' baristas, wholesale for cakes rather than bread.
- Her second hotel: opened on borrowed money in a good year, nearly closed in the next bad one, and her head chef left. She does not say "don't". She says open when the first business can lose money for a year without anyone noticing.

## What I Took Away

- Write down what "yes" requires, so the December decision is a check, not a mood ([[should-we-open-a-second-site]]).
- Ask Priyanka and Amira what they want before assuming a second site is the answer. Not done yet.
