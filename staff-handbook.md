# Staff Handbook

Our twelve-page handbook given to every new starter, version of March 2026.

## Summary

Opening hours and shift patterns; how the rota and leave work in Deputy; uniform and hygiene rules; allergens and what to say at the counter; food safety basics and where the sheets are; breaks and staff food (one drink and one item per shift); what to do if you are ill before a shift (tell the shift lead by phone, not message, and stay off food handling for 48 hours after vomiting or diarrhoea); team meetings; how to raise a concern.

## Why It Matters

It is what Hana read in her first week, and what I point to when a rule is questioned. It needs updating for the new oven shutdown and the current Deputy leave rules, which is on the [[run-daily-operations]] list.

## Caveats

Employment terms are in each contract, not in the handbook.
