# Should We Keep The Oat Milk Surcharge?

## Why It Matters

Customers ask about it weekly, and some cafés have dropped theirs. It came up in the [[price-review-2026]] because the surcharge is a price like any other.

## Current Answer

Yes, at 0.40 EUR, unchanged. Oat milk costs us about 0.19 EUR more per drink than whole milk, with extra waste from the smaller jugs ([[oat-milk-sales-and-cost-2025]]). Dropping it would cost about 3,800 EUR a year at 31 oat drinks a day. Raising it was rejected because the cost gap has not grown.

## Revisit when

Oat milk's price per litre falls below 1.80 EUR, or oat passes a quarter of milk drinks. Amira checks it every January.
