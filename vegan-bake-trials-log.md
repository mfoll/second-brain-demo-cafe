# Vegan Bake Trials Log

Trial rounds with [[priyanka-desai]], on Mondays when the basement is free. Recipes and costings are in [[vegan-croissant-trial-recipes]].

## Croissant

- Round 1, 2025-11-17: plant butter from the cash and carry, 27 layers. Butter melted out on the proof; flat, bready. June Adeyemo's verdict was not kind. Team score 1 of 4 "would buy".
- Round 2, 2026-01-26: a block plant fat for pastry, colder dough, longer rest between folds. Better layers, waxy finish. 1 of 4.
- Round 3, 2026-03-16: same fat, laminated at 12°C, a small amount of glucose syrup in the dough for colour. Good layers, greasy mouthfeel. 2 of 4, June said "nearly".
- Round 4, 2026-05-18: tried a mix of block fat and a coconut-based spread. Worse. Paused for the summer.

## Cake (easier)

- Aquafaba meringue kisses, 2026-01-26: hold their shape well, bake low and long. Good enough to sell; nobody asked for them yet ([[aquafaba]]).
- Egg-free chocolate sponge with apple sauce, 2026-03-16: moist, a bit heavy. Fine for a traybake ([[egg-substitutes]]).

## Notes for next round (November 2026)

- Laminate first thing on a Monday, when the basement is coldest.
