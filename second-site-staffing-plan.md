# Second Site Staffing Plan

Draft staffing for a seven-day counter café in unit 7, written with [[lena-marsh]] on 2026-06-05. Front of house only; production is in [[central-production-kitchen]].

## Summary

Two full-time equivalents plus a weekend part-timer to cover 08:00-17:00 seven days, about 145 paid hours a week including opening and closing. A shift lead would come from Hollowgate.

## Why It Matters

Staff cost (9,800 EUR a month) is the largest line in the [[saltings-hall-three-scenario-model]] after rent, and the rota is where Lena expects trouble.

## Key Details

- Shift lead: offered first to [[amira-saleh]] or [[grace-oyelaran]]; Amira would want to keep coffee training across both sites.
- Two new hires: one full-time barista and counter, one part-time weekends.
- Cover for holidays and sickness from Hollowgate, which Lena says would leave Saturdays short twice a month.
- Deputy can run both sites under one account with separate locations ([[rota-system-switch]]).

## Caveats

No conversation with Amira or Grace yet; this is a plan on paper. Hours are rota averages, not individual contracts.
