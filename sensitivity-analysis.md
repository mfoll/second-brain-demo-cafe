# Sensitivity analysis

## Summary

Describes sensitivity analysis as working out how much of the uncertainty in a model's output comes from each uncertain input, and notes it is best done together with uncertainty analysis.

## Why It Matters

Read when [[farid-nasser]] suggested by email that three scenarios were not enough on their own. It led to the sensitivity lines in the [[saltings-hall-three-scenario-model]]: average spend moves the base-case [[break-even-month]] most, about two months per 0.50 EUR.

## Caveats

Much of the article is about formal statistical methods; I only changed one input at a time, which is the simplest form.
