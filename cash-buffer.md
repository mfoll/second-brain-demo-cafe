# Cash Buffer

The cash we keep that is not spoken for by the next VAT payment, rent quarter or payroll, so that a bad week does not become a crisis.

## How We Use It

Two levels:

- **Weekly floor**: the end-of-week balance in the [[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]] should not drop below 15,000 EUR, about three weeks of payroll. Set in March 2025 after the [[overdraft-squeeze-january-2025]]. Any forecast week below it gets a written plan.
- **Expansion buffer**: before taking on fixed commitments like a second site, three months of payroll and fixed costs in cash, about 95,000 EUR in 2026 ([[cash-buffer-calculation-2026-09]]). This is [[ruth-marchbank]]'s rule and is still being argued ([[how-big-a-cash-buffer-do-we-need]]).

## What Does Not Count

- The VAT pot. That money belongs to the tax office ([[vat-money-is-not-our-money]]).
- Christmas deposits, until the orders are collected.
- The overdraft, except as a last resort.

## Distinctions

A buffer is not profit: we made 38,600 EUR before tax in 2025 and still have less buffer than Ruth wants. It is closer to the working capital idea in [[working-capital]], but measured at the worst week rather than at the year end.
