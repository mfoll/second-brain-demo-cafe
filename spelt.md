# Spelt

English Wikipedia article on spelt, also called dinkel wheat.

## Summary

Spelt is a species of wheat, a relict crop still eaten in parts of Europe, and high in protein. The article notes that it is marketed as a health food in some countries.

## Why It Matters

Spelt is the heritage flour closest to becoming a product: a 30% spelt sourdough won both tastings ([[heritage-grain-tasting-notes]]). The article's first line is the one I want on the counter card: spelt is wheat. Customers sometimes assume it is safe for coeliacs; it is not, and it is labelled as a cereal containing gluten ([[allergen-management]]). The health-food marketing it mentions is something I do not want to lean on.
