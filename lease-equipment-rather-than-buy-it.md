# Lease Equipment Rather Than Buy It

## Stance

For big equipment that we cannot work without, lease rather than buy from cash, so that the cash buffer survives the breakdown that caused the purchase.

## Scope

Ovens, chillers, mixers over about 10,000 EUR. Small items we buy.

## Why I think this

- The oven failure came in July, with the January VAT squeeze six months behind us ([[cash-flow-forecast]]). Buying for 41,500 EUR would have left almost nothing.
- Leasing took two weeks; a loan needed a guarantee and longer.

## Why it is under revision

[[ruth-marchbank]] points out the lease cost 7,220 EUR in finance charges, about 1,100 EUR more than the bank loan, and that the 812 EUR a month counts against us when Pellow Mutual looks at the second-site loan ([[second-site-financing]]). Once we hold the three-month buffer she wants, buying outright may be the better habit.

## What would change my mind

A cash buffer of about 95,000 EUR. At that point, buy.
