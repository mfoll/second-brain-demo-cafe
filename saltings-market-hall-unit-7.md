# Saltings Market Hall Unit 7

The heads of terms, the option agreement and the plans for unit 7 at Saltings Market Hall, the converted fish market on the estuary that reopens as a food hall in spring 2027. This is the only live second-site candidate.

## Summary

Unit 7 is a 28 m2 corner counter unit on the hall's east side, with a back store of 9 m2 and access to the shared seating (about 160 covers for the whole hall). The hall offered heads of terms at 1,450 EUR a month. We signed a six-month option on 2026-06-19 for a 2,900 EUR deposit, which holds the unit for us until 2026-12-19.

## Why It Matters

It is the basis of the [[saltings-hall-feasibility]] model and the deadline for the [[second-site-decision]]. The option date is why the decision must be made by 2026-12-15 ([[second-site-review-2026-09-30]]). If we let it lapse, [[priscilla-adomako]] has said she has another bakery interested, which I take with some salt.

## Key Details

Heads of terms (issued by the hall's solicitor, 2026-05-28):

| Item | Term |
|---|---|
| Rent | 1,450 EUR a month, reviewed every three years, capped at inflation |
| Service charge | 310 EUR a month (cleaning, security, shared seating, waste, common pest control) |
| Lease | 10 years, tenant break at year 5 |
| Rent-free | 3 months from opening |
| Deposit | Three months' rent on signing the lease |
| Hours | Hall opens 08:00-17:00 seven days; evening trading optional from year 2 |
| Use | Bakery and café; no hot cooking beyond ovens and coffee |
| Fit-out | Shell with power, water, drainage; extraction for ovens provided by the hall |
| Signage | Hall-wide scheme, own name on the fascia |

Option agreement: deposit 2,900 EUR, credited against the lease deposit if we sign, refundable minus 500 EUR if we withdraw before 2026-12-19. After that date the deposit is lost and the unit is free to let.

Plans: the unit drawing allows a 4 m counter, one display case, a two-group espresso machine, a deck or convection oven up to 9 kW, two undercounter fridges and a hand-wash sink. The hall's extraction makes a small oven possible without a flue of our own, the exact problem that sank [[tanners-row-site]].

Opening date: the hall says "spring 2027"; [[priscilla-adomako]]'s working date is 2027-04-17. Fit-out access from 2027-02-15.

Council position: the unit would be a separate food business registration ([[imogen-hale]]), and needs no pavement or premises licence of our own ([[aled-vaughan]]).

## Caveats

- The opening date depends on the hall's own building works. If it slips past summer 2027, the rent-free months help but our staff and loan would already be committed.
- The service charge is estimated for year one only.
- Footfall figures come from the hall ([[saltings-hall-footfall-projections]]), not from trading.
