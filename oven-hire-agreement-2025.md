# Oven Hire Agreement 2025

Hire agreement for a two-deck electric oven from a catering equipment hire firm, signed 2025-08-05, in service 2025-08-06 to 2025-11-14.

## Key Details

- 1,150 EUR a month plus 450 EUR delivery and collection; minimum term one month, then weekly.
- Total paid: 4,140 EUR including delivery and collection.
- Two decks, smaller than our old oven, steam on the top deck only.
- Insurance: hired equipment added to our policy by [[petra-szabo]] for 38 EUR.

## Why It Matters

It kept the shop baking. It also set the ceiling: roughly 70% of the old daily output, with only one deck able to steam bread. Kofi's schedule put the shop first, so the 60 wholesale loaves a day became the squeeze, and that led to the pause on 2025-10-20 ([[why-did-we-pause-wholesale-to-cafes]]).
