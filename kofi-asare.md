# Kofi Asare

Head baker and lead of the early shift since 2020. Runs the basement bakery from 04:30: dough schedule, the oven, ordering of flour and yeast with me, and training of everyone who bakes.

## How we work together

Kofi decides the bread; I decide the viennoiserie and the range. We meet on Tuesdays at 12:30 when his shift ends to go through the week's production and waste ([[end-of-day-waste-log]]). He prefers to be told about changes face to face, not by message, and he will say no plainly when something does not work in the bakery. He is usually right about the bakery and sometimes too cautious about trying new things; the Tarnwick flour trials ([[flour-supplier-review]]) only worked once he ran them his way.

## Current threads

- Hours: 47 a week on average in summer 2025, 43.5 in September and October 2026 with [[hana-kobayashi]] on the rota. The aim is under 42 ([[can-we-get-the-head-baker-under-42-hours]]).
- Training Hana to run the bake alone by December 2026 ([[assistant-baker-hiring]]).
- His objection to 03:00 starts if Hollowgate bakes for a second site, raised at [[second-site-review-2026-09-30]]. He is not against a second site; he is against one built on the early shift working longer ([[central-production-kitchen]]).
- Co-wrote the critical control points in the [[food-safety-management-plan]].

## Background

Kofi trained in a large commercial bakery before joining us in 2020, when the bakehouse was a year old and I was still doing the early bake myself. He built the sourdough programme we have now: the levain schedule, the overnight retard in the walk-in, and the house loaf that outsells everything except croissants. He ran the bake trials with Beatrix Coull that moved our bread flours to Tarnwick Mill in July 2025, and he chose the new oven on the strength of his test bakes ([[deck-oven-quotes-2025]]).
