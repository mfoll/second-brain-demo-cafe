# Tanner's Row Extraction And Flue Quote

Quote from Harrowby Ventilation, dated 2026-03-27, for a kitchen extract system and external flue at the empty Tanner's Row shop.

## Summary

38,000 EUR including VAT for a canopy over a two-deck oven and a small hob, a fan, filtration and a stainless flue running 11 m up the rear wall of a three-storey building to clear the roofline. Eight weeks' work, subject to planning consent for the flue.

## Why It Matters

It ended the [[tanners-row-site]] project. With a fit-out of about 70,000 EUR and working capital, the shop would have cost more than 130,000 EUR before opening ([[why-did-we-drop-tanners-row]]).

## Key Details

- Survey on 2026-03-12, fee 450 EUR (paid).
- Flue route needs consent from the flat owners above; two of three had not replied.
- Planning consent for an external flue: not guaranteed in that street, and adds 8-12 weeks.
- A recirculating hood (no flue) for ovens only was quoted as an alternative at 9,500 EUR, but would not allow hot food and the landlord would not accept it without a fire engineer's report.
