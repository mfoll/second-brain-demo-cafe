# Open The Bakery At 0430

For whoever leads the early shift: Kofi, Joel, Hana when ready, or Odile. Follow it in order; it takes about 20 minutes before the first shaping.

## Steps

1. Enter by the yard door. Disarm the alarm (code on the card you were given, never written down here).
2. Lights, extraction on, then oven on: all three decks to 250 °C. It needs 45 minutes.
3. Clock in on the Deputy kiosk.
4. Wash hands at the basement hand-wash sink. Check it is clear and stocked.
5. Do the bakery opening checks on the sheet ([[daily-food-safety-checks]]): fridges, walk-in, yesterday's cooling entries, signs of pests. Sign.
6. Read the day's quantities on the board. If Lena or Odile have left a note about weather, an order or an event, adjust now.
7. Croissants and pains au chocolat from the retarder to the prover (26 °C, 75% humidity).
8. Sourdough from the walk-in for shaping.
9. Check flour stock for tomorrow's mix; if short, text Odile before 06:00 so the order goes in.
10. Start the bake as per the [[early-shift-production-schedule]].

## If something is wrong

- Oven will not heat: check the isolator, then call Colm Ashworth's emergency number (in the front of the equipment folder). Bake in the café's convection oven and cut the range to the eight best sellers.
- Walk-in above 5 °C: follow the "limit missed" section of the checks. Do not wait for someone to answer the phone.
- Fewer than two bakers: call Odile, then Joel or Kofi, whoever is off.

## Handover

At the end of the shift, hand over the cooling log in person to Priyanka or Mateus with the times of the next checks.
