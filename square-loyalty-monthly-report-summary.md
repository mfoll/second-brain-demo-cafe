# Square Loyalty Monthly Report Summary

A spreadsheet where I copy five aggregate numbers from the Square Loyalty reports on the first Monday of each month: total members, new members, stars issued, rewards redeemed, and share of hot drinks scanned. Started July 2025. No member-level data is ever exported.

## Key Details

| Month | Members | New | Rewards | Scanned |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2025-06 | 702 | 702 | 21 | 31% |
| 2025-07 | 1,104 | 402 | 118 | 36% |
| 2025-12 | 1,420 | 48 | 196 | 39% |
| 2026-03 | 1,610 | 61 | 162 | 37% |
| 2026-06 | 1,840 | 74 | 171 | 38% |
| 2026-09 | 1,950 | 39 | 168 | 38% |

## Why It Matters

It is how I know the scheme still works without opening Square every week. December peaks because of hot chocolate. It fed [[loyalty-programme-first-year-figures]].

## Caveats

Square's member count includes people who joined once and never came back; "active in 90 days" is the better figure and I only copy it quarterly.
