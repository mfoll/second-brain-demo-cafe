# Forward Buying

Agreeing today a price for goods delivered later, usually against a volume commitment. It buys certainty, not necessarily a lower price. The general financial idea is the [[forward-contract]].

## How it applies to us

- Butter: Glenvarrow's fixed 8.40 EUR/kg for six months against 900 kg ([[glenvarrow-fixed-price-offer-2026-10]]).
- Chocolate: buying six weeks of batons at the old price before Kestrin's January rise ([[kestrin-chocolate-price-notice-2026-01]]). That is forward buying in stock, not on paper, and it needs space and shelf life.

## Rules I set myself

1. Commit only to volumes we used in the same months last year, minus 10%.
2. Fix prices to make the costing reliable, not to bet on the market. Ruth Marchbank's phrase: "you're a bakery, not a trader".
3. No more than six months at a time.
4. Read the shortfall clause before the price.

## Risk

If prices fall, we pay over the market for the term. That is the cost of a known price.
