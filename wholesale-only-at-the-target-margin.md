# Wholesale Only At The Target Margin

## Stance

We should never again sell bread wholesale below 35% gross margin counted with the baker hours it needs, even to friends, even as a way in.

## Scope

Any business-to-business bread or pastry supply: other cafés, a second site run as a separate cost centre, events. Not the small standing counter orders like Tomas Vidal's baguettes at 10% off retail.

## Why I Think This

- In 2025 I priced wholesale at about 63% of retail to win the two accounts, planning to raise prices later. Raising prices on a friend's café is harder than setting them right at the start; I never did it.
- The margin was 33% on paper and lower in reality once the early-shift overtime was counted ([[wholesale-pricing-floor]]).
- When the oven failed, the low-margin work was the work that squeezed the shop. A thin margin leaves no room for a bad month.

## What Would Change My Mind

A central production kitchen with its own staff and costs, where volume brings the cost per loaf down far enough that 30% on a much bigger turnover beats 35% on 60 loaves.

## Implications

Any restart starts from the floor price, not from the 2025 price list ([[when-could-wholesale-resume]]).
