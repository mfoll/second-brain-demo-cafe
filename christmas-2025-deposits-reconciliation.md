# Christmas 2025 Deposits Reconciliation

The figures come from the [[christmas-2025-deposit-reconciliation|deposit ledger spreadsheet]].

My reconciliation of the Christmas 2025 preorder deposits in Xero against collections, done on 2026-01-12, and what they did to the January cash low point.

## Summary

214 preorders, 30% deposits taken from 2025-11-03, about 4,900 EUR received before 2025-12-15. Five orders not collected; deposits kept as per the terms. The January 2026 low point was 18,200 EUR in the week of 2026-01-26, and the overdraft was not used.

## Why It Matters

The test of the idea behind deposits: Christmas money arriving early covers the January dip ([[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]]). It is the reason [[christmas-preorders-2026]] keeps 30% deposits and aims for 320 orders.

## Key Details

- Average order 76 EUR; average deposit 22.90 EUR.
- Deposits held in "Deposits received" until collection, then released to sales ([[square-to-xero-daily-import-settings]]).
- Without deposits the forecast's January low point would have been about 13,300 EUR, under the 15,000 EUR floor.
- Q4 2025 VAT payment: 12,400 EUR, paid on 2026-01-29 from the VAT pot.

## Caveats

The deposit is not extra money, only earlier money; the balance is paid on collection.
