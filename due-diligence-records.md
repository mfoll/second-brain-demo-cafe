# Due Diligence Records

The written records that show we did what the plan says, kept so that we can prove it to an inspector, an insurer or ourselves. The working rule since June 2025: if it is not on the sheet, it did not happen.

## What counts

Readings, times and initials made at the moment, in pen; corrective actions written when they happen; the Monday sign-off; training certificates; pest control and cleaning reports; supplier specifications. Not: a reading filled in later from memory.

## Why it matters to us

We lost a hygiene point for cooling we were doing but not recording ([[environmental-health-inspection-2025-06-18]]). Good practice without a record looks the same as no practice to anyone who was not there.

## Our rules

- Sheets kept for two years in the red binder.
- Gaps are raised with the person, not hidden; a gap with a note ("probe broken, used spare at 07:10") is better than a neat invented number.
- Checking is part of the job, so the time is on the rota.
