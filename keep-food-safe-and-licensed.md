# Keep Food Safe And Licensed

Food safety, allergens, the premises and the licences that let us trade. The hygiene rating sits in the window and on every listing, so this is the area where a slip costs the most. It has no end date.

## Scope

- The [[food-safety-management-plan]], written on HACCP principles, and the daily records that prove we follow it ([[daily-food-safety-checks]]).
- Inspections by the Brackenmouth Borough Council environmental health team ([[imogen-hale]]) and the Borough Food Hygiene Rating.
- Allergens: the [[allergen-matrix]], labels on everything we prepack, and what staff tell customers ([[allergen-management]]).
- Pest control ([[callum-reid]]), cleaning, and the premises as far as food safety goes. Repairs to the building go through [[cyril-ashdown]] under [[run-daily-operations]].
- Licences: the pavement seating licence ([[aled-vaughan]]) and the food business registration.
