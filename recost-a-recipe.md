# Recost A Recipe

How to update a product's cost in the [[recipe-costing-sheet]] when an ingredient price changes, and what to do if it goes off target.

## When

- A key ingredient (butter, flour, chocolate, eggs, coffee, milk) moves more than 5% on an invoice or notice.
- A recipe changes: new quantity, new supplier, new size.
- Every January for every product, before the price review.

## Steps

1. Update the ingredient's price on the Ingredients tab from the invoice, with the date and supplier.
2. Open the Summary tab and sort by "points off target". Every product using that ingredient has recalculated.
3. For each product more than 3 points over its target food cost ([[food-cost-percentage]]), check the recipe block is current: quantities, yield, waste allowance.
4. Write the product, old and new food cost and the reason on the "Changes" tab.
5. If a product is more than 3 points off target for two months running, put it on the list for the next price review or the next calendar slot. Do not change prices between reviews ([[price-rises-once-a-year-not-in-dribs]]) unless a product would lose money.
6. Butter only: also update the [[butter-price-tracker]].

## Checks

- Ex VAT for the sheet; shelf price only for quick counter maths.
- Waste allowance not changed to make a number look better.

## Output

An up-to-date sheet and a short list of products to watch, which goes to Lena and Priyanka once a month.
