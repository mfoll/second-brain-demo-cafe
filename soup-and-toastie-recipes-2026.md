# Soup And Toastie Recipes 2026

The recipe cards for the restored lunch, written up by Mateus and me in the week before 2026-04-20 from Dominic's old notes and costed by Priyanka.

## Summary

A soup base method with six seasonal soups (12 litres a batch, 40 portions of 300 ml), and two toasties: cheese and leek on house sourdough, ham and mustard on the seeded rye. Everything is prepped between 10:00 and 11:30; at lunch the toasties go in the panini press, so no hob is needed during service.

## Why It Matters

These recipes are what made the partial reversal at the [[menu-review-2026-04-09]] possible with one person instead of a cook. They use bread Kofi already bakes and cheese we already buy.

## Key Details

- Soup with bread 6.80 EUR, food cost 1.94; cheese and leek toastie 7.50, 2.21; ham and mustard toastie 7.80, 2.36.
- Soups rotate with the [[seasonal-bake-calendar]]: squash, leek and potato, tomato and lentil, pea and mint, minestrone, celeriac.
- Allergens for each recipe are in the allergen matrix kept under [[allergen-management]].

## Caveats

Soup is cooled and held under the [[daily-food-safety-checks]] cooling log; the cards point there and do not repeat the limits.
