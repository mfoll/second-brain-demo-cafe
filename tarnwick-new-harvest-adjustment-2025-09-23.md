# Tarnwick New Harvest Adjustment 2025-09-23

The first new-harvest flour from Tarnwick arrived with Tuesday's delivery. Beatrix had phoned Kofi the Friday before.

## What happened

The batch sheet showed protein at 12.2% (down from 12.8%) and a lower falling number. Kofi's Tuesday dough was slack at 76% and the loaves spread. Wednesday he dropped to 74%, Thursday 75%, and by the following Tuesday he was back to 76% as the flour settled.

## What we changed

- Kofi now drops hydration by two points for the first two deliveries after Beatrix's harvest call, and works back up ([[house-sourdough-recipe-card]]).
- Beatrix agreed to put "new harvest" in red on the first batch sheet each year.

## Why it is worth keeping

This is the cost of a stone-ground mill with named farms: the flour changes with the year. The old wholesaler blended it away. Two days of flatter loaves, about 40 sold as seconds in yesterday's bags. Worth it, but it needs to be in the routine, not in Kofi's head.
