# Courier Article On Larder Donations 2025-11

"Bread that would have been binned now feeds families", by [[orla-kinsella]], Brackenmouth Courier, 20 November 2025, page 7. About 600 words with a photo of Bernard and me at the shop door.

## Summary

- Describes the Community Larder's food bank and community fridge and the businesses that give it surplus food.
- Quotes [[bernard-osei]] on bread being the item the Larder runs out of first.
- Quotes me on the waste log and the donation agreement, and gives our figure of about 1.5 tonnes of bread since April (to the end of October).
- Ends with an appeal for more bakeries and greengrocers to join.

## Why It Matters

Two other bakeries signed up with the Larder within a month. We had a noticeably busier Saturday after it ran, and several Christmas orders that mentioned the article. It is also the wording on donations that the Larder approved, which we reuse ([[community-larder-donation-agreement]]).

## Caveats

The paper rounded our waste figure to "under 5%", which was true for October only.
