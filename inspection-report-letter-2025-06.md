# Inspection Report Letter 2025-06

The written report of the 2025-06-18 inspection from Imogen Hale, Brackenmouth Borough Council environmental health team, received 2025-06-24.

## Summary

Confirms a Borough Food Hygiene Rating of 4. Scores the three areas separately: hygienic food handling good, structure and cleaning good with one item, confidence in management marked down. Lists two required actions (record cooling of cooked high-risk foods; keep hand-wash basins accessible and stocked at all times) and one recommendation (consistent cleaning sign-off at weekends). Explains how to request a re-rating visit after improvements and the right to reply.

## Why It Matters

This letter, not the conversation on the day, is what the action plan answers ([[environmental-health-inspection-2025-06-18]]). I replied on 2025-06-20 before it arrived and again on 2025-06-30 with photos of the sink and the cooling sheet.
