# Team Culture

How the nine of us work together: hours, early starts, who gets asked first, how decisions are explained, and what happens when something goes wrong. This is a lateral surface; the work itself lives under [[run-daily-operations]].

## Threads worth knowing

- The early shift carries the most strain. Kofi's hours became visible only after the move to Deputy ([[rota-system-switch]], [[summer-2025-early-shift-pressure]]). The stance behind the hire: [[no-one-should-routinely-work-more-than-45-hours]].
- Decisions that cut hours land hard. Cutting the savoury lunch cost us Dominic ([[dominic-fairley]], [[team-meeting-2026-02-05]]); I explained the margin well and the people side badly. Mateus taking the soup and toasties in April went better because he asked for it ([[mateus-ferraz]]).
- Kofi's objection to 03:00 starts for a second site is a culture question as much as a logistics one ([[second-site-review-2026-09-30]], [[central-production-kitchen]]).
- Blame-free handling of mistakes: the walnut label near miss was reported by Finn within minutes because nobody gets shouted at for raising a problem ([[walnut-label-near-miss-2025-08-22]]).

## Habits we keep

- Team meeting first Thursday of the month at 16:15, paid, with cake.
- Rota out by Thursday for the following fortnight ([[rota-principles]]).
- Everyone does the Level 2 food safety course in paid time ([[staff-training-matrix]]).
- Front of house and bakery swap for a morning twice a year so each side knows what the other does.
