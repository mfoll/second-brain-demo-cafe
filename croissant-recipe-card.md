# Croissant Recipe Card

The croissant recipe as Priyanka and I make it, rechecked on 2026-01-20 for the costing run.

## Summary

Dough for 96 croissants: 5 kg flour (pastry flour from the old wholesaler), 550 g sugar, 100 g salt, 200 g fresh yeast, 1.4 litres milk, 1.2 litres water, 400 g dough butter. Lamination: 3.9 kg block butter, three single folds, rest 30 minutes in the chiller between folds. Rolled to 4 mm, cut into triangles of about 85 g, proofed at 26 °C for 2 hours, egg washed, baked at 180 °C for 17 minutes.

## Why It Matters

The croissant is our top seller and the item most exposed to butter. About 45 g of butter in each one (dough plus block), which is why every Glenvarrow notice turns into a line in the [[croissant-cost-breakdown]] and the [[butter-price-tracker]].

## Key Details

- Waste allowance in costing 5%; trims go into the almond croissants and the morning buns.
- Retarded overnight after shaping on Tuesdays and Saturdays when I bake; otherwise laminated the afternoon before.
- Pain au chocolat uses the same dough with two batons each.

## Caveats

Proof times move with the season; the card gives summer times.
