# Harriet Lowe

Chair of the Brackenmouth Traders' Association and owner of the gift shop next door at 12 Hollowgate. Our nearest neighbour and the person who knows what every trader on the street thinks before the council does.

## Role

- Chairs the Traders' Association (about 40 members). Runs the late-night Christmas shopping evening and the summer street fair.
- Edits the association's monthly newsletter ([[traders-association-newsletter-2025-11]]).

## How we work together

- We swap posters: our Christmas preorder poster goes in her window, her events go on our counter.
- She gives an early read on anything that touches the pavement. For the 2027 [[pavement-seating-licence]] renewal (6 extra covers) she checked that the extra tables leave her shop front clear and offered a letter of support in the consultation.
- She buys a dozen mince pies for her shop every December Saturday and pays on account.
