# Pavement Seating Licence

Renewing the pavement seating licence for 2027 and using the renewal to ask for six more covers outside, from 12 to 18, by adding two tables along the side wall on the Hollowgate side street.

## Outcome

Pavement licence renewed for 2027 with 6 extra covers. Application submitted 2026-09-21; public consultation ends 2026-11-02.

## Current State

- Application submitted to the Brackenmouth Borough Council licensing team on 2026-09-21 with the plan, the fee of 410 EUR and the public liability certificate ([[pavement-licence-application-2027]]).
- Notice posted in the window on 2026-09-21, as required, with a photo filed. Consultation closes 2026-11-02.
- [[aled-vaughan]] confirmed on 2026-10-06 that the 1.8 m clear walkway on the plan is acceptable.
- No objections known so far. [[harriet-lowe]] said she would support it if the tables do not block her shop window.
- The 2026 licence ([[pavement-licence-2026]]) runs to 2026-12-31.

## Scope and Boundaries

The licence, the plan, the consultation and the conditions. Furniture purchase is small and comes after the decision. Staffing for the extra covers is Lena's under [[run-daily-operations]].

## Working Context

Pavement seating is worth about 9% of takings from May to September and almost nothing from November to March ([[square-pos-sales-data]]). Six more covers would add roughly 6,000 to 8,000 EUR of takings over a summer, on Lena's estimate from the 2025 table turns. The conditions are what worry me: furniture in by 18:00, no barrier fixed to the pavement, and the walkway kept clear for prams and wheelchairs. The side-street tables sit next to the gift shop, so Harriet's view matters more than any formal objection.

We considered asking for 24 covers and dropped it: it would need the space in front of the tapas bar, and [[tomas-vidal]] uses that for his own licence in the evenings.
