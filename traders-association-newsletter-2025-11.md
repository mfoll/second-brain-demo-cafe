# Traders' Association Newsletter 2025-11

The November 2025 newsletter of the Brackenmouth Traders' Association, edited by [[harriet-lowe]], emailed to members on 3 November 2025.

## Summary

- Late-night shopping on Thursday 4 December, 16:00 to 20:00, Hollowgate closed to traffic from 17:00. Members asked to stay open and post the shared image on 1 December.
- Council consultation on parking charges on Quayside.
- A reminder that pavement licence renewals for 2026 were due by 30 November.
- Welcome to two new members.

## Why It Matters

It set the date and shape of the evening that became our best preorder day of 2025 ([[traders-association-late-night-shopping-2025-12-04]]). The pavement licence reminder was the one we had already handled; the 2027 renewal is [[pavement-seating-licence]].
