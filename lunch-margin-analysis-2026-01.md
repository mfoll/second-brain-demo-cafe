# Lunch Margin Analysis 2026-01

The workbook I built in January 2026 that compared the savoury lunch with the bakery, using the costing sheet, Square item sales for October to December 2025 and the lunch cook's hours.

## Summary

Lunch was about 20% of weekday takings and ran at 51% gross margin on food and packaging, against 71% for bakery, counter and coffee. With the 30 cook hours a week added, lunch contributed little after labour. The hot plates were worst: shakshuka 44%, quiche plate 47%.

## Why It Matters

The analysis behind the decision at the [[team-meeting-2026-02-05]]. It was right about the dish margins and wrong about the whole visit: it did not count the coffee and cake that lunch customers buy, which the [[menu-review-2026-04-09]] found out.

## Key Details

| Item | Price | Gross margin |
|---|---|---|
| Soup with bread | 6.50 | 63% |
| Toasties (two) | 7.20 to 7.50 | 60 to 62% |
| Focaccia sandwich | 7.90 | 55% |
| Sausage roll plate | 8.50 | 52% |
| Grain bowl, roast veg bowl | 10.50 | 48%, 49% |
| Quiche plate | 10.90 | 47% |
| Shakshuka | 10.50 | 44% |

Weighted average 51%. The cheapest three to make were also the three best margins.

## Caveats

Attach rate (coffee and cake per lunch ticket) not measured. Square could have shown it; I did not ask. See [[lunch-should-stay-small-but-not-disappear]].
