# Flour Supplier Review

Whether to keep buying all our flour from the old roller-mill wholesaler or move the bread flours to a regional stone-ground mill. Done: bread flours moved to Tarnwick Mill from 2025-07-01, pastry flour stayed put.

## Outcome

Bread flours (strong white and wholemeal) supplied by Tarnwick Mill from 2025-07-01 on a 12-month agreement, after side-by-side bake trials, with pastry flour left with the old wholesaler.

## Current State

- Completed 2025-06-27. Agreement signed with [[beatrix-coull]] at Tarnwick for 2025-07-01 to 2026-06-30, renewed in June 2026 for another year at the same price plus 4%.
- Tarnwick strong white costs 0.96 EUR/kg delivered against 0.78 at the old wholesaler: about 340 EUR a month more for roughly 1,900 kg.
- The house sourdough went from 72% to 76% hydration to suit the new flour ([[dough-hydration]]).
- Pastry flour stayed with the old wholesaler after Priyanka's lamination test; see [[should-pastry-flour-move-to-tarnwick]].

## Scope and Boundaries

Bread flours only in the end. Rye and spelt for specials are bought in small sacks as needed and were not part of the review. Heritage wheats are exploration under [[heritage-grains]], not a supply decision.

## Working Context

Why we looked: the old wholesaler raised prices twice in 2024 and Kofi had been complaining about inconsistent protein between batches since the autumn. I wanted the bread to taste of something and to be able to say where the flour came from. The worry was cost and a supply cut if a small mill had a breakdown.

What decided it:

1. The blind trial on 2025-05-14 ([[flour-bake-trial-2025-05-14]]): Tarnwick loaves won on crumb, crust and taste with all six tasters, and kept a day longer.
2. Beatrix agreed to hold two weeks of our flour at the mill as a buffer and to deliver Tuesdays and Fridays.
3. The extra 340 EUR a month is about 2% of bread takings. We put "Tarnwick stone-ground flour" on the bread board and on the bags, and did not raise bread prices until the March 2026 review.

Rejected: moving everything at once. Priyanka's croissant test with Tarnwick's finer flour gave a tighter honeycomb and specks of bran in the layers; not worth it for viennoiserie.
