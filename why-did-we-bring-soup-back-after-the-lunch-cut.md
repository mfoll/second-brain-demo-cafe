# Why Did We Bring Soup Back After The Lunch Cut?

## Why It Matters

Staff, regulars and Ruth have all asked. It is also the clearest case we have of a decision made on the margin of a dish that ignored the margin of the visit, and the next time someone proposes cutting a section the answer should be here.

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> Because cutting lunch to bakery only on 2026-02-23 cost more than it saved. Weekday takings between 11:30 and 14:30 fell 22% over six weeks, coffee and cake fell with lunch, and regulars asked for the soup daily. At the menu review of 2026-04-09 we brought back soup and two toasties from 2026-04-20, made by Mateus Ferraz in about 12 hours a week, while plated hot dishes stayed off.

## How we got there

1. [[team-meeting-2026-02-05]]: lunch at 51% gross margin against 71% for bakery, and 30 cook hours a week. Cut decided against Lena's advice.
2. Dominic Fairley left on 2026-03-27 after his hours were cut.
3. [[lunch-takings-six-week-review-2026-04]]: 815 to 636 EUR a weekday in the lunch window.
4. [[menu-review-2026-04-09]]: soup and two toasties chosen because one trained person can make them before 11:30 with bread and cheese we already have.
5. The restored lunch runs at about 64% margin and takings recovered to within 6% by late May ([[savoury-lunch-trim]]).

## What stays settled

Shakshuka, the quiche plate and the salad bowls stay off; going back to a full cooked lunch was rejected ([[should-we-drop-lunch-entirely]] is closed the other way). The lesson is in [[lunch-should-stay-small-but-not-disappear]].
