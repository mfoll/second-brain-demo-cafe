# Finance Course Final Presentation 2025-12-08

Last session of the [[food-business-finance-course]] at Brackenmouth College, Monday 8 December 2025. Each of the 14 participants presented a ten-minute model of their own business to [[farid-nasser]] and the group.

## What I presented

The [[break-even-model-workbook]]: fixed costs of about 31,800 EUR a month, contribution margin of about 66%, break-even net takings of about 48,200 EUR a month, against a 2025 average of 50,800 EUR. Then three what-ifs: the January dip, the wholesale pause, and the new oven lease.

## Feedback

- Farid: the 66% average hides a big spread between bakery and lunch; I should split the model by section. He was right, and the split is what made the lunch margin so visible in the [[savoury-lunch-trim]] a few weeks later.
- A participant who runs a food truck asked why I counted all wages as fixed. Because the rota does not change when it rains, which is the whole point.
- Farid also suggested a "cash low point" line in the model alongside break-even, linked to the [[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]].

## After

Course passed. I added the section split over Christmas and sent the workbook to Ruth Marchbank in January 2026.
