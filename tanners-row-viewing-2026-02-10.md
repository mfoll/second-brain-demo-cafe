# Tanner's Row Viewing 2026-02-10

First viewing of the empty shop on Tanner's Row with [[simone-ward]]. [[kofi-asare]] came after his shift to look at the back room.

## What We Saw

- Good frontage and light; a 74 m2 room that would take about 24 covers.
- No three-phase power, no extraction, no floor drain. Back room 12 m2.
- Simone said straight away that the building had never had a flue and the flats above were owner-occupied.

## Reactions

- Kofi: fine as a counter for Hollowgate bread, wrong for baking on site without a flue.
- Me: the location is good for the morning trade from the car park. The building is the risk.
