# What Should We Do With Unsold Pastries?

## Why It Matters

Viennoiserie was about 30% of end-of-day waste by value in March 2025 ([[end-of-day-waste-log]]). The Larder cannot take anything with cream or custard and prefers bread, so pastries needed their own answer.

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> Plain and fruit viennoiserie left at closing go into "yesterday's bags" the next morning: three pastries for half their combined price, on the counter from opening until sold, never past the day after baking. Anything with cream, custard or a cooked filling is not kept; it is binned and logged.

In place since 6 May 2025. Staff may take one item home at closing; nothing else leaves free.

## Options Considered

- Give pastries to the Larder: refused by the Larder for filled items, accepted for plain ones only in small amounts. We tried in April 2025; the volunteers asked us to stop because they went stale before distribution.
- Discount at 15:00: rejected. It trained regulars to come late, and the Saturday trial on 26 April 2025 cut full-price sales after 14:00 by more than it recovered.
- Reworking (almond croissants from day-old croissants): Priyanka does this on Saturdays only, up to 24, because it needs a cook and an oven slot.

## Evidence

[[yesterdays-bag-sales-summary]]: about 45 bags a week at an average of 3.80 EUR, 95% sold by 11:00, bringing in roughly 170 EUR a week. Related stance: [[donating-beats-discounting-bread]].
