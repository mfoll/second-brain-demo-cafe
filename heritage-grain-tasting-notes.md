# Heritage Grain Tasting Notes

Two tastings of heritage grain loaves baked from Tarnwick samples ([[tarnwick-heritage-flour-sample-list]]). Bakes by Kofi and Joel on Mondays; tasting by whoever was in.

## Tasting 1, 2025-11-24

| Loaf | Result |
|---|---|
| 100% wholemeal spelt, yeasted | Nutty, sweet, crumbly. Over-mixed on the first attempt; fine on the second with half the mixing time. |
| 50% spelt sourdough | The favourite. Open crumb, mild sourness. |
| 100% emmer sourdough | Dense, dark, rich. Split the room. |

## Tasting 2, 2026-04-27

| Loaf | Result |
|---|---|
| 30% spelt sourdough | Favourite again. Close to our white sourdough in handling, with more flavour. |
| 20% einkorn in a white loaf | Golden crumb, slightly sticky dough. Joel liked it; Kofi did not like handling it. |
| Khorasan and white, 40/60 | Buttery colour, good toast. Flour too expensive for now. |

## What Kofi said

Spelt doughs are fine once you respect them: short mix, warm water, shorter bulk. But a new dough in the morning sequence means a new timing, and he will not take it on until [[hana-kobayashi]] is fully trained.

## Price per loaf (flour only)

30% spelt sourdough: about 0.25 EUR more than the white sourdough. Shelf price would need to be about 5.20 EUR.
