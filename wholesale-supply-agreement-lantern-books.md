# Wholesale Supply Agreement Lantern Books

Supply agreement with Lantern Books café, signed by [[ewan-macrae]] and me on 28 May 2025, in force from 2 June 2025. Same template as [[wholesale-supply-agreement-pier-street-coffee]] with three differences.

## Key Details

- Minimum order 15 loaves a day instead of 25.
- No rye on Saturdays (Ewan's Saturday menu is toasties on white).
- Payment within 21 days, because the bookshop invoices its own café monthly.
- Everything else the same: standing order Tuesday to Saturday, collection 06:45 to 07:15 from the basement door, two weeks' notice, prices fixed to 31 December 2025.

## Why It Matters

The smaller minimum made Lantern's bake less efficient per loaf: about 22 loaves of four products on most days. In the [[wholesale-margin-workbook-2025]] Lantern's margin was about 31% against Pier Street's 34%.
