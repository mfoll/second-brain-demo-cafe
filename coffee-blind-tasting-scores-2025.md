# Coffee Blind Tasting Scores 2025

Score sheet from the tasting of 2025-10-15: three roasters' house espressos, each tasted as espresso and as a flat white, labelled X, Y and Z by Amira.

## Summary

Eight tasters ranked the three in each format. Ombrel's Estuary (Y) came first for espresso with six of eight and first in milk with all eight. The cheapest (Z, 21.00 EUR/kg) came second in milk but last as espresso. The third roaster (X, 23.80 EUR/kg) split the room.

## Why It Matters

The decision evidence for the [[coffee-roaster-change]]. Most of what we sell is milk drinks, so the flat white result counted double in my head; Ombrel winning both made the extra cost easy to defend.

## Key Details

- Tasters: Odile, Amira, Lena, Finn, Grace, Priyanka, Kofi and June Adeyemo as a customer voice.
- Same grinder and machine, dialled in by Amira for each coffee the morning of the test.
- Comments most used for Y: "chocolate", "sweet in milk", "not bitter".

## Caveats

Each coffee dialled in for one morning only; X might have done better with more time. Prices compared per kg, not per cup.
