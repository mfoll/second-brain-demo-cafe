# School Bake Club

A monthly after-school bake club with Year 4 at Saltings Lane Primary School, run with [[samir-belkacem]]. The 2025-26 year was the pilot; this year it runs again with a new class.

## Outcome

A monthly after-school bake club with Saltings Lane Primary run through the 2026-27 school year: eight sessions from October 2026 to May 2027, each with at least 10 children, without disrupting the café.

## Current State

- Second year started on Wednesday 7 October 2026 with 12 children. Next session 4 November 2026 (soda bread).
- Sessions run on the first Wednesday of the month, 15:30 to 16:45, in the school's food room. We bring weighed ingredients and pre-made dough; the children shape, bake in the school ovens and take everything home.
- Staff: me plus one of [[priyanka-desai]] or [[grace-oyelaran]], about 2.5 hours each including travel and preparation. Costs about 38 EUR of ingredients a session, paid by the bakehouse.
- The pilot year ran eight sessions with an average of 11 children. The school asked to keep it ([[is-the-bake-club-worth-two-staff-hours-a-month]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

We run the baking; the school runs the children. A teacher is present at every session and handles consent, pickup and any medical information. We never hold children's names or allergy details: the school tells us which allergens to leave out for that group, and we plan the recipe around it ([[bake-club-allergy-consent-form]]). No photographs of children on our social media; the school posts its own if it wants.

## Working Context

It started from an email from Samir on 10 September 2025 asking whether a local business could help with the Year 4 topic on "food from field to table" ([[saltings-lane-primary-partnership-email]]). The pilot was meant to be three sessions and became eight.

What works: one product per session that bakes in under 25 minutes, dough made in our basement the day before, and a 10-minute talk at the start about where the flour comes from. What did not: anything laminated (too slow, too much butter on the floor), and yeasted dough made at school (not enough time to prove). The plans are in [[bake-club-session-plans]] and the routine in [[running-a-bake-club-session]].

The food-room ovens are domestic and run hot; [[school-bake-club-risk-assessment]] covers hot trays, knives and the allergen check.
