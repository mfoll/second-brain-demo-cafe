# Loyalty Points Should Reward Habits Not Spend

## Stance

Our loyalty scheme should reward coming back, not spending more per visit. One star per visit-shaped purchase (a hot drink), not points per euro.

## Scope

A small café whose regulars come several times a week. Not a view on loyalty schemes in general.

## Why I Think This

- Our best customers are not big spenders; they are the people who come in four mornings a week for a flat white. A spend-based scheme rewards the occasional cake-for-the-office order instead.
- Points per euro would make the reward slow to arrive for a 3.20 EUR coffee and confuse the counter conversation.
- Simple rules get used. Customers can explain "nine coffees, one free" to each other; they cannot explain a points table. Background reading: [[loyalty-program]].

## What Would Change My Mind

If the first-year figures had shown that scanned customers spend no more often than the rest. They did: scanned customers came back on average 2.6 times a week against an estimated 1.4 for unscanned card payments ([[loyalty-programme-first-year-figures]]), with the obvious caveat that keen regulars are the ones who join.

## Implications

Any pastry reward should also count visits, not euros ([[should-loyalty-rewards-cover-pastries]]).
