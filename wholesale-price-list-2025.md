# Wholesale Price List 2025

The wholesale prices offered to Pier Street Coffee and Lantern Books on 20 May 2025, excluding VAT. Fixed to 31 December 2025.

## Key Details

| Product | Wholesale | Shop price | Wholesale as % of shop |
|---|---|---|---|
| White sourdough, 900 g | 2.90 | 4.60 | 63% |
| Seeded rye, 800 g | 3.20 | 5.10 | 63% |
| Baguette, pack of 2 | 2.20 | 3.40 | 65% |
| Sandwich tin loaf, 800 g | 2.60 | 4.00 | 65% |

## Why It Matters

These prices were set from food cost alone and a rough look at what other bakeries charge, without counting baker time. That is the mistake [[wholesale-pricing-floor]] corrects: at October 2026 costs the white sourdough floor is about 3.35 EUR.

## Caveats

Shop prices are those of May 2025, before the March 2026 price review.
