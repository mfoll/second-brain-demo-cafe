# Who Covers The Early Shift When Kofi Is Off

When the head baker is on leave or ill, who runs the 04:30 bake?

## Current Answer

[[joel-whitcombe]] leads, with [[hana-kobayashi]] as second since September 2026. Before that I was the second, which meant I baked four mornings in a row during Kofi's holidays and ran the business in the afternoons. For a week or more of leave the range is cut by two bread varieties and the Saturday late bake is dropped. Kofi writes the week's quantities before he goes.

## If Joel is also off

I lead with Hana; range cut to the eight best sellers. It has not happened since 2024.

## Revisit

When Hana runs a section alone (planned December 2026) she can lead with Joel off. Answered 2026-09-01 with the autumn rota ([[rota-pattern-autumn-2026]]).
