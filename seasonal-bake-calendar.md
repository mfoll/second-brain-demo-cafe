# Seasonal Bake Calendar

A year of specials worked out in advance, so the board follows the produce and the costing sheet instead of whatever Priyanka and I think of on Friday afternoon.

## Outcome

A 12-month calendar of seasonal specials tied to produce availability and planned four weeks ahead.

## Current State

- The calendar sheet covers June 2026 to May 2027, two specials a month plus a soup rota ([[seasonal-bake-calendar-sheet]]).
- Planned four weeks ahead on the first Monday of each month with [[priyanka-desai]] and Stellan's availability sheet ([[how-far-ahead-should-we-plan-specials]]).
- Autumn slot live from 2026-09-22: plum and frangipane tart, apple and cheddar scone, roast squash soup ([[autumn-menu-2026]]).
- November slot planned on 2026-10-05: pear and ginger cake, chestnut and chocolate bun.
- Specials averaged 66% gross margin June to September, slightly under the 68% target; the strawberry tart in June was the drag.

## Scope and Boundaries

Counter specials and the soup rota. Christmas lines belong to [[christmas-preorders-2026]]; the calendar just leaves room for them in December.

## Working Context

Before this, specials were invented week by week and often costed after they sold. At the [[seasonal-calendar-planning-2026-05-04]] session we agreed three rules: a fruit goes on only when it is in season and under the price in the [[recipe-costing-sheet]]; every special uses something we already stock; and each one is tested on a Saturday before it goes on the calendar. Stellan sends availability and rough prices four to six weeks out ([[brook-and-daughters-seasonal-availability-sheet]]).
