# June Adeyemo Blog Post On The Soup Return

A post on [[june-adeyemo]]'s local food blog, published 28 April 2026 and read on 29 April: "The soup is back on Hollowgate". About 500 words with two photos of the counter.

## Summary

- Recalls her March post criticising the cut of the savoury lunch to bakery only.
- Reports that soup and two toasties returned on 20 April, made by Mateus, and that the shakshuka and salad bowls have not.
- Rates the leek and potato soup well and the cheese and onion toastie highly, at 6.50 EUR with soup as a meal deal.
- Says she understands why plated dishes stayed off but misses the quiche.

## Why It Matters

It is the public side of the partial reversal in the [[savoury-lunch-trim]] decided at the [[menu-review-2026-04-09]]. Her March post was one of the signals that regulars wanted soup back; this one told her readers it had happened. Lunch takings recovered part of the fall in May.

## Caveats

A friendly regular's blog, not a measure of trade. Takings evidence is in [[square-pos-sales-data]].
