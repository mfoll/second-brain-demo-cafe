# Christmas 2025 Deposit Ledger

The spreadsheet behind the [[christmas-2025-deposits-reconciliation|written reconciliation report]]: one row per preorder, deposit and collection.

The reconciliation of Christmas 2025 deposits between the paper forms, Square and Xero, done by me on 9 January 2026 and checked by Ruth Marchbank's office.

## Key Details

- Orders taken: 214. Deposits received: 4,903.50 EUR.
- Cancelled before 12 December: 7 orders, 163.00 EUR refunded.
- Not collected: 5 orders, 118.50 EUR of deposits kept and recognised as income on 24 December.
- Balances taken on collection: about 11,250 EUR across 23 and 24 December.
- Deposits were held as a liability in Xero until collection, then released to sales with VAT.

## Why It Matters

This is the evidence for the claim that the deposits carried January 2026: the low point in the forecast was 18,200 EUR in the week of 26 January, the week of the VAT payment ([[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]]). It is the base of [[customer-deposits-as-working-capital]].

## Caveats

The balances figure is from Square sales tagged "Christmas balance"; a few customers paid balances early in December and may be counted in the deposits.
