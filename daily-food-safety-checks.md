# Daily Food Safety Checks

The checks every shift does to show the [[food-safety-management-plan]] is being followed. Written to be followed at 04:30 by whoever opens the bakery and at 16:00 by whoever closes the café. Sheets live on the clipboard by the basement door and the clipboard under the till.

## Inputs

- Today's sheet (one A4 page, two sides, version 3.1).
- Probe thermometer and wipes. Check the probe reads 0 °C ± 1 in iced water if it has been dropped.
- Pen. Not pencil.

## Opening (bakery, 04:30, early shift lead)

1. Check the basement hand-wash sink: soap, paper towels, hot water, nothing stored within a metre of it. Write "OK" or what you fixed.
2. Read and record every fridge and the walk-in chiller. Limit: at or below 5 °C.
3. If any reading is above 5 °C, probe one high-risk item inside. If the item is above 8 °C, or you cannot tell how long it has been warm, move the stock and tell Odile before 07:00. Record what you did.
4. Check yesterday's cooling entries are complete. A missing 6-hour reading means the batch goes in the bin. Record it.
5. Check for signs of pests in the dry store and under the bench. Any droppings or gnawing: stop, bag the affected stock, call [[callum-reid]] and Odile.
6. Sign the opening box.

## Opening (café, 07:00, front of house lead)

1. Record the display fridge and the under-counter fridges. Limit: at or below 8 °C for the display, 5 °C for the others.
2. Check the allergen folder is on the counter and dated this week.
3. Check labelled prepacked items from yesterday against their dates. Out of date: remove and record in the [[end-of-day-waste-log]].
4. Sign.

## During the day

1. Deliveries of chilled goods: probe one item. Above 8 °C, refuse the delivery and record it.
2. Cooked fillings, chicken and soup: probe the core at the end of cooking (at least 75 °C) and record the time.
3. Cooling: shallow trays, uncovered, on the rack by the extractor. Record time and core temperature at 2 hours (must be below 20 °C) and at 6 hours (must be below 5 °C). Write the times you are due to check on the tray label.
4. Soup reheating: core at least 75 °C, once only. Record.

## Closing (café, 16:00)

1. Record display and under-counter fridges.
2. Clean to the closing list ([[cleaning-schedule]]), including the coffee machine. Sign each area.
3. Bag unsold bread for the Community Larder per the [[community-larder-donation-agreement]]; record waste.
4. Sign the closing box and put the sheet in the tray in the office.

## When a limit is missed

- Do not guess and do not fill in a reading later. Write what happened, what you did and the time.
- If high-risk food may be unsafe, throw it away. Cost is not a reason to keep it.
- Tell Odile the same day. If she is unreachable, tell Lena or Kofi.

## Verification

Odile reads every sheet on Monday, signs the week and files it in the red binder. Gaps are raised with the person on the next shift, never in the group chat. Sheets are kept for two years ([[due-diligence-records]]).
