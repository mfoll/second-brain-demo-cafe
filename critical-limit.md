# Critical Limit

The measurable line at a critical control point that separates safe from unsafe: a temperature, a time, or both. If a reading is on the wrong side, the batch is out of control and the corrective action happens, no discussion.

## How we use it

- Every limit in our [[food-safety-management-plan]] is something a person can read on a probe or a clock at 05:00. "Cool quickly" is not a limit; "below 20 °C within 2 hours" is.
- A limit is not a target. We aim well inside it: fridges run at 3 °C so that a door left open does not breach 5 °C.
- Each limit has one corrective action written beside it so nobody has to decide under pressure.

## Our four

Chilled delivery at or below 8 °C; cooking core at least 75 °C; cooling below 20 °C in 2 hours and below 5 °C in a further 4 ([[two-stage-cooling]]); cold holding at or below 5 °C, display at or below 8 °C.
