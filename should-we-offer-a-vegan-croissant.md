# Should We Offer A Vegan Croissant?

## Why It Matters

It is the product customers ask for most that we do not make: two or three requests a week, mostly Saturdays. But our croissant is the product the shop is known for, and a poor vegan version on the same counter would hurt both.

## Evidence So Far

- Four trial rounds with [[priyanka-desai]] between November 2025 and May 2026 ([[vegan-bake-trials-log]]). Best result: version 3 with a block plant fat, acceptable layers, greasy mouthfeel. [[june-adeyemo]] called version 1 "a bread roll with ambitions".
- Food cost of version 3 is about 0.78 EUR, cheaper than the butter croissant now ([[vegan-croissant-trial-recipes]]).
- Allergens: it cannot be sold as milk-free because it is laminated on the same bench and baked on the same trays. Label would say "made without milk; may contain milk".
- Lamination time: about 40 minutes extra a week for a batch of 24 on a separate bench slot.

## Current Answer

Not yet. Not a product while it is clearly worse than the butter version.

## What Would Settle It

A version that three of four blind tasters in the team rate "would buy", and a separate bench slot that does not touch the early shift. Next trial round in November 2026, after Christmas planning if it slips.
