# Bookkeeping Move To Xero

Move the books off the spreadsheet I had kept since 2019 and onto Xero, with bank feeds and the Square takings coming in automatically.

## Outcome

Bookkeeping moved from spreadsheets to Xero with bank feeds and Square sales imported daily; first VAT return filed from it. Reached on 2025-07-31.

## Current State

- Completed. Xero is the books. The old [[bookkeeping-spreadsheet-2019-2025]] is archived read-only.
- Bank feed from the Pellow Mutual current account and the VAT pot; Square takings import once a day ([[square-to-xero-daily-import-settings]]).
- The first VAT return from Xero was for Q2 2025, filed on 2025-07-28 ([[first-xero-vat-return-2025-07-28]]).

Bookkeeping and VAT only. Payroll stays with Marchbank Ochoa's payroll bureau, fed by Deputy timesheets from the [[rota-system-switch]]. The forecast stays a spreadsheet on purpose ([[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]]).

## Working Context

Why: the spreadsheet took me about five hours a week, mostly typing bank lines. In January 2025 it was three weeks behind, which is part of why I did not see the squeeze coming ([[overdraft-squeeze-january-2025]]).

How it went:

- April 2025: chart of accounts agreed with Ruth ([[xero-chart-of-accounts-setup]]). We split sales into bakery, lunch, drinks and wholesale so the margin report works ([[margin-by-menu-section-2025]]).
- May 2025: bank feeds on; opening balances at 2025-03-31 from the spreadsheet, checked by Ruth.
- June 2025: Square import tested. The first attempt posted card fees as sales; fixed by mapping fees to their own account.
- July 2025: parallel running for the Q2 quarter, then the VAT return filed from Xero.

Rejected: keeping the spreadsheet with a bank CSV import (still my evenings), and handing all bookkeeping to Ruth's firm (1,450 EUR a quarter, and I would lose sight of the numbers).

Time now: about 50 minutes a week reconciling, Monday mornings before the forecast.
