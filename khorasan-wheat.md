# Khorasan Wheat

English Wikipedia article on khorasan wheat, also called Oriental wheat.

## Summary

Khorasan is a tetraploid wheat whose grain is about twice the size of modern wheat, with a rich, nutty flavour.

## Why It Matters

Background for the 40/60 khorasan and white loaf at the April 2026 tasting ([[heritage-grain-tasting-notes]]). The flavour description matched what we found: buttery colour and good toast. Tarnwick buys it in and supply varies (2.90 EUR/kg, [[tarnwick-heritage-flour-sample-list]]), so it stays a curiosity rather than a product.

## Caveats

Very short article; nothing on supply or baking.
