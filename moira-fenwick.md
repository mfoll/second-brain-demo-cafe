# Moira Fenwick

Account manager at Glenvarrow Dairy, our butter, milk and cream supplier since we opened in 2019.

## Role

She sets our prices, sends the monthly price notices ([[glenvarrow-dairy-price-notices]]) and is the person to call when a delivery is short. She has managed our account since 2021.

## How we work together

- Deliveries every day we are open except Sunday; the dairy order is placed by Lena or me by 14:00 the day before.
- Moira gives a month's notice of price changes and explains them, usually feed and milk-fat prices. She has never hidden a rise.
- Meets me in person twice a year, normally March and October.

## Current threads

- On 2026-10-06 she offered a fixed 8.40 EUR/kg for butter from November 2026 to April 2027 against a 900 kg commitment, with an answer wanted by 2026-11-06 ([[glenvarrow-fixed-price-offer-2026-10]], [[glenvarrow-meeting-2026-10-06]]).
- She knows we are getting a second quote and said so herself: "fair enough".
- Whatever happens to butter, milk and cream stay with Glenvarrow.
