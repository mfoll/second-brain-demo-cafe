# Kofi Email On Central Production

[[kofi-asare]]'s email of 2026-09-23 in reply to my draft of [[central-production-kitchen]]. Summarised with his agreement; kept because it set the terms of the [[second-site-review-2026-09-30]].

## Summary

He supports a second site in principle and objects to the production plan as drafted. A 03:00 start, every day the shop is open, for him and Joel, is a line he will not cross and will not ask Hana to cross.

## Why It Matters

It is the second of the two conditions on the second site ([[should-we-open-a-second-site]]) and a fair warning about the early shift ([[baker-hours-limit-growth]]).

## Key Details

- His points: the early shift already starts at 04:30; summer 2025 averaged 47 hours a week for him; Hana was hired to bring that under 42 and has been here a month.
- He would consider an afternoon shift that prepares doughs and shapes for the next morning, if it is staffed by a new baker and not by stretching the current three.
- He wants the bakers to propose the pattern, not to be handed one.
- He asked that unit 7 not be mentioned to the wider team until a decision is made.
