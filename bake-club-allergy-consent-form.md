# Bake Club Allergy Consent Form

The template we wrote with Samir for the school to send to parents before the first session, September 2025. The school sends it, collects it and keeps it; we never see the completed forms.

## Summary

- Explains that children will handle and eat flour, butter, milk, eggs and, on some dates, seeds; lists the 14 allergens we track.
- Asks parents to tick allergies and intolerances and to give consent for the child to taste what they bake.
- States that the bakehouse handles nuts and sesame in its own kitchen and cannot guarantee products are free from them.

## Why It Matters

It keeps children's health information with the school, where it belongs. The school turns the forms into a list of allergens to exclude for the group, with no names, and sends us that list ([[running-a-bake-club-session]]).

## Source Location

Template in Community/Bake club; completed forms held only by Saltings Lane Primary.
