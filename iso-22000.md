# ISO 22000

## Summary

Wikipedia article on ISO 22000, the International Organization for Standardization's standard for food safety management systems. It combines HACCP-style hazard control with management-system requirements: documented policy, communication along the supply chain, prerequisite programmes and continual improvement. Organisations can be certified against it.

## Why It Matters

A supplier questionnaire from a hotel group in January 2025 asked whether we were certified to ISO 22000. We are not and will not be: certification costs and audits make no sense for nine staff. But the structure was useful for the [[haccp-plan-rewrite]]: it pushed me to write a short section on supplier approval and on who reviews the plan and when, which version 2 lacked.

## Key Details

- Management-system standard in the same family style as other ISO management standards.
- Covers interactive communication with suppliers and customers, system management, prerequisite programmes and HACCP principles.
- Applies to any organisation in the food chain regardless of size.

## Caveats

We borrowed ideas, not compliance. Nothing in our plan claims conformity.
