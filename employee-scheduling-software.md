# Employee scheduling software

## Summary

Wikipedia's article on employee scheduling software: tools that create and maintain work schedules for hourly and shift-based staff, track time off, availability and conflicts, and export hours for payroll. Some systems try to optimise the schedule; others mainly coordinate and record it.

## Why It Matters

Lena and I read it at the start of the [[rota-system-switch]] to agree what we were buying. We wanted coordination and records (swaps, leave, clock-in, a payroll export), not automatic optimisation. That became the comparison table ([[deputy-and-alternatives-comparison]]). The unexpected value was in the last line of the article's description: once hours are recorded, they can be analysed, which is how we saw Kofi's overtime ([[deputy-timesheet-export-summer-2025]]).

## Key Details

- Core functions: building schedules, availability, time off, conflicts.
- Data can be exported for payroll and analysed later.
- Optimisation features are optional and vary.

## Caveats

Describes the category, not any product. Deputy is our choice for reasons in the comparison.
