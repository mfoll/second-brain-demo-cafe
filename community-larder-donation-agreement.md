# Community Larder Donation Agreement

The two-page agreement between Hollowgate Bakehouse and the Brackenmouth Community Larder for the donation of unsold bread, signed by me and [[bernard-osei]] on 14 April 2025. First collection on 15 April 2025. Runs for two years, to 13 April 2027, and either side can end it with four weeks' notice.

## Summary

We give the Larder our unsold bread at the end of each trading day. The Larder collects it, stores it and passes it on through its weekly food bank sessions and the community fridge. The agreement sets what can be given, how it is labelled, who collects when, and who is responsible for what once the bread leaves our door.

## Why It Matters

It is the second half of the [[food-waste]] work. The [[end-of-day-waste-log]] showed that bread was about 60% of our end-of-day waste by value in March 2025, and bread keeps well enough for a day or two. Giving it away costs us nothing extra and the Larder needed bread more than anything else. I set it against discounting in [[donating-beats-discounting-bread]].

It also has to fit our food safety plan: what leaves the shop must be labelled correctly, with allergens, which made us tighten labelling for donations before the [[allergen-labelling-overhaul]] covered the shop.

## Key Details

What we give:

- Loaves, rolls, baguettes and plain focaccia baked that day.
- Not accepted: anything with cream, custard, cream cheese or cooked fillings; anything that has been on a customer's table; anything past the day after baking.
- Pastries are not part of the agreement. They go into yesterday's bags at half price the next morning ([[what-should-we-do-with-unsold-pastries]]).

Labelling (our responsibility):

- Each bag carries a printed label: product name, "baked on" date, the allergens present (cereals containing gluten in every case; sesame, mustard, milk or nuts where they apply) and the line "made in a bakery that handles milk, eggs, nuts and sesame".
- Labels come from the same allergen matrix as the shop. Until December 2025 they were handwritten from a laminated sheet; since then they are printed.

Collection:

- Tuesday to Saturday at 16:15 from the shop door by a Larder volunteer. If nobody comes by 16:45 we freeze the bread and text Bernard.
- Sunday leftovers go to the community fridge by whoever closes, before 15:00.
- Bread is handed over in our paper sacks, weighed and recorded on the weekly sheet ([[larder-donation-record-2025]]).

Responsibility:

- Once the bread is handed over, storage and distribution are the Larder's responsibility under its own food safety rules.
- We do not charge and the Larder does not pay. We may mention the donations publicly; the Larder approves any wording.

## Caveats

- No minimum quantity. On good days there is almost nothing, which is the point of the waste work.
- The agreement says nothing about Monday, when we are closed; the frozen-bread Monday pickup Bernard asked for in 2026 would need an amendment.
- The routine that puts this into practice is [[end-of-day-waste-and-donation-routine]].
