# Cash flow forecasting

## Summary

General article on predicting a business's future cash position from expected receipts and payments. It describes the practice as common to firms of every size and outlines several methods, from a simple receipts-and-disbursements schedule to methods derived from projected accounts.

## Why It Matters

Read in the first week of the [[cash-flow-forecast]] project, after the [[overdraft-squeeze-january-2025]]. It confirmed that the receipts-and-payments approach, listing each expected payment on its real date, was the right one for a shop where timing is the whole problem. The [[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]] is built that way.

## Caveats

Written for businesses in general and partly for larger companies. It does not suggest a horizon; thirteen weeks was [[ruth-marchbank]]'s suggestion, not the article's.
