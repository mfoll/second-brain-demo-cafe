# Food Bank

English Wikipedia article on food banks.

## Summary

Describes a food bank as a non-profit, charitable organisation that collects food and passes it on to people who struggle to buy enough, usually through intermediaries such as food pantries and soup kitchens, though some give food out directly.

## Why It Matters

Read before my first meeting with [[bernard-osei]] in April 2025. The Brackenmouth Community Larder does both: a weekly food bank session through the church hall and a community fridge anyone can use. Understanding that the food bank side works through sessions and volunteers explained why the Larder wanted bread that keeps for a day or two and a fixed collection time, which went straight into the [[community-larder-donation-agreement]].

## Caveats

General article; how our Larder works is set by its own rules, not by this.
