# First In First Out

Stock rotation by age: the oldest stock is used first, and new deliveries go behind or under what is already there. Simple, and the first thing to slip when the dry store is full.

## How we do it

- Day dot labels on everything opened or made in-house: the colour for the day plus a written date and initials.
- Flour and sugar: new sacks go on the bottom rack, open sacks on top. The new racking in June 2025 was designed around this ([[basement-storage-changes-2025]]).
- Walk-in: new deliveries at the back, a "use first" shelf at eye level.
- Butter is the exception that proves the rule: a 900 kg contract would mean more butter in storage, so rotation would matter more ([[dairy-contract-renegotiation]]).

## Why it matters

It is a prerequisite in the [[food-safety-management-plan]] and part of keeping waste down ([[end-of-day-waste-log]]). The day the flour sacks ended up against the hand-wash sink was a rotation failure as much as a storage one.
