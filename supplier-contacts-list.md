# Supplier Contacts List

The laminated sheet by the office phone and its copy in the shared drive: every supplier, the person to call, the account number we quote, the order method and the cut-off time.

## Summary

Nine lines: Glenvarrow Dairy ([[moira-fenwick]]), Tarnwick Mill ([[beatrix-coull]]), the old flour wholesaler (pastry flour only), Ombrel Roasters ([[dev-sandhu]]), Brook & Daughters ([[stellan-brook]]), Quarrymoor Farm ([[ada-quarrie]]), Kestrin Chocolate ([[rafaela-montoya]]), Fold & Carry Packaging ([[yusuf-karimi]]) and the oven service contract ([[colm-ashworth]]). Since October 2026 a tenth, pencilled: Coldbeck Creamery ([[nils-jorgensen]]).

## Why It Matters

At 05:00, when a delivery has not arrived, Kofi needs a name and a number, not a vault note. This sheet is the reason that works. It is also the first thing I update when a rep changes.

## Key Details

- Cut-off times: dairy 14:00 the day before; Brook & Daughters 20:00 by text; Tarnwick Monday 18:00 for Tuesday; Kestrin Thursday portal for Monday.
- Emergency rule: if dairy is short, Lena may buy up to 10 kg of butter from the cash-and-carry and keep the receipt.

## Caveats

Phone numbers and account numbers are on the sheet only, not copied into the vault.
