# Saltings Hall Feasibility

Work out whether a counter café in unit 7 of Saltings Market Hall could pay for itself, with a cash flow for three scenarios and an honest footfall estimate.

## Outcome

Three-scenario cash flow and footfall study for unit 7; the base case breaks even in month 14. Reached on 2026-06-26 ([[saltings-feasibility-sign-off-2026-06-26]]).

## Current State

- Completed. The study fed the [[second-site-review-2026-09-30]], where the decision was deferred.
- The model ([[saltings-hall-three-scenario-model]]) gives month 14 to cumulative break-even in the base case, month 9 in the high case and none within 36 months in the low case.
- The six-month option on unit 7 was signed on 2026-06-19 so the study's conclusions would not be wasted ([[unit-7-option-signed-2026-06-19]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

Unit 7 only. The study assumed the unit sells Hollowgate bread, viennoiserie, cakes and coffee, with pastries finished on site and no hot lunch. It did not decide financing ([[second-site-financing]]) or the production model ([[central-production-kitchen]]); both were inputs with stated assumptions.

## Working Context

Inputs and where they came from:

- Rent 1,450 EUR a month plus 310 EUR service charge; three rent-free months at opening ([[saltings-market-hall-unit-7]]).
- Footfall: the hall's own projection of 9,000 visitors a week in year one ([[saltings-hall-footfall-projections]]). I cut it by a third for the base case because it comes from the people letting the units, and because the hall is not open yet.
- Average spend 7.40 EUR (Hollowgate 2025 average is 8.10 EUR, but the hall has more passing trade).
- Gross margin 66%, two points below Hollowgate because of a higher coffee share and more waste early on ([[gross-profit-margin]]).
- Staff: two full-time equivalents plus a weekend part-timer, 7 days, 08:00-17:00 hall hours ([[second-site-staffing-plan]]).
- Fit-out and working capital 120,000 EUR ([[saltings-unit-7-fit-out-budget]]).

Scenarios:

| Case | Weekly takings by month 6 | Cumulative break-even |
|---|---|---|
| Low | 3,900 EUR | Not within 36 months |
| Base | 5,600 EUR | Month 14 |
| High | 7,300 EUR | Month 9 |

[[ruth-marchbank]] checked the model on 2026-06-22 and made one change: the loan repayments and the extra accountancy fee belong in the unit's costs, not hidden in Hollowgate's. That moved the base case from month 12 to month 14.

What the study did not resolve: whether Hollowgate itself can carry the low case without going back into the overdraft. That became the buffer question ([[how-big-a-cash-buffer-do-we-need]]).
