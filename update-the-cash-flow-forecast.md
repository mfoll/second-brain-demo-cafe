# Update The Cash Flow Forecast

Every Monday, before the supplier order calls. 25-40 minutes. Written so that Lena or Ruth could do it if I were ill.

## Inputs

- Xero, reconciled up to Sunday (bank feed and the Square daily import).
- Pellow Mutual online banking: current account and VAT pot balances.
- Deputy: next fortnight's rota hours.
- The [[thirteen-week-cash-flow-forecast]] spreadsheet.

## Steps

1. **Reconcile Xero** up to Sunday. Fix anything unmatched before touching the forecast.
2. **Move the VAT.** Transfer 9% of last week's gross takings from the current account to the VAT pot ([[should-we-set-aside-vat-weekly]]). Note the amount in the forecast.
3. **Roll the week.** Copy last week's actuals into the actuals block; delete the oldest history week so four remain. Add a new week 13 at the end.
4. **Receipts.** Set weekly takings for each week from the same week last year, adjusted by the last four weeks' like-for-like change. Add preorder deposits expected ([[christmas-preorders-2026]] in season).
5. **Fixed outgoings.** Check every fixed date in the next 13 weeks: rent quarters, VAT payment ([[vat-return-calendar]]), oven lease, insurance, payroll runs, corporation tax, loan repayments if any.
6. **Payroll.** Use Deputy's rota for the next two weeks, the average of the last eight weeks after that.
7. **Variable costs.** Ingredients and packaging at 29% of takings unless a known order (Christmas stock, flour contract) is bigger.
8. **Check the floor.** If any end-of-week balance is under 15,000 EUR, write in the notes column what we will do: move a payment, ask for deposits earlier, hold an order ([[cash-buffer]]).
9. **Compare.** Look at last week's forecast against actual. If off by more than 2,500 EUR, write one line on why.
10. **Second-site tab** (while the decision is open): refresh the opening balance only. Do not change the scenario inputs without telling Ruth.
11. **Save.** The drive keeps versions. On the last Monday of each quarter, save a named copy.

## Guardrails

> [!warning]
> Never count the VAT pot or uncollected deposits as available cash, and never change last week's forecast to match what happened.

## Output

An updated forecast and, if needed, a written plan for any week under the floor. Tell Lena if a payment to a supplier will move.
