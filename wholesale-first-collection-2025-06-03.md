# Wholesale First Collection 2025-06-03

First day of [[wholesale-to-cafes]]. Pier Street Coffee and Lantern Books collected their first standing orders from the basement door on Tuesday 3 June 2025.

## What happened

- Pier Street ([[nadia-volkova]]): 34 loaves (20 white sourdough, 8 seeded rye, 6 baguette packs). Collected 06:50.
- Lantern Books ([[ewan-macrae]]): 22 loaves. Collected 07:12 by bicycle trailer.
- Bake started at 04:30 as usual; wholesale went into the second load, after the shop's first bread. [[joel-whitcombe]] packed and labelled the crates.
- Delivery notes were printed from a spreadsheet; invoices monthly on 14 days, as in the agreements ([[wholesale-supply-agreement-pier-street-coffee]], [[wholesale-supply-agreement-lantern-books]]).

## First problems

- Crates: we had six; we needed ten. Ordered more from Fold & Carry the same day.
- Labels: the wholesale bread needed allergen labels as prepacked bread for another business. We used handwritten labels until the printed ones arrived on 10 June.
- The shop counter was short of rye at 08:00 because the rye went to wholesale. Kofi added a second rye batch the next day.

## Afterwards

Kofi said the first week added about 3 hours a day across him and Joel. That figure was the start of the baker-hours problem in [[why-did-we-pause-wholesale-to-cafes]].
