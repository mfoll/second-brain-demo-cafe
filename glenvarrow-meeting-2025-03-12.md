# Glenvarrow Meeting 2025-03-12

Moira Fenwick's spring visit, 45 minutes in the café after the lunch rush.

## Discussed

- Butter at 6.35 EUR/kg from March, after 6.20 in January and February. Moira expected "a slow climb through the year" on feed costs. It turned out faster than slow.
- I asked about a fixed price for the year. Glenvarrow did not offer fixed prices to customers our size in 2025.
- Milk up 4 cents a litre from April.
- Delivery times: the van had been arriving after 07:30 on Saturdays; Moira moved us to the first drop.

## What I did after

Started the [[butter-price-tracker]] properly from the January invoice, with the croissant cost next to it, because if butter was going to climb I wanted to see it month by month. That sheet is why we had the numbers ready for the [[price-review-2026]] and for the fixed-price offer eighteen months later ([[glenvarrow-meeting-2026-10-06]]).
