# Oven Commissioning Checklist

The signed commissioning checklist for the new three-deck oven, completed by the installer and Colm Ashworth on 2025-11-13 and 2025-11-14, with Kofi's test bake notes.

## Key Details

- Electrical: three-phase supply tested; isolator labelled; earth bonding signed off.
- Each deck: temperature at set point within ±5 °C on a probe grid at 230 °C; recovery after loading a full deck in 11 to 13 minutes.
- Steam: each deck delivers a full burst within 4 seconds; drain tested.
- Extraction: canopy airflow checked with the old canopy, which is adequate for electric.
- Training: Kofi, Joel and Odile on programmes, cleaning, and the shutdown sequence.
- Test bakes: sourdough, baguette and croissant on each deck. Bottom deck needed 10 °C less on the sole for baguettes.

## Why It Matters

This is the baseline for the warranty and for Colm's annual service in November 2026. The bottom deck setting is now in the [[early-shift-production-schedule]].
