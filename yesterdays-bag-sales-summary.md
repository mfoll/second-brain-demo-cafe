# Yesterday's Bag Sales Summary

Aggregate Square sales of the "Yesterday's bag" item from 6 May 2025 to 31 December 2025, pulled on 12 January 2026 for the year's waste review.

## Key Details

- Bags sold: about 1,500, or about 45 a week.
- Average price: 3.80 EUR. Takings about 5,700 EUR, about 170 EUR a week.
- 95% sold by 11:00; on average 2 bags a week binned at 13:00.
- Busiest days: Wednesday and Saturday. Tuesday has almost none, because Monday is closed and Sunday leftovers go to the Larder or the bin.

## Why It Matters

Evidence for [[what-should-we-do-with-unsold-pastries]]. It also showed no sign that yesterday's bags cut into full-price pastry sales: morning croissant sales held level compared with spring 2025 ([[square-pos-sales-data]]).

## Caveats

Sunday's pastries cannot become Tuesday's bags (two days old), so they are binned or taken home by staff; that waste stays in the log.
