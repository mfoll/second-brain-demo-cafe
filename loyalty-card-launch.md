# Loyalty Card Launch

Replaced the paper stamp cards with Square Loyalty, linked to the till, so that regulars get their reward without a card and we can see repeat custom as a number instead of a feeling.

## Outcome

Paper stamp cards replaced by Square Loyalty; 1,100 members by the end of July 2025. Reached on 25 July 2025 with 1,104 members.

## Current State

- Completed. The scheme now runs as routine under [[build-local-community]] and is reviewed once a year.
- Rules: one star per hot drink, nine stars for a free hot drink of any size ([[loyalty-scheme-rules-2025]]). Pastries are not in the scheme; that question is open ([[should-loyalty-rewards-cover-pastries]]).
- Members: 1,104 at close, about 1,950 in October 2026. Scan rate on hot drinks settled around 38%.
- Paper cards stopped being stamped on 31 July 2025 and were honoured until 31 December 2025. The old rules are archived in [[paper-stamp-card-rules]].

## Scope and Boundaries

The project covered the scheme design, setting up Square Loyalty on the existing Square Point of Sale, training the counter team, and the switch from paper. It did not cover a mailing list or marketing texts; we decided not to send promotional texts at all. Member phone numbers stay in Square; nothing about individual members is copied into the vault or any spreadsheet.

## Working Context

Why we changed: the paper cards were easy to forge (a stamp from a stationery shop matched ours), cost about 95 EUR a year to print, and told us nothing. In 2024 we estimated that redemptions were running at around 4% of hot drinks, but nobody could say if that was the same 200 people or 2,000.

Options considered in May 2025:

- Keep paper with a new stamp: cheapest, but nothing learned. Rejected.
- A separate loyalty app: more features, but a second system at the counter and a monthly fee of about 60 EUR. Rejected after [[lena-marsh]] tried one for a week at her previous job and said the queue would hate it.
- Square Loyalty on the till we already use: phone number at payment, no app for the customer. Chosen. The cost is a monthly subscription, which I set against the printing and the free coffees we were giving away to forged cards.

What we learned in the launch: the counter script matters more than the poster. Customers joined when [[amira-saleh]] or Lena asked at the moment of payment, and almost never from the poster alone ([[loyalty-launch-poster-and-counter-script]]). The best week was the second week of June, when the first nine-star rewards started arriving and people told each other.

My own view on what loyalty is for is in [[loyalty-points-should-reward-habits-not-spend]].
