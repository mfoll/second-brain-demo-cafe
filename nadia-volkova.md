# Nadia Volkova

Owner of Pier Street Coffee, a small espresso bar near the harbour. Our larger wholesale customer while [[wholesale-to-cafes]] ran: about 35 loaves a day, Tuesday to Saturday, from 3 June to 18 October 2025.

## How we work together

- Terms were in [[wholesale-supply-agreement-pier-street-coffee]]: standing order by 14:00 the day before, collection from our basement door between 06:45 and 07:15, monthly invoice on 14 days.
- She paid on time every month and was easy to deal with. Her main complaint was the 07:15 cut-off on Saturdays.
- When I gave notice on 6 October 2025 ([[wholesale-pause-letter-2025-10-06]]) she was disappointed but understood the oven situation. She moved to a supermarket bake-off supplier and says her customers noticed.

## Current threads

- On 12 May 2026 she asked whether we could restart, even at a higher price ([[wholesale-resumption-request-2026-05-12]]). I said not before the second-site decision in December 2026. She is the first call if [[when-could-wholesale-resume]] gets a yes.
- She still buys croissants at retail for her own staff on Saturdays.
