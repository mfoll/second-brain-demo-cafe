# Central Production Capacity Study

My notes from two weeks of logging oven, mixer and bench use in the Hollowgate basement, 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-13, to see whether it could bake for a second site.

## Summary

Oven capacity is there; people and cold storage are not. The three-deck oven runs flat out 05:00-09:30, half-loaded until 11:00, and is idle from 11:00 to closing apart from cakes. Unit 7 would need about 35% more morning output.

## Why It Matters

It is the starting fact base for [[central-production-kitchen]] and [[can-the-basement-bakery-supply-two-sites]].

## Key Details

- Oven load: 96% of deck time used 05:00-09:30; 48% 09:30-11:00; under 15% after 11:00.
- Spiral mixer: free after 08:00.
- Retarder: full every night; a second one (about 6,500 EUR) would be needed for any overnight dough for unit 7.
- Freezer: no space for frozen viennoiserie for another site.
- Labour: the morning bake takes three bakers ([[kofi-asare]], [[joel-whitcombe]], [[hana-kobayashi]]) from 04:30. Adding unit 7 output in the same window means starting at about 03:00 or adding a shift.
- Transport: one van run at 07:00, 12 minutes each way.

## Caveats

Two weeks in September, a mid-season month; December would look different. Hours are averages logged by the bakers on a sheet, not exact.
