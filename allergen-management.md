# Allergen Management

How we keep track of the fourteen allergen groups across about 70 products, tell customers the truth about them, and stop them getting into the wrong thing. A bakery is a hard place for this: flour is in the air, nuts and sesame are on the bench, and recipes change with the season.

## Where things live

- The single source of truth is the [[allergen-matrix]]. Labels, the counter folder and the website list are printed from it, never typed by hand ([[print-allergen-labels]]).
- Supplier specifications for every ingredient: [[supplier-allergen-specifications]].
- Our stance on "may contain" wording: [[precautionary-allergen-labelling]] and [[do-we-need-may-contain-labels]].
- The near miss that started the overhaul: [[walnut-label-near-miss-2025-08-22]].
- What staff say at the counter: [[allergen-counter-answers]].

## Recurring points

- Any recipe change, including a supplier swap, goes through the matrix before the product goes on sale. The flour move to Tarnwick ([[flour-supplier-review]]) and the change of dark chocolate at Kestrin Chocolate in 2026 both changed specifications.
- We do not sell anything as free from gluten or nuts. The basement is one room.
- Staff allergen training happens at induction and again every year ([[staff-training-matrix]]).
