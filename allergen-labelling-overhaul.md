# Allergen Labelling Overhaul

Getting every product we prepack on site (sandwiches, boxed cakes, bags of cookies, granola, yesterday's bags) onto a printed label with the full ingredient list and allergens in bold, all generated from one allergen matrix.

## Outcome

Every product prepacked on site carries a full ingredient and allergen label printed from one allergen matrix. Reached on 2025-12-12.

## Current State

- Completed 2025-12-12. 64 products in the [[allergen-matrix]]; 31 of them are prepacked and labelled.
- Labels print from the matrix through a small thermal printer at the pass ([[label-printer-and-template-spec]], [[print-allergen-labels]]).
- Supplier specification sheets for every ingredient are filed and dated ([[supplier-allergen-specifications]]).
- The counter folder for unwrapped products is printed from the same matrix and dated on the cover.

## Scope and Boundaries

Prepacked products and the counter allergen folder. Allergen controls in production (segregation, cleaning between batches) are in the [[food-safety-management-plan]]. Wholesale bread labels were left out because wholesale was paused on 2025-10-20.

## Working Context

On 2025-08-22 Finn noticed that a banana loaf, sliced and wrapped for the counter, had a handwritten label that left out walnuts after Priyanka added them to the recipe ([[walnut-label-near-miss-2025-08-22]]). Nobody was harmed and the loaves were relabelled within the hour. It showed that our labels came from three places: Priyanka's recipe cards, Lena's label template in a word processor, and memory.

The fix was to make one matrix the only place an ingredient list is written. Priyanka and I went through every recipe in September and October against the supplier specifications, which turned up two surprises: the glaze contained milk powder and the seeded mix carried a sesame "may contain" from the miller. Labels moved to a thermal printer with a template that pulls from the matrix; Yusuf Karimi at Fold & Carry supplied the label stock. Training for all staff was done on 2025-11-25 ([[allergen-training-2025-11-25]]).

We considered buying a labelling app with nutrition calculation and decided against it: 49 EUR a month for features we do not need, and the matrix already does the one job that matters.
