# Loyalty Launch Poster And Counter Script

The A3 poster for the window and counter and the one-line script for staff, written by Lena and me on 5 June 2025.

## Summary

- Poster: "Your coffee card is now your phone number. Nine coffees, the tenth is on us." With a photo by Grace of a flat white on the counter.
- Script at payment for any hot drink: "Do you want to join the coffee card on your phone number? It takes five seconds."
- If asked about data: "We only use it for your stars. No texts, no marketing."
- If someone has a paper card: "Bring it in any time before the end of the year and we'll move your stamps."

## Why It Matters

The launch showed that the script drove sign-ups and the poster did almost nothing on its own ([[loyalty-launch-day-2025-06-10]]). Worth reusing for anything new at the till, such as the Christmas preorder line.
