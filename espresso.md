# Espresso

## Summary

Overview of espresso: concentrated coffee made by forcing hot water at high pressure through finely ground coffee, served in small volumes of around 25 to 30 ml with crema on top. The article covers its history, the machines, and the variables that shape a shot.

## Why It Matters

Amira circulated it to the front of house in September 2025, at the start of the [[coffee-roaster-change]], so that everyone tasting in the blind test would use the same words. It is the general background to our recipe of 18 g in and 38 g out ([[espresso-dial-in-notes]]), which is a longer shot than the article's typical volume because we serve mostly milk drinks.

## Key Details

- Hot water under high pressure through finely ground coffee; small serving, with a layer of crema.
- Our own rule, not the article's: fix dose and yield, adjust the grind.

## Caveats

General reference; recipes and serving sizes vary widely between cafés.
