# Coffee Sales By Drink 2025

Units of each coffee drink sold in 2025 from the Square item export, put together for the price review.

## Summary

About 61,000 coffee drinks in 2025. Flat white 29%, latte 22%, cappuccino 16%, americano 14%, espresso and macchiato 7%, mocha 5%, others (filter trial, cortado) 7%. Milk drinks 76% of the total.

## Why It Matters

Showed that what matters most is how the espresso tastes in milk, which weighted the [[coffee-blind-tasting-2025-10-15]] and the 0.20 EUR rise on milk drinks in the [[price-review-2026]]. At about 90 kg of beans a month, roughly 18 g a drink, the Ombrel premium is about 0.08 EUR a cup.

## Key Details

- December was the busiest month (5,900 drinks), February the quietest (4,300).
- Average 200 drinks per trading day.

## Caveats

Units only, aggregated ([[square-pos-sales-data]]); staff drinks are not rung through and are not included.
