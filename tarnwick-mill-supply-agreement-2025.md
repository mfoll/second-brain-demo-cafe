# Tarnwick Mill Supply Agreement 2025

Two-page supply agreement between Hollowgate Bakehouse and Tarnwick Mill, signed 2025-06-27, for bread flours from 2025-07-01.

## Summary

Twelve months from 2025-07-01, renewable. Strong white and wholemeal at list price ([[tarnwick-mill-price-list-2025]]), delivered Tuesday and Friday. Expected volume about 1,900 kg a month, no minimum. The mill holds two weeks of our expected use in stock as a buffer. 30-day payment.

## Why It Matters

The buffer clause is what made a small mill acceptable: if a stone breaks or a harvest is late, we have two weeks to find flour. Renewed 2026-06-19 to 2027-06-30 at +4% (strong white 1.00 EUR/kg).

## Key Details

- Each delivery comes with a batch sheet: protein, moisture, falling number.
- Either side may end it with two months' notice.
- No exclusivity: we may still buy specialty flours elsewhere.

## Caveats

No price protection if wheat prices jump mid-year beyond a clause allowing a review "in exceptional circumstances"; not used so far.
