# Croissant Cost Breakdown

What goes into the cost of one croissant, at three points in time. Worked out from the [[croissant-recipe-card]] and the [[recipe-costing-sheet]], with 5% waste.

| Line | Jan 2025 | Jan 2026 | Sep 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Butter (45 g) | 0.28 | 0.37 | 0.40 |
| Flour, sugar, salt, yeast | 0.13 | 0.17 | 0.18 |
| Milk | 0.05 | 0.06 | 0.06 |
| Egg wash | 0.04 | 0.05 | 0.05 |
| Bag or tray share, waste | 0.12 | 0.16 | 0.17 |
| **Food cost** | **0.62** | **0.81** | **0.86** |
| Shelf price | 2.60 | 2.60 | 2.95 |
| Food cost % of shelf price | 24% | 31% | 29% |

## What it shows

- Butter is the largest single line and about half the increase. The rest is everything else going up a little at once.
- The 2.95 price bought back four points; butter took two of them back by September.
- At Glenvarrow's fixed 8.40 the butter line would be 0.38 and the total about 0.84, 28% ([[should-we-sign-the-fixed-price-butter-contract]]).

## Caveat

Shelf-price percentages, as I use at the counter. Ex VAT the September figure is 32% ([[food-cost-percentage]]).
