# Danger zone (food safety)

## Summary

Short Wikipedia article on the temperature range in which food-borne bacteria grow readily. Different food safety agencies draw the edges differently; the article gives the range as roughly 4 to 63 °C and notes the common advice that perishable food should not sit in it for long, with two hours a frequently quoted limit for some agencies.

## Why It Matters

Read on the Saturday after the June 2025 inspection, when I was setting the timings for the [[cooling-log-sheet]]. It confirmed the shape of what we needed (get through the range fast, then keep food cold) rather than any particular number. The fact that agencies disagree on the edges is why our plan states its own limits and explains them ([[two-stage-cooling]], [[critical-limit]]).

## Key Details

- The range is a band, not a line: the middle of it is where growth is fastest.
- Time in the band matters as much as the temperature.
- Hot holding and cold holding both exist to keep food outside it.

## Caveats

The article quotes specific agencies' rules; we do not follow any one country's scheme from it. Our limits are in the [[food-safety-management-plan]].
