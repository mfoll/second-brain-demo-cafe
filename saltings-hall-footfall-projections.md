# Saltings Hall Footfall Projections

The footfall projection for Saltings Market Hall supplied by [[priscilla-adomako]] on 2026-03-20, prepared for the hall's owners when they financed the conversion.

## Summary

9,000 visitors a week in year one, rising to 11,500 in year three, with Saturday the busiest day (about 2,400) and Tuesday the quietest (about 800). Peak hours 12:00-14:00 and 17:00-19:00.

## Why It Matters

Footfall is the biggest unknown in the [[saltings-hall-feasibility]]. I used two-thirds of the hall's figure (6,000 a week) as the base case in the [[saltings-hall-three-scenario-model]] and half (4,500) as the low case. The morning peak matters more to us than the evening one, and the projection has no morning breakdown.

## Key Details

- Method, as described: comparison with "similar converted market halls" plus car park and bus counts near the estuary. No named comparators.
- Assumed capture rate for any single unit: 8-12% of visitors.
- The hall projects 60% of visits after 12:00. A bakery sells most before 12:00, so our capture in the morning is the open question.

## Caveats

Produced by the party letting the units. Not independently checked. The hall is not open, so nothing here is measured.
