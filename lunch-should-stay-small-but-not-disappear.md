# Lunch Should Stay Small But Not Disappear

## Stance

A short lunch that one trained person can make before 11:30 is worth keeping even at a lower margin than the bakery, because it brings in the coffee and cake trade. A full cooked lunch is not worth it for a bakehouse our size.

## Scope

Hollowgate as it is: 38 covers, a basement bakery, no extraction at the counter. A second site in a food hall would be a different question.

## Why I Think This

- Cutting lunch to nothing cost us 22% of weekday takings between 11:30 and 14:30, and the coffee and cake went with it ([[lunch-takings-six-week-review-2026-04]]).
- Soup and two toasties run at about 64% margin with 12 hours a week of prep, against 51% and a 30-hour cook for nine items ([[savoury-lunch-trim]]).
- I judged dishes, not visits. Even the cost-margin side of [[menu-engineering]] ranks items by the gross profit they bring in, not by food cost alone; I looked at percentages and nothing else.

## What Would Change My Mind

- If the soup and toasties drop under 55% margin for a quarter.
- If lunch takings fall with the restored menu too, which would mean the trade had moved elsewhere.
- If Mateus moves on and no one else can make the soup.
