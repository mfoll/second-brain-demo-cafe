# Manage Suppliers And Costs

Who we buy from, what we pay, and how the orders get placed every week. It has no end date: as long as we bake, someone has to watch the butter price and remember that Quarrymoor does not deliver on Thursdays.

## Scope

- Suppliers of ingredients, coffee and packaging, their terms, delivery days and contacts.
- Ingredient prices and the monthly price trackers, starting with [[butter-price-tracker]].
- Contracts and fixed-price offers, and supplier changes after trials.
- The weekly order itself ([[weekly-ingredient-order]]).
- Not here: what we charge customers and recipe costings, which sit under [[develop-menu-and-products]]; equipment servicing, which sits under [[run-daily-operations]]; paying the invoices, which is [[manage-money-and-cash-flow]].

## Current State

- Seven regular suppliers; contacts and order days in [[supplier-order-calendar]] and [[supplier-contacts-list]].
- Butter from [[moira-fenwick]] at Glenvarrow Dairy is 8.90 EUR/kg (September 2026), up from 6.20 in January 2025. Glenvarrow has offered a fixed 8.40 EUR/kg for November 2026 to April 2027 against 900 kg; [[nils-jorgensen]] is quoting for Coldbeck Creamery first. See [[dairy-contract-renegotiation]].
- Bread flours have come from Tarnwick Mill ([[beatrix-coull]]) since 2025-07-01; pastry flour stays with the old wholesaler ([[flour-supplier-review]]).
