# Seasonal Bake Calendar Sheet

The twelve-month grid for specials, June 2026 to May 2027: two counter specials and a soup rota per month, each with its costed margin and a "tested" tick.

## Summary

Rows are months, columns are special 1, special 2, soups, key produce and expected margin. Filled and tested to November 2026; December holds space for the Christmas range; January to May 2027 pencilled.

## Why It Matters

The working document of the [[seasonal-bake-calendar]] and the record of what sold. Specials averaged 66% gross margin June to September.

## Caveats

Margins use costing-sheet prices at planning time, not the invoice prices of the week.
