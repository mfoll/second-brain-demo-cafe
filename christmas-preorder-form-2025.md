# Christmas Preorder Form 2025

The white two-part paper order form used for Christmas 2025. Superseded by [[christmas-preorder-form-2026]]; kept for the 2025 prices and the wording of the deposit terms.

## Summary

- Products and 2025 prices: stollen 22.50 EUR, bûche de Noël 32.00 EUR, mince pies box of 6 12.50 EUR, fruited Christmas sourdough 9.00 EUR.
- Deposit 30% at ordering; balance at collection.
- Terms: refundable until 12 December, not after; uncollected orders released at 14:00 on 24 December.
- Collection on 23 or 24 December.

## Why It Matters

It is the baseline for the 2026 price rise (about 6%) and the source of the refund rule ([[should-christmas-deposits-be-refundable]]). Its main flaw: no box for a collection slot, which Lena added by hand on about 80 forms ([[christmas-2025-preorder-debrief]]).
