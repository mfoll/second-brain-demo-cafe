# Wholesale Supply Agreement Pier Street Coffee

One-page supply agreement with Pier Street Coffee, signed by [[nadia-volkova]] and me on 27 May 2025, in force from 2 June 2025. Paused by notice on 6 October 2025 ([[wholesale-pause-letter-2025-10-06]]).

## Key Details

- Products and prices from [[wholesale-price-list-2025]].
- Standing order Tuesday to Saturday, changes by 14:00 the day before. Minimum 25 loaves a day.
- Collection from the basement door, 06:45 to 07:15. No deliveries.
- Bread labelled with product, baked-on date and allergens as prepacked for another business.
- Invoice monthly, payment within 14 days.
- Either side may end or pause the agreement with two weeks' written notice.
- Prices fixed to 31 December 2025, then reviewed.

## Why It Matters

The two-week notice clause is what made the pause clean. The fixed prices to the end of 2025 are what stopped me raising them when costs went up in the summer ([[wholesale-only-at-the-target-margin]]).
