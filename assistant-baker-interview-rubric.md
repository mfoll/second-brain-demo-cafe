# Assistant Baker Interview Rubric

The scoring sheet Kofi and I used for interviews and trial shifts in July 2026.

## Key Details

Scored 1 to 4 on each line; the trial shift counts double.

1. Reliability for 04:30 starts (travel, past patterns of early work).
2. Hands: shaping test with 10 pieces of baguette dough, judged on speed and evenness.
3. Food safety habits seen on the trial: hand washing, probe use, clean as you go.
4. Learning: how they took a correction from Kofi mid-shift.
5. Teamwork: would Joel want to work next to them.
6. Interest in the bread itself.

## Why It Matters

Making the trial count double was Kofi's call, and it decided the hire. The two candidates with the best interviews scored lowest on the shaping test. Individual scores are kept in the recruitment folder, not here ([[hiring-lessons-2026]]).
