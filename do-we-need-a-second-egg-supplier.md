# Do We Need A Second Egg Supplier?

## Why It Matters

Quarrymoor supplies all our eggs. In February 2026 laying dropped and Ada cut us to 800 a week for three weeks.

## Current Answer

No, not for now. Ada warns two weeks ahead, the shortfall is predictable (late winter), and liquid egg from the cash-and-carry covers egg wash for those weeks at about 30 EUR extra a week. A second farm would want a standing order all year, which we do not need.

## Revisit when

- Ada misses a delivery without warning, which has happened once (2025-08, a van breakdown; she came at 09:00 instead).
- A second site doubles our egg use.
