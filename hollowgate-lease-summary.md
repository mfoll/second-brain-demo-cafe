# Hollowgate Lease Summary

My one-page summary of the lease of 14 Hollowgate from [[cyril-ashdown]], written on 2025-02-10 while listing fixed outgoings for the [[cash-flow-forecast]].

## Summary

Shop and basement on a 12-year lease from 2019 to 2031. Rent 39,000 EUR a year, paid quarterly in advance: 9,750 EUR on 1 January, 1 April, 1 July and 1 October.

## Why It Matters

The rent quarter on 1 January is one of the three payments that land together in January ([[overdraft-squeeze-january-2025]]). The alterations clause matters for [[central-production-kitchen]].

## Key Details

- Rent review every four years, upward only; next June 2027.
- Tenant repairs internal; landlord structure, roof and drains.
- Alterations need the landlord's written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
- Use: café, bakery and ancillary food production. Baking for another site of ours is arguably ancillary; [[ruth-marchbank]] suggested asking a solicitor before relying on it.
- No break clause. Assignment allowed with consent.
- Deposit held by landlord: 9,750 EUR.

## Caveats

My summary, not legal advice. The lease itself governs.
