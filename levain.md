# Levain

The portion of sourdough starter built up for a specific bake. Our starter is the mother; the levain is what Kofi mixes from it each day to raise the dough.

## Ours

- Stiff levain at 50% hydration, kept since 2019 and fed at 08:00 and 14:00.
- 4 kg built for each 40-loaf batch of the house sourdough ([[house-sourdough-recipe-card]]).
- Fed with Tarnwick strong white and a little wholemeal since July 2025. The change of flour made it more active within a week, which is why the [[flour-bake-trial-2025-05-14]] used two builds.

## Why stiff

A stiff levain ferments more slowly and tastes less sour than a liquid one, which suits a 15-hour cold retard. It also survives a missed feed on a Monday better.

## Continuity

There is always a jar of the starter in the walk-in and a dried flake in the office drawer, in case the basement loses power. Kofi checked in 2024 that the dried flake revives in three days.
