# Tanner's Row Dropped 2026-04-16

The day I told [[simone-ward]] we would not pursue the Tanner's Row shop. The [[tanners-row-site]] project was marked Superseded by [[saltings-hall-feasibility]].

## Why

The extraction and flue quote of 38,000 EUR ([[tanners-row-extraction-and-flue-quote]]) made the total over 130,000 EUR before working capital, the landlord would not contribute, and the planning position on an external flue was uncertain. The cold-only alternative would have forced central production a year early. Full reasoning: [[why-did-we-drop-tanners-row]].

## How It Was Done

Phone call to Simone in the morning, confirmed by email. She was not surprised. Cost to us: the 450 EUR survey and about 20 hours of my time.

## Notes

I felt I should have asked about the flue before the viewing. Added "previous food use and extraction" to the top of my checklist for any site, which I then used on the hall ([[things-to-ask-priscilla-about-the-hall]]).
