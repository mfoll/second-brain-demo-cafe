# Could We Use Heritage Grain Flour In A Signature Loaf?

## Why It Matters

A loaf nobody else in Brackenmouth makes would give the bread counter something to talk about, and would fit the Tarnwick relationship. But it costs flour money and baker hours, both short.

## Evidence So Far

- Two tastings ([[heritage-grain-tasting-notes]]): a 30% spelt sourdough was the clear favourite; a 100% emmer loaf was dense and the team was split.
- Flour cost: wholemeal spelt 1.85 EUR/kg against 0.92 EUR/kg for our white bread flour ([[tarnwick-heritage-flour-sample-list]]). A 30% spelt loaf adds about 0.25 EUR in flour.
- Kofi says spelt doughs need shorter, more careful mixing and he does not want a new dough in the morning sequence until Hana is fully trained.

## Current Answer

Deferred to spring 2027. Technically yes for a 30% spelt sourdough at about 5.20 EUR; not before the early shift has hours to spare.

## What Would Settle It

A two-week counter trial (Tuesday to Saturday, 20 loaves a day) with sales and waste tracked in the [[end-of-day-waste-log]]. Selling 15 a day or more at full price would make it a product in the [[seasonal-bake-calendar]].
