# Deck Oven Annual Service 2026

The full annual service of the new three-deck oven is due in November 2026, one year after commissioning ([[new-deck-oven-commissioned-2025-11-14]]). Colm Ashworth does it as one of his two scheduled visits a year; the terms and what is included are in [[deck-oven-service-contract]].

## What to book

- A Monday, so no bake is lost. Colm prefers to start at 08:00 with the oven cold.
- Steam generator descale, door seals, deck stones checked for cracks, controller firmware.
- Kofi wants the bottom-deck thermostat checked against a probe: he thinks it runs about 5 degrees hot at the back since September.

## Why it matters

The old oven failed on a controller we had never had looked at ([[deck-oven-failure-2025-07-29]]). One of the [[lessons-from-the-oven-failure|lessons]] was to service on a fixed date rather than when something sounds wrong. The lease also requires us to keep a service contract in place ([[deck-oven-lease-agreement]]).

## Status

Not booked yet. Ring Colm in the week of 2 November.
