# Second Site Decision

Decide whether Hollowgate Bakehouse opens a second site, and if so, where and how to pay for it. Umbrella for the site searches, the feasibility study, the financing and the production question.

## Outcome

A yes or no on a second site by 2026-12-15, before the Saltings Market Hall lease option expires on 2026-12-19.

## Current State

- Open. At the [[second-site-review-2026-09-30]] I deferred the decision to 2026-12-15.
- The only live candidate is unit 7 at Saltings Market Hall: 1,450 EUR a month, hall reopening in spring 2027, option held until 2026-12-19 ([[saltings-market-hall-unit-7]]).
- Money: fit-out and working capital 120,000 EUR. Pellow Mutual offered 85,000 EUR over five years at 7.9% on 2026-09-08 ([[pellow-mutual-second-site-loan-offer]]). [[ruth-marchbank]] advises waiting until we hold a three-month buffer of about 95,000 EUR; we held 41,000 EUR in September.
- Production: [[kofi-asare]] objects to baking for both sites from the Hollowgate basement on 03:00 starts ([[central-production-kitchen]]).
- My working answer is "not yet, unless both conditions move by December" ([[should-we-open-a-second-site]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

The decision and its evidence. Day-to-day operations at Hollowgate stay in [[run-daily-operations]]. No commitment (lease, loan, staff) is made under this umbrella before 2026-12-15. Staff are told about the second site only through Lena and the team meeting, never as rumour.

## Working Context

Why a second site at all: Hollowgate turns customers away on Saturdays between 09:30 and 12:00 (about 40 walk-outs counted on three Saturdays in January 2026), the basement bakery has spare oven time after 11:00 since the new deck oven ([[deck-oven-replacement]]), and [[priyanka-desai]] and [[amira-saleh]] both want more responsibility than one shop gives them. Why not: one shop already takes all my hours, the January cash low point is real ([[overdraft-squeeze-january-2025]]), and the bakers already work long weeks.

The food hall beat a standalone shop because the hall provides extraction, seating, toilets and footfall ([[a-food-hall-unit-beats-a-standalone-shop]]).
