# Lunch Feedback From Regulars

Lena's page of what customers said at the counter between 2026-02-24 and 2026-04-04, typed up for the [[menu-review-2026-04-09]]. No names; she tallied comments, not people.

## Tally

- Asked for the soup back: 41 times.
- "Nowhere to get something hot round here now": 12.
- Asked for the toasties: 9.
- Asked for the shakshuka: 4.
- Said they liked the savoury cheese bun instead: 7.
- Asked why lunch had gone: about 30, answered with the line from [[lunch-cut-staff-briefing]].

## Lena's notes

- The Tuesday and Thursday groups (retired, six to eight people) came less often, and when they did they bought one coffee each instead of lunch, coffee and cake.
- Office workers from the council annexe mostly went elsewhere.
- June Adeyemo wrote that she missed the soup; several people mentioned reading it.

## What it told us

The soup was the anchor, not the hot plates. That shaped what came back on 2026-04-20 ([[soup-and-toasties-return-2026-04-20]]).
