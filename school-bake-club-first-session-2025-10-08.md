# School Bake Club First Session 2025-10-08

First session of the [[school-bake-club]] at Saltings Lane Primary, Wednesday 8 October 2025, 15:30 to 16:45, in the school food room. Me, [[priyanka-desai]] and [[samir-belkacem]], 12 children from Year 4.

## What we did

- Focaccia. Dough made in our basement at 10:00 and carried over in two lidded tubs; the children stretched it into trays, pressed in rosemary and olive oil, baked 20 minutes.
- Ten-minute talk at the start: a wheat ear, a bag of Tarnwick flour, and what a mill does.
- Allergen check: Samir's list said no sesame and no nuts for this group. Our focaccia has neither.

## What went well

- Everyone took home a tray. Three parents came into the café on Saturday and mentioned it.
- The children loved pressing the dimples. Priyanka kept the time.

## What did not

- The school ovens run about 20°C hot; the first tray caught at the edges. Now on the checklist in [[running-a-bake-club-session]].
- We took too much equipment. Next time: scraper, tray and apron per child, nothing else.
- 75 minutes is tight for anything that needs a second prove.

## Decision

Samir asked to keep going monthly. We agreed eight sessions for the school year on the first Wednesday of the month ([[bake-club-session-plans]]).
