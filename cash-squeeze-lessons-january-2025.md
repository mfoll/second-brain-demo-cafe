# Cash Squeeze Lessons January 2025

Written the Sunday after the [[overdraft-squeeze-january-2025]], tired and cross. Kept because it is honest.

1. Every payment that hurt was known months ahead. Rent quarter, VAT, an old oven. I just never looked at them in the same week.
2. The budget said January was profitable. It was. Profit is not cash.
3. The VAT was never ours. I spent it anyway because it was sitting there ([[vat-money-is-not-our-money]]).
4. The books were three weeks behind, so I was driving with the windscreen fogged.
5. Glenvarrow and Fold & Carry were decent about paying late. Do not make a habit of asking.
6. Paying wages on time was right. Never move that line.
7. Devon was helpful but asked for a forecast. He's right.
