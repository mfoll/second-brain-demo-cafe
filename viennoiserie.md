# Viennoiserie

## Summary

Overview of viennoiserie: yeast-raised doughs enriched with sugar, butter, eggs or milk, sitting between bread and pastry. It splits them into non-laminated products such as brioche and laminated ones such as the croissant and Danish pastry, usually eaten at breakfast.

## Why It Matters

Used when I grouped products for the [[price-review-2026]]. Splitting viennoiserie into laminated and non-laminated gave two cost groups with different butter exposure: laminated items carry 40 to 50 g of butter each, brioche and buns far less. That is why croissants and pains au chocolat went up 0.35 EUR and the cinnamon bun only 0.30.

## Key Details

- Two classes: laminated (croissant, Danish) and non-laminated (brioche and similar).
- Enrichment with butter, eggs and milk is what puts these products at the mercy of dairy prices.
