# Staff Pulse Survey 2026-05

An anonymous ten-question survey of all staff, run on paper from 12 to 23 May 2026 and summarised by me on 25 May. Eight of nine staff answered. Only totals are kept; the paper forms were shredded after counting.

## Key Details

Scores out of 5, average:

| Statement | Average |
|---|---|
| I know what is expected of me on a shift | 4.4 |
| I have the equipment I need | 4.1 (3.4 before the new oven, per memory) |
| My hours are predictable | 3.9 |
| I hear about big decisions before customers do | 2.6 |
| I can raise a problem without it being held against me | 4.0 |
| I would recommend working here to a friend | 4.1 |

Free-text themes: the lunch cut (four comments: "we read it on the menu"), early starts (three), wanting more training (two).

## Why It Matters

The lowest score was on hearing decisions first, which matched what Margot had told me after the lunch cut ([[mentor-sessions-with-margot]]). Since June, any change to hours or menu goes to a team meeting before it is public.

## Caveats

Eight people; one strong view moves an average by half a point. Not a survey I would compare year to year too closely.
