# Thirteen-Week Cash Flow Forecast

The rolling cash forecast I built in February and March 2025 after the January overdraft squeeze, and have updated every Monday since 2025-03-31. It is the single place where I see whether we can pay the VAT, the rent and the wages in the weeks they fall due.

## Summary

One spreadsheet, one row per line of money, one column per week for the next 13 weeks, plus the last four weeks of actuals for comparison. The bottom line is the end-of-week balance across the current account and the VAT pot. If any week drops below 15,000 EUR, I write down what I will do about it before I close the file ([[cash-buffer]]).

## Why It Matters

- It exists because of [[overdraft-squeeze-january-2025]]: the 11,800 EUR VAT payment and the 3,400 EUR oven repair landed together and the 6,000 EUR overdraft was used in full for 19 days. A monthly budget ([[monthly-budget-2024]]) had shown January as profitable.
- It is why we take 30% deposits on Christmas preorders. In the 2025 forecast the week of 2026-01-26 was the low point; about 4,900 EUR of deposits on 214 orders came in during November and December and the overdraft was not touched ([[christmas-2025-deposits-reconciliation]], [[christmas-preorders-2026]]).
- It underpins the second-site work: the second tab models unit 7 with the Pellow Mutual loan ([[second-site-financing]]), and [[ruth-marchbank]]'s three-month buffer advice at the [[second-site-review-2026-09-30]] came from reading it.

## Key Details

Structure:

| Block | Lines | Source of numbers |
|---|---|---|
| Receipts | Card takings, cash takings, preorder deposits, wholesale (zero since 2025-10-20) | Square history by week, adjusted for season |
| Fixed outgoings | Rent quarters, oven lease, insurance, Square fees, software, loan repayments | Contracts and Xero |
| Payroll | Two fortnightly runs, employer costs | Deputy timesheets via payroll bureau |
| Variable | Food and packaging at 29% of takings, utilities by month | Xero, 13-week average |
| Tax | VAT quarter payments, corporation tax in September | [[vat-return-calendar]] |

Fixed dates that drive the shape:

- VAT payments on 31 January, 30 April, 31 July and 31 October.
- Rent quarters of 9,750 EUR on 1 January, 1 April, 1 July and 1 October ([[hollowgate-lease-summary]]).
- Deck oven lease of 812 EUR a month since December 2025 ([[deck-oven-replacement]]).
- Pavement licence fee in December, insurance in March.

The forecast on 2026-10-19 (rounded):

| Week starting | End balance |
|---|---|
| 2026-10-26 | 42,100 EUR (Q3 VAT paid) |
| 2026-11-30 | 38,900 EUR |
| 2026-12-21 | 47,200 EUR (Christmas peak, deposits in) |
| 2027-01-04 | 33,600 EUR (rent quarter) |
| 2027-01-25 | 21,300 EUR (Q4 VAT, quiet weeks) |

The 41,000 EUR "held for the second site" is not a separate account; it is the part of the current account balance above the 15,000 EUR floor in the week of 2026-09-21, which is why Ruth does not count it as a buffer.

Accuracy: across 2025-26 the 4-week-ahead balance was within 2,500 EUR of the actual in 41 of 52 weeks. The misses were the oven failure (July 2025) and two late wholesale payments.

## Caveats

- Takings are forecast from history; a new competitor, roadworks or a hot week can move them 10-15%.
- It is cash only. Profit and margin live in Xero and [[margin-by-menu-section-2025]].
- The second-site tab uses the low case from [[saltings-hall-three-scenario-model]] on purpose; it is a stress test, not a prediction.
