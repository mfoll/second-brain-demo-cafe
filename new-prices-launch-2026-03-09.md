# New Prices Launch 2026-03-09

New prices loaded into Square on Monday 2026-03-09 while we were closed; first trading day Tuesday 2026-03-10.

## What we did

- Lena and I updated 41 items in the Square item library on Monday afternoon and printed a test receipt for each section.
- New boards chalked by Grace on Monday; the counter card ([[price-board-wording-2026]]) went up by the till.
- Front of house briefed at the Tuesday 07:15 huddle: one sentence on why, and no apologising.

## First week

- Two comments at the counter on Tuesday, both about the croissant. One regular said "about time, they're still cheaper than in town".
- Croissant units Tuesday to Saturday: 1,012 against 1,048 the week before (-3%).
- One mistake: the oat milk modifier had been duplicated and charged twice on two tickets; refunded the same day.

## Six weeks on

Checked on 2026-04-21: croissant units down 3% on the same weeks of 2025, croissant takings up 10%. Overall item volumes held. Notes in [[price-review-2026]].
